Historical unadjusted October16 close isUS$33.27, up approximately3.6% fromUS$32.11 the prior session. The stock does not fall on this particular closing-price comparison despite the guidance cut announced Friday evening.

The preceding approximatelyone-year window recordsUS$54.93 high andUS$31.77 low. The close is39.4% below that observed high. Session volume is63.32 million shares versus calculated20-session average28.79 million.

On the former adjusted EPS guidanceUS$3.25–3.45, the close implies9.6–10.2x. On the revisedUS$1.45–1.65 range, it implies20.2–22.9x. Describing the security simply as10x after the update retains a stale denominator.

The new guidance includes inventory and contractual transition effects. It is also not automatically the right perpetual earnings figure. A normalized valuation should identify one-time reversals/charges separately and estimate recurring post-pandemic product demand, rather than extrapolate either pandemic peaks or a single disrupted year.

Maintaining theUS$0.41 quarterly dividend impliesUS$1.64 annually and4.9% indicated yield. H1 cash-flow coverage is weak due partly to working capital, and prospective acquisition obligations matter. The yield is not an independent proof of cheapness.

Using5.640 billion basic weighted-average shares gives approximatelyUS$187.6 billion indicative equity value. Adding July2 simple net financial debtUS$20.556 billion gives aboutUS$208.2 billion before other claims. The share measure is historical/weighted, not an exact October16 count.

This bridge is not a completed-Seagen pro forma valuation. Acquisition financing is already partly reflected in debt and financial assets, while the target's future cash flows and purchase payment require their own consistent treatment. Adding debt again without removing cash or adding assets would distort value.

A credible model needs product-level revenues, patent/competition risks, replacement launches, research and acquisition returns, costs, taxes and capital expenditure. No complete independent pipeline probability model or live consensus table is recovered.

The attraction is diversified drug cash at a much lower price. The unresolved issue is whether sustainable owner earnings, after replacing unusual COVID profits and funding growth, are sufficiently predictable and discounted under conservative assumptions.
