The calculated cash residual is explicitly unadjusted. Management reports 2025 adjusted FCF approximately $4.2 billion. That adjusted figure differs from CFO less physical/software investment and should not be substituted without its reconciliation.

FY2025 adjusted EPS was $6.95 versus $6.07 in 2024. Adjusted operating income was approximately $5.992 billion, versus reported $4.929 billion. Acquisition amortization and other adjustments are substantial, making a consistent valuation denominator important.

Q1 2026 net revenue was $2.977 billion, up 20%, with $1.657 billion transaction revenue, up 34%, and $1.320 billion recurring revenue, up 7%. Reported operating income was approximately $1.7 billion and adjusted approximately $1.9 billion.

Quarterly reported EPS $2.48 exceeded adjusted $2.35 because adjustments remove investment gains as well as add back costs. A $389 million fair-value adjustment to equity investments is a major factor. Treating the larger GAAP number as recurring exchange earnings would be inappropriate.

Q1 CFO was approximately $1.3 billion, with $64 million capex and $112 million software development. Defined FCF was $1.150 billion. The company returned $848 million, including $551 million repurchases and $297 million dividends.

Unrestricted cash was $863 million and debt $20.4 billion, giving approximate net debt $19.537 billion. Clearing-member deposits are not discretionary corporate cash. A consistent ROIC series is unavailable.
