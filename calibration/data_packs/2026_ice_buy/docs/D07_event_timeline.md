February5,2026: ICE reported2025 results, later reproduced in its annual report. Net revenue reached$9.931billion, reported EPS$5.77 and adjusted EPS$6.95. Acquisition-related and mortgage economics remained material to the consolidated cash/debt picture.

March: The annual report made three-year statements and detailed business descriptions publicly available. Its cash-flow table explicitly separates physical capex and capitalized software, both relevant to owner-cash valuation.

April22–May1: Major listed exchanges reported strong first-quarter results. ICE's April30 release showed net revenue up20%, transaction revenue up34%, recurring revenue up7% and strong FCF. Peers CME and Cboe also reported record or sharply higher activity-related earnings.

May19: ICE and Ornn announced plans for GPU compute futures, offering price discovery and hedging tools for compute markets. This is evidence of product development around AI, not proof of achieved trading volume, revenue or a defensible new benchmark.

LateMay: The CFTC enabled perpetual crypto futures through regulated domestic exchanges, according to the June2 Reuters account. This was a real market-structure development, but did not by itself establish approval or adoption across every other asset class.

June2: Cboe fell9%, while CME and ICE fell approximately4%. Analysts debated the speed of extension to other assets and the distinction between retail speculation and institutional hedging. The report includes both competitive concerns and defenses based on product differences and established liquidity.

June30: ICE closed$123.11 on7.12million shares. This is the conservative review date, not an asserted investor execution day. Its price incorporates the public June competitive debate but no later earnings.

The chronology separates operating performance from changing expectations. Current revenues and margins were strong before the market worried about future competitive structure. The relevant underwriting must determine whether that future threat materially changes the value of the present profit pools.

The chronology ends at the current price and public market-structure debate, without assuming any future earnings or price path. Mortgage normalization, AI-data effects and perpetual adoption remain scenarios to assess rather than facts supplied by hindsight.
