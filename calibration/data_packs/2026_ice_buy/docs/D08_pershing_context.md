Title: Permanent capital and a new vehicle, without double-counted buying power

Date: 2026-05-15

The February11 presentation reported February9 gross core-fund AUM$19.748billion and equity$16.077billion. These are stale scale measures by June, not current deployable cash. PSH gross assets and equity were$17.872billion and$14.202billion.

On April30, Pershing announced closing the combined IPO of Pershing Square USA and Pershing Square Inc., with aggregate gross proceeds$5billion before expenses. The fund and manager are different entities. The total offering amount cannot be assumed entirely available to PSH or added to old AUM as uncommitted cash.

The new permanent-capital vehicle expands the relevant capital context, but exact June allocations, deployment and cash balances were not recovered. Public share prices do not automatically change the investment pool in the same way as primary issuance.

May15 public Ackman commentary disclosed Microsoft ownership and a workflow/network-quality thesis. That is legitimately pre-cutoff context. It does not reveal any unpublished decision concerning ICE or establish that all current buying power is unrestricted.

Other publicly discussed core franchises include Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Brookfield, Uber, Universal Music, Restaurant Brands, Howard Hughes and Fannie/Freddie. Complete exact June weights and all vehicle-specific exposures are unavailable; US13F percentages would not capture every foreign, OTC or derivative asset.

The stated framework favors simple, predictable cash generation, durable barriers, strong finances and capable management. An exchange franchise potentially fits through benchmark liquidity, clearing and recurring data, but its debt, cyclical volume and mortgage acquisition require explicit testing.

An investor must also consider portfolio correlation. Financial-market infrastructure differs from consumer platforms, yet can be sensitive to market levels, issuance, policy and technology narratives already affecting other holdings. A sector label alone does not establish diversification.

No authenticated pre-June30 ICE-specific Ackman recommendation is included. The decision must derive from the current evidence and public principles. A low multiple and strong recent cash flow can support an investment only if core liquidity and pricing remain sufficiently durable; permanent capital is not a substitute for that judgment.
