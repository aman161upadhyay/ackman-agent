The P/E on adjusted FY2025 EPS is backward-looking. An authenticated forward multiple would require a dated earnings forecast, whose assumptions can differ materially from historical results. The cash residual deducts both physical capex and capitalized software; omitting software would inflate yield.

The enterprise-value ratio uses EBIT, not EBITDA. Acquisition amortization, debt and clearing-related balance-sheet classifications require reconciliation before comparison with data-vendor metrics. Member collateral is not spare cash available to reduce economic enterprise value.

Exact June30 forward EPS consensus, a harmonized EV/EBITDA, historical own-forward-multiple distribution, current rolling 52-week range and same-date S&P500 forward valuation were not verified. A discount to historical valuation cannot be precisely measured without that dated series.

Reuters' June2 reporting documents a roughly4% ICE decline during the broader perpetual-futures scare, but that is not a complete peak-to-cutoff drawdown calculation. The pack does not manufacture one from a current quote page containing future highs/lows.

The price offers a meaningful observable historical cash yield, but the investment question is forward durability. It requires testing whether core exchange liquidity and clearing preserve economics, whether data pricing holds, and how much mortgage recovery is necessary to achieve an acceptable return.
