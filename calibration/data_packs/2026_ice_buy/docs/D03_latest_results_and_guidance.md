Title: April30 — strong exchange activity, different mortgage economics
Source: https://ir.theice.com/press/news-details/2026/Intercontinental-Exchange-Reports-Record-First-Quarter-2026/default.aspx
Date: 2026-04-30

ICE reported Q1 net revenue $2.977 billion, up 20%, GAAP EPS $2.48 and adjusted EPS $2.35. Reported operating margin was 56% and adjusted 65%. The quarter benefited from strong transaction activity, but its investment-related gains must be distinguished from recurring operations.

Transaction revenue increased 34% to $1.657 billion, while recurring revenue grew 7% to $1.320 billion. This mix demonstrates why a volatility-sensitive exchange can grow quickly during unsettled markets without every part of the company growing at the same rate.

Exchanges contributed about $1.8 billion net revenue and $1.4 billion reported operating income. Fixed Income/Data contributed $657 million revenue and $275 million operating income. Mortgage Technology contributed $539 million revenue and a $13 million reported operating loss.

The mortgage segment's adjusted profit differs from reported results because of acquisition amortization and other costs. The pack does not equate a reported loss with zero economic value, nor assume a housing recovery is already achieved. Origination/refinancing conditions and integration execution remain relevant.

The EPS reconciliation removes $389 million equity-investment fair-value gains and $26 million unconsolidated-investee income, while adding back acquisition-intangible amortization of $237 million, transaction/integration cost $31 million and a $4 million regulatory item, alongside tax adjustments. This explains why adjusted EPS is lower than GAAP.

The company generated about $1.3 billion CFO and $1.150 billion defined FCF. It returned $848 million to shareholders. Corporate liquidity must be separated from clearing-member collateral and guaranty funds, which are not available to fund discretionary repurchases.

The release forecasts full-year GAAP operating expense $5.095–5.145 billion and adjusted expense $4.145–4.195 billion. It gives Q2 diluted-share guidance of 565–571 million. A verified full-year EPS consensus is not supplied.

Operating evidence is therefore strong before the June market-structure scare. That is relevant counterevidence to a claim of already-observed core deterioration. It does not settle long-run competitive effects from perpetual contracts or AI-driven workflows.

The forward task is to estimate sustainable volume, recurring growth, fees, software investment and capital allocation, while treating mortgage normalization as uncertain rather than required near-term earnings.
