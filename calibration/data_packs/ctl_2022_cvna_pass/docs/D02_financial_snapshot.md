Simple CFO less capex is negativeUS$813 million. Working-capital movements contribute to this quarter, so it is not automatically a fixed future burn rate. It nevertheless establishes that near-term operations are not financing the whole expansion.

At March31, short-term revolving facilities areUS$2,786 million, current long-term debtUS$178 million and other long-term debtUS$3,286 million. Their sum isUS$6,250 million, calculated. TheUS$3,464 million long-term total alone excludes the inventory/revolving facilities and must not be labeled total debt.

April's new notes addUS$3,275 million principal with10.25% annual interest and maturity in2030. Annual stated interest on those notes alone is approximatelyUS$335.7 million, calculated, before other borrowing costs. The financing also raises approximatelyUS$1.25 billion equity.

Management's acquisition supplement showsUS$4.876 billion pro-forma total liquidity resources at March31, includingUS$2.960 billion cash/revolving availability, US$1.763 billion unpledged real estate andUS$153 million other assets. These categories are not all cash. Financeable property may require a new loan or sale.

The financing funds aUS$2.2 billion acquisition, planned site improvements, transaction costs and other needs. It should not be presented asUS$4.525 billion unrestricted cash added to the old balance sheet.

Carvana's structure includes noncontrolling economic interests. Consolidated losses and class-A per-share results are different measures. A fully diluted equity/enterprise valuation needs the complete economic ownership and liabilities, not only publicly traded class-A shares.
