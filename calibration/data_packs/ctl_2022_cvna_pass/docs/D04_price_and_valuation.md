The April debt coupon10.25% is a useful market signal of funding cost. It is not an equity return hurdle by itself, but common holders rank behind those interest and principal obligations. A cheap-looking share price can coexist with a high enterprise value relative to uncertain future profit.

Class-A-only capitalization would omit economic interests in the consolidated operating structure. No precise current fully diluted ownership bridge is reconstructed here; an unsupported enterprise/revenue multiple is therefore not supplied.

The ADESA real estate and potential capacity can support value, but property market value is not necessarily surplus liquidation cash. The facilities may be required to execute the operating plan, and monetization could create rent or financing costs.

A credible upside model needs sustainable units, gross profit per unit, selling expense, working capital, sustaining capex, interest and tax. The downside needs realistic liquidity and asset-recovery assumptions. The equity is a residual claim after financing needs, not a direct pro-rata claim on gross vehicle revenue.

No later financing deal or realized profitability is imported. The attraction is possible network scale at a much lower quotation; the unresolved issue is the capital and time required to prove that scale creates owner value.
