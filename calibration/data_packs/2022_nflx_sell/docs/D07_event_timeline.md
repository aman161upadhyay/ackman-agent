Title: Dated timeline through the April results
Source: compiled from: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528022000035/ex991_q421.htm; https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/2022/01/26170421/Pershing-Square-Capital-Management-L.P.-Releases-Letter-to-Investors-01-26-2022.pdf; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528022000144/ex991_q122.htm; https://www.cnbc.com/2022/04/19/netflix-is-exploring-lower-priced-ad-supported-plans-after-years-of-resisting.html
Date: 2022-04-19

**2021:** Netflix added about 18 million paid memberships, versus about 37 million in 2020. The January earnings letter reports this comparison. The slowdown provides essential context for distinguishing pandemic normalization from a more durable acquisition problem.

**Late 2021:** Squid Game demonstrated the global appeal of locally produced programming. Netflix's January letter reports 1.65 billion viewing hours during its first four weeks. The Roald Dahl Story Company acquisition was funded from cash during Q4. These developments support content reach and intellectual-property ownership, but require capital allocation rather than prove subscriber growth by themselves.

**December 2021:** Netflix lowered prices across its four plans in India. January's letter says low local pay-TV prices make India unusual, and that the goal is maximizing long-run revenue in each market. This is a useful counterweight to a universal-pricing-power thesis.

**January 20, 2022:** Q4 results showed 8.28 million paid net additions, slightly below the 8.5 million forecast. Q1 guidance was only 2.5 million. Management targeted 19%-20% 2022 operating margin, compared with 21% in 2021, in part because of dollar strength.

**January 21-26:** The manager's January 26 letter publicly describes purchases beginning January 21 and a holding exceeding 3.1 million shares. It emphasizes recurring revenues, management, content scale, pricing power, margins and improving cash generation. This is information already public for the existing-position replay.

**March 2022:** Netflix introduced paid-sharing features in three Latin American markets. The April letter dates this launch and describes it as part of tests started earlier. It has not supplied a mature financial record for the initiative.

**Q1 2022:** Suspension and winding-down of Russian subscriptions reduced paid memberships by 0.7 million. The April letter separates this effect from the underlying acquisition shortfall.

**April 19:** Results show negative 0.2 million net additions and guide to negative 2.0 million in Q2. Hastings discusses exploring an advertising-supported tier over one to two years. These are distinct changes: current subscriber performance, a deteriorating near-term forecast, and an unproven future monetization strategy. No subsequent operating results are included.
