Title: Portfolio concentration, investment standards and financing context
Source: compiled from: https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/2022/03/29140526/Pershing-Square-Holdings-Ltd.-2021-Annual-Report.pdf; https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/2022/01/26170421/Pershing-Square-Capital-Management-L.P.-Releases-Letter-to-Investors-01-26-2022.pdf
Date: 2022-03-29

The March 29 annual report provides a portfolio overview already public before the April results. It describes approximately 30% of the equity portfolio in music/video streaming, comprising Universal Music Group and Netflix; 26% in restaurants and franchising, comprising Chipotle, Restaurant Brands and Domino's; 15% in home improvement through Lowe's; with Hilton, Canadian Pacific and Howard Hughes representing other principal exposures. These are category/manager descriptions, not reconstructed daily NAV weights.

The distinction between UMG and Netflix matters. Both benefit from digital distribution and recurring consumption, but music-rights licensing and subscription-video production have different content economics. A favorable view of streaming as a category does not establish an identical predictability standard for each holding.

In January, purchases of more than 3.1 million Netflix shares were funded by $1.25 billion of proceeds from unwinding most of the interest-rate hedge. That letter says the notional hedge was reduced by 80%, its dollar investment by more than 90%, while some longer-dated out-of-the-money protection remained. Those historical proceeds are not April 20 uncommitted cash.

The January 26 letter states: “We invest in hedges not to protect the funds from a short-term mark-to-market loss”. It instead describes hedges as potential liquidity when stocks become cheap, structured to limit losses if the anticipated risk does not occur. This supports evaluating opportunity cost and prospective risk/reward rather than defending an earlier purchase mechanically.

The same letter says many attractive investments arise when short-horizon investors discard great companies. That philosophy supports tolerating volatility when intrinsic-value assumptions remain intact; it does not say every decline warrants adding capital. Management quality, recurring revenue, scale economics, pricing power and cash generation were the explicit Netflix purchase criteria.

A precise April 20 manager-wide AUM, same-day cash balance and each security's current weight are not supplied in these extracts. PSH is one listed fund, while manager-wide holdings span multiple vehicles; 13F equity totals would omit cash, derivatives and foreign holdings. These distinctions prevent a false precision in position sizing. The portfolio context is concentrated ownership of a few businesses, not an index-like allocation that can ignore a widening range of outcomes in one major holding.
