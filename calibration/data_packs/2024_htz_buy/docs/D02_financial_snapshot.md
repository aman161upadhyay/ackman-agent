The 2023 release reports net income $616 million and diluted EPS $1.39, but adjusted income was only $172 million and adjusted EPS $0.53. Q4 alone had an adjusted $418 million loss and included the $245 million EV-related depreciation charge. Tax and other nonoperating effects make GAAP net income an unreliable normalized earnings base.

Latest-quarter GAAP net loss was $1,332 million, or $4.34 per share, including a $1 billion noncash impairment. Adjusted net loss was $208 million or $0.68 per share. Excluding the impairment does not make the ongoing quarter profitable.

GAAP operating cash flow was $894 million, but company-adjusted operating cash flow was negative $132 million and adjusted free cash flow negative $154 million. Rental-fleet investment and disposal accounting make ordinary industrial-company CFO-minus-capex shortcuts dangerous. A full three-year consistently adjusted FCF/capex series is not recovered here.

September nonvehicle debt was $4,653 million and vehicle debt $12,303 million. Corporate liquidity was $1.6 billion, including approximately $0.5 billion unrestricted cash and $1.1 billion available revolving capacity. Liquidity is not the same as net cash: using the revolver adds borrowing.

No current dividend or buyback-driven capital return is a central source of value in this snapshot. A comparable ROIC series is unavailable. The historical peak EBITDA occurred under unusual used-car conditions and should not be treated as a guaranteed recovery target. Equity value depends on financing the transition and improving depreciation and operating efficiency before liquidity is exhausted.
