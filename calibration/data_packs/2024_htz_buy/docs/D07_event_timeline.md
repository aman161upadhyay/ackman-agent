Title: EV retreat, new management and expensive bridge financing
Source: compiled from: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1657853/000165785324000010/htz-20240111.htm; https://newsroom.hertz.com/press-releases/press-release-details/hertz-announces-appointment-of-gil-west-as-chief-executive-officer-as-stephen-scherr-steps/; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1657853/000095010324009159/dp213388_8k.htm; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1657853/000165785324000138/q32024earningsrelease.htm
Date: 2024-11-12

January 11, 2024: Hertz disclosed the planned sale of approximately 20,000 EVs, with a $245 million Q4 2023 depreciation charge. The economic loss belongs to 2023 accounts even though the filing was published in 2024.

February 6, 2024: annual results showed $9.371 billion 2023 revenue and only $561 million adjusted Corporate EBITDA, versus $2.305 billion a year earlier. Q4 adjusted EBITDA was negative $382 million. The repair need was visible before the chief-executive change.

March 15, 2024: Hertz announced Gil West would replace Stephen Scherr, effective April 1. West's Delta and Cruise operating background is relevant to the execution challenge. Appointment alone does not establish improved fleet economics.

June 21–28, 2024: Hertz priced and completed $1 billion of financing: $750 million first-lien senior secured notes at 12.625%, and $250 million exchangeable second-lien secured PIK notes at 8.000%, both due 2029. The financing adds liquidity but also expensive claims ahead of common equity and potential dilution.

August 1, 2024: Q2 results reported $2.4 billion revenue, an $865 million GAAP loss and $1.8 billion corporate liquidity at June 30. Fleet costs were already central to the investment problem.

November 7, 2024: Cox published October's used-vehicle index at 202.8, down 3.2% from a year earlier. This is an external reality check on expected resale values.

November 12, 2024: Q3 results reported $1.6 billion corporate liquidity, a $1 billion impairment and negative $157 million adjusted Corporate EBITDA. Management targeted substantial completion of fleet rotation and the operational transformation by end-2025.

The decision cutoff is after that release. It contains no later financing, tariff policy change, investor campaign, autonomous partnership or subsequent recovery evidence. The key near-term milestones are operating progress and liquidity preservation, not a scheduled corporate transaction guaranteeing common shareholders an exit.

Quarterly depreciation, rental pricing, utilization and available borrowing capacity provide measurable checks on whether the announced operating plan is working.
