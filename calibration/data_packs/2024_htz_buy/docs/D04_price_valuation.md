Title: Equity price, liquidity and why P/E is not informative

Date: 2024-11-12

The date-bounded historical series shows Hertz closing November 12 at $3.66 on 16,576,200 shares, after an opening price of $3.00. The day's intraday range was approximately $2.97–$3.735. A weak premarket response reported in the press is not the closing return; the session was volatile.

The preceding approximately 52-week window has an intraday high of $10.82 and low of $2.47. The close is approximately 66.2% below that high, calculated. Average daily volume over the window was approximately 6.19 million shares. A meaningful institutional position remains possible, but volume does not provide a fundamental margin of safety.

Multiplying $3.66 by the latest quarter's approximately 307 million average shares gives illustrative equity value of $1.124 billion. This is not exact common capitalization and excludes the contingent effect of warrants and exchangeable securities. The difference between basic shares during losses and a fully diluted recovery share count is important.

Trailing P/E is not meaningful given current losses and a large impairment. Applying a P/E to 2023 GAAP EPS would also be misleading because adjusted earnings were much lower and present fleet costs are different. A reliable forward consensus EPS or standardized EV/EBITDA multiple was not recovered.

Corporate debt of $4.653 billion already substantially exceeds illustrative equity value; vehicle debt adds $12.303 billion secured against rental assets. An EV calculation that adds all vehicle debt but ignores fleet assets or vehicle depreciation is not comparable with an asset-light operating business. Conversely, simply excluding fleet debt hides collateral and residual-value risk.

A useful valuation bridge would require assumptions about sustainable rental days, RPD, monthly depreciation, operating cost per day, interest and diluted shares. The company's sub-$300 DPU objective supplies one input, not an entire valuation. Current FCF is negative on the company's adjusted measure, so a positive FCF yield is unavailable.

A matching point-in-time peer valuation table, long-run multiple range and S&P forward P/E are not recovered. No later investor's price target is used to fill those gaps.
