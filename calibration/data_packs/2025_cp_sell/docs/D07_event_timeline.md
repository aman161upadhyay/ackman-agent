Title: Integration progress meets a changing policy environment

Date: 2025-03-17

Late 2021: Pershing reinitiated a CP investment. Its March 2022 explanation focuses on the KCS acquisition, management execution, merger synergies and a valuation below history and peers. This is not the start of the original activist campaign.

December 2021: CP acquired KCS economically through a voting trust. Pending operating-control approval, KCS contributed through equity accounting rather than full consolidation. This distinction later affects the historical financial tables.

April 14, 2023: CPKC obtained control and consolidated KCS. The trilateral network became an operating combination rather than only an anticipated strategic concept. Reported 2023 accounts contain major acquisition-related tax and remeasurement items.

During 2024: Integration synergies progressed despite labor disruptions and a difficult freight market. FY2024 statements show stronger CFO, but ongoing investment and debt remain substantial. Full-year reported EPS and adjusted combined EPS move differently because their accounting bases differ.

January 29, 2025: CPKC reported Q4 adjusted EPS C$1.29 and gave 12–18% core adjusted EPS growth guidance for 2025, with mid-single-digit ton-mile growth and C$2.9 billion capital spending. Management expressed confidence in continued cross-border commerce despite tariff threats.

February 25: The 2024 annual report made full reported financial statements available. It documents C$14.546 billion revenue, C$5.269 billion CFO and C$22.623 billion debt before cash.

March 3–6: US tariff announcements were followed by a temporary exemption for USMCA-compliant Canadian and Mexican goods. Policy remains unsettled rather than definitively hostile to every shipment or definitively resolved.

March 14: Pershing's published annual report reiterated a constructive CPKC thesis, citing realized run-rate revenue synergies, management, regional integration and double-digit prospective earnings/FCF-per-share growth. That public statement is important counterevidence against assuming the thesis was already abandoned.

March 17: Toronto shares closed C$107.54 and NYSE shares US$75.29. This is the review date. Later tariff decisions, quarterly results, portfolio disclosures and price outcomes are excluded. The live question is how much policy uncertainty should alter confidence in the previously articulated growth case.
