Title: Three annual statements — consolidation changes the comparison
Source: https://s21.q4cdn.com/736796105/files/doc_financials/2024/ar/CPKC-2024-Annual-Report-Web_Final.pdf
Date: 2025-02-25

All dollar amounts are C$ millions except per-share figures. These are audited reported statements, not a pro forma combined-company growth series. Before April 14, 2023, KCS was equity-accounted; thereafter it was consolidated. The 2022–24 revenue growth implied by the table is therefore not organic growth.

The calculated cash residual is a transparent CFO-minus-investment measure, not the company's defined adjusted FCF. It excludes proceeds from asset sales and other investing adjustments. FY2024 property-sale proceeds were C$64 million. Exact comparability to management's free-cash-flow presentation requires its reconciliation.

FY2023 net income contains a C$7.175 billion KCS remeasurement loss and C$7.885 billion deferred-tax recovery. These offsetting acquisition-accounting effects make GAAP EPS growth a poor stand-alone indicator of recurring operations. FY2024 reported EPS fell while the January earnings release reports 11% core adjusted combined EPS growth.

At December 31, 2024, current debt maturities were C$2.819 billion and long-term debt C$19.804 billion, total C$22.623 billion. Less C$739 million cash gives calculated net debt C$21.884 billion, before any further lease or pension adjustment. The 2023 corresponding debt sum was C$22.494 billion and cash C$464 million.

Common shares outstanding were 933.5 million versus 932.1 million. FY2024 diluted weighted-average shares were 934.6 million. No cash-flow repurchase line appears in this table; forecast repurchases should not be presented as already executed.

Latest Q4 reported revenue was approximately C$3.9 billion, reported EPS C$1.28 and core adjusted combined EPS C$1.29. Full-year core adjusted combined EPS was C$4.25. A harmonized adjusted EBITDA or ROIC series has not been extracted, so neither is manufactured from mixed accounting bases.
