Title: Public questions, capital returns and continued operating growth
Source: compiled from: https://ir.herbalife.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/473/herbalife-responds-to-stock-drop-today; https://ir.herbalife.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/472/herbalife-announces-427-9-million-share-repurchase; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1180262/000115752312005483/a50458256ex99_1.htm; https://www.cnbc.com/2012/05/03/herbalife-ceo-is-top-paid-greenberg.html
Date: 2012-10-29

**May 1 — distribution questions move the share price.** David Einhorn asks about sales outside the network, incentives for converting customers into distributors and the disappearance of distributor-category disclosure. The company answers on the call and issues a written defense that afternoon. There is no disclosed Einhorn position or regulator finding established by the questions.

**May 3 — US$427.9 million repurchase agreement announced.** Herbalife says it will use cash and senior secured revolver borrowings to fund an agreement with Merrill Lynch International, completing the existing US$1 billion authorization. The number of shares retired depends on volume-weighted prices. An announced authorization and an executed repurchase agreement are different events; neither should be confused with an open-ended future program.

**May 3 — governance scrutiny.** CNBC's Herb Greenberg reports a GMI compensation survey identifying Michael Johnson's US$89.4 million realized compensation including substantial option-exercise gains. The article states beneficial ownership of approximately 3.6% as of February 2012. This reflects already vested or exercised awards and should not be presented as annual salary. Incentive alignment and realized insider liquidity are distinct considerations.

**October 1 — Q3 release/call scheduled.** The company describes more than 80 markets and independent distribution. The schedule makes October 29 results and October 30 management discussion the latest completed regular earnings cycle for this cutoff.

**October 29 — broad growth and capital returns.** The release reports 17% volume-point growth with double-digit increases in every geographic region, EPS of US$1.04, a US$0.30 dividend and completion during Q3 of the previously announced US$427.9 million repurchase agreement. Q3 alone included US$181.9 million repurchases.

**October 29 — next-year guidance introduced.** Management projects US$4.40–US$4.55 2013 EPS and 10–12% sales growth, explicitly incorporating a changed Venezuelan currency assumption while excluding specified one-time effects.

The next scheduled test is Q4/full-year performance, including whether growth converts into recurring end demand rather than only distributor purchasing. No regulatory hearing, shutdown order or publicly announced new activist short campaign has been authenticated as of December 18. The sparse last several weeks of independently recovered material is a visible source gap, not an invitation to backdate subsequent headlines. The May events are retained because their capital and disclosure implications remain directly relevant.
