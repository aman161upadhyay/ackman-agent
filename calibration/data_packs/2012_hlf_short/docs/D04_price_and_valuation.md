Low multiples may reflect a risk premium for distribution-model questions rather than an obvious mispricing. Conversely, genuine repeat consumption and repurchases can make this price attractive to longs and costly to shorts. Cash generation allows the company to retire stock, while limited float or rising borrow fees can defeat an otherwise plausible fundamental short.

Contemporaneous locate availability, loan fee, utilization, short-interest days-to-cover and a verified S&P forward multiple are not recovered. No squeeze probability or carrying cost is invented. A short position cannot be sized from a “zero price target” alone: upside loss is not capped at the initial notional, while catalysts may take years.
