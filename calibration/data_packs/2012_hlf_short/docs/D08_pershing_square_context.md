These percentages use the entire filed table denominator, including the PG call rows. They are not long-only common-book weights, NAV weights or an estimate of available cash. The form does not disclose ordinary stock shorts, most derivatives, cash or all non-US securities. A missing Herbalife row cannot establish the absence of a short.

The portfolio combines consumer franchises, asset-rich real estate, rail operating improvement and difficult retail change. The public record includes campaigns for management/governance changes and corporate restructuring. That experience supports intensive analysis and persistence, but does not confer control over a regulator or another investor's financing decisions.

The Globe profile describes earlier high-profile bets against overhyped companies as well as activist efforts to repair underperformers. It is not a dated Herbalife recommendation. No authenticated pre-cutoff Ackman statement on Herbalife is available in this pack; future presentation language is excluded.

A hypothetical sizable public short would add a different risk shape to these concentrated longs: capped gross profit at a zero price, theoretically unlimited mark-to-market loss, dividend carry and borrow-dependent survival. Fund capacity is therefore not measured by subtracting the 13F table from the US$11 billion headline AUM. Required collateral and technical risk need independent diligence.
