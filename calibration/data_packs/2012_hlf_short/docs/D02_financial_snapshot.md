Sales grew approximately 17.6% in 2010 and 26.3% in 2011. The improving margin and cash flow are affirmative evidence for the business as reported. They cannot establish how much distributor purchasing is supported by genuine retail consumption, but neither should they be dismissed without analysis.

The annual report describes approximately US$48.7 million, US$53.7 million and US$85.5 million dividends paid during 2009, 2010 and 2011, respectively, and approximately US$73.2 million, US$150.1 million and US$298.8 million repurchases. Capital returns absorb substantial operating cash.

At September 30, 2012 cash was US$321.722 million, total debt US$500.437 million and net debt US$178.715 million. Shareholders' equity was US$371.405 million, versus US$560.188 million at year-end 2011. A declining book-equity balance alongside repurchases is not necessarily a deterioration in operations; it also amplifies return-on-equity ratios.

Q3 net sales were US$1,016.887 million, operating income US$160.843 million and net income US$117.827 million. Operating margin is approximately 15.8%, calculated. Q3 diluted EPS of US$1.04 rose faster than net income because of reduced shares.

Nine-month EPS is US$3.01. Q3 CFO was US$142.4 million, cash capex US$20.0 million and repurchases US$181.9 million. This financially active issuer is not a passive target. Acquisition-inclusive ROIC and a clean maintenance/growth-capex split are unavailable; no invented efficiency ratio is substituted.
