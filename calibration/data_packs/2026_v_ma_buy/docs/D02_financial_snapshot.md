Visa's calculated FY2025 operating margin was 60.0%, versus 65.7% in 2024. Litigation provision increased from $462 million to $2.562 billion. That affects GAAP growth and should not be mistaken for equivalent deterioration in underlying transaction economics.

Mastercard's FY2025 calculated margin was 57.6% versus 55.3%. Its reported EPS rose despite a higher tax expense. Comparisons of adjusted margins require company-specific reconciliations rather than removing only whichever costs are inconvenient.

Visa's latest January release reports $16.9 billion cash/equivalents/investment securities and $19.602 billion long-term debt. Mastercard's year-end cash was $10.566 billion, with $749 million short-term and $18.251 billion long-term debt. These definitions exclude some settlement/restricted assets and do not represent identical liquidity classifications.

Latest-quarter Visa revenue was $10.9 billion, GAAP EPS $3.03 and adjusted EPS $3.17. Mastercard Q4 revenue was $8.8 billion, GAAP EPS $4.52 and adjusted EPS $4.76. Strong cash conversion is observable; a consistent ROIC series and exact normalized earnings bridge are not included.
