Title: A related pair of franchises within a concentrated capital base
Source: compiled from: https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/11144917/2026-Annual-Investor-Presentation.pdf; https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/15162701/PSH-Fact-Sheet-December-2025-Website.pdf
Date: 2026-02-11

The February 11 presentation reports February 9 gross core-fund AUM of $19.748 billion and core equity $16.077 billion. PSH gross assets were $17.872 billion and equity $14.202 billion. Gross assets include financing and are not identical to available cash.

The presentation's December 31 capital weights include Amazon 13%, Meta 10% and Hertz 2%. Other publicly discussed franchises include Alphabet, Brookfield, Uber, Universal Music, Restaurant Brands, Howard Hughes and Fannie/Freddie exposure. Exact March weights and cash were not independently reconstructed.

The December fact sheet describes a concentrated strategy, generally eight to twelve core large-cap liquid North American businesses, with one to three new core investments annually. A full US reportable-stock table would not capture every foreign, OTC or derivative exposure and should not be treated as a complete NAV allocation.

Public investment principles emphasize simple, predictable FCF, durable competitive barriers, strong finances, capable management and limited dependence on capital markets or extrinsic factors. Visa and Mastercard should be tested against those standards through actual transaction economics, not because the word “network” resembles other successful holdings.

The two companies offer a potential fit with recurring, capital-efficient toll economics. Yet their earnings are not contractual annuities: spending, travel, regulation, incentives and technology can change. Buying both would require a combined exposure limit because much of the downside narrative is shared.

The portfolio already has substantial technology-enabled franchise exposure. Card networks might diversify product-market demand, but could also add another position sensitive to AI-related valuation and consumer spending. Correlation must be assessed economically rather than by sector labels alone.

No verified pre-March-31 Visa/Mastercard-specific Ackman recommendation is included. The analysis is therefore a fresh application of public principles to the evidence, not an imitation of an unpublished investment explanation.

Exact entry sizing cannot be inferred from the manager's overall AUM. Each candidate requires its own downside case, prospective return and liquidity plan, followed by a joint check on shared regulation and rail-displacement risk. Permanent capital permits patience but does not justify capitalizing uncertain fee growth as if it were guaranteed.
