Title: Board changes and an unresolved CEO search
Source: compiled from: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lowes-announces-three-new-appointments-to-board-of-directors-300585116.html; https://corporate.lowes.com/newsroom/press-releases/lowes-announces-ceo-succession-plan-03-26-18; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/60667/000006066718000015/exhibit991_02022018.htm
Date: 2018-04-02

**January 19:** Lowe's announces appointments of David Batchelder, co-founder of Relational Investors, and Lisa Wardell, CEO of Adtalem, effective March 22. Brian Rogers, chairman/former CIO of T. Rowe Price, is to be nominated with them for election at the annual meeting.

The board additions create a potential oversight and capital-allocation catalyst. They do not establish that all directors agree on a turnaround plan, that an activist controls the company or that a particular CEO will be selected.

**February 28:** FY2017 results show a large, profitable retailer with a substantial margin gap to Home Depot. The 2018 outlook forecasts EPS growth but operating-margin decline. That makes operating execution a live issue rather than a completed repair.

**March 22:** The announced effective date for Batchelder and Wardell joining the board arrives. There is no need to backdate later annual-meeting outcomes into this snapshot.

**March 26:** Niblock announces his plan to retire after a 25-year company career when a successor is appointed. He remains chairman, president and CEO during the search. The company says the board has initiated that search.

**April 2:** The share price closes at $84.59. A new investor sees a scaled franchise, a visible peer gap, refreshed oversight and an open succession process. The eventual hire, appointment date and plan are not known.

Precise current Pershing AUM, cash and name-by-name weights were not recovered from an authenticated pre-cutoff update. The manager's historical concentrated ownership approach is relevant, but it is not a quantitative capacity estimate or evidence that a Lowe's stake has already been announced.

The earnings release also identifies conversion, gross margin and inventory management as execution priorities. Those are already public management objectives, not a later investor-authored plan. No later manager purchase disclosure is included.

The decision can therefore be conditional: whether the starting valuation and likely operating opportunity warrant capital before a CEO is named, or whether management/plan evidence is still insufficient. The model should not treat a future high-caliber appointment as an April fact or claim that the manager personally installed the eventual CEO.
