Title: April price and the earnings/margin bridge

Date: 2018-04-02

Historical daily data show Lowe's opening at $87.35 and closing at $84.59 on April 2, on volume of 9,611,700 shares. Only that historical row is used; the database's current operating description and later forecasts are excluded.

The close implies 20.7x FY2017 GAAP EPS of $4.09. Against FY2018 guided EPS midpoint of $5.45, it implies 15.5x. These multiples use known company results/guidance rather than an invented consensus estimate.

The large difference between trailing and forward multiples needs explanation. Management simultaneously forecasts an operating-margin decline, so the EPS increase incorporates tax, share-count and other effects; it is not solely an operating turnaround.

Q4 diluted weighted shares of 829 million give an indicative equity-size proxy of $70.125 billion at the close. This is not exact market capitalization because weighted-average diluted shares are not April spot outstanding shares.

Year-end debt totaled $16.995 billion and cash/investments $1.098 billion. Adding that simple net-debt measure to the equity proxy gives approximately $86.022 billion before leases and other adjustments.

The same equity proxy against FY2017 CFO-minus-capex of $3.942 billion gives roughly 5.6%. This is a backward-looking cash-flow yield proxy, not a normalized forecast or an exact current-market calculation.

The margin sensitivity is material. Each 100bp of operating margin on FY2017 revenue equals approximately $686 million of annual EBIT, before taxes, required investment and changes in revenue. This is an explicit arithmetic sensitivity, not a forecast that the improvement occurs.

Home Depot's FY2017 margin of about 14.5% exceeds Lowe's 9.6% by approximately 490bp. Eliminating that entire gap would be an aggressive scenario requiring justification about mix, productivity, investment and execution; the gap alone is not intrinsic value.

A verified April 52-week range, daily historical-multiple series, contemporaneous S&P multiple and forward peer P/Es were not recovered. The supported valuation analysis is the observed price versus guidance and a clearly labeled margin sensitivity, with implementation and cycle risk left visible.
