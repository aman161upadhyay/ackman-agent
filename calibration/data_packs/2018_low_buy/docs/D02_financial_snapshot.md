FY2017 sales grew 5.5%, but the extra week in FY2016 distorts direct comparison. Comparable sales grew 4.0% for the year. Q4 comparable sales grew 4.1%.

The release says FY2017 program repurchases totaled approximately $3.1 billion and dividends paid approximately $1.3 billion. The retained-earnings allocation for repurchases is not total buyback cash. Buybacks reduce the share count, but sustainability depends on operating cash generation and financing capacity.

Q4 weighted-average diluted shares were 829 million and FY2017 weighted-average diluted shares 840 million. They are EPS denominators, not an exact April 2 outstanding share count.

Year-end cash was $588 million, short-term investments $102 million and long-term investments $408 million. Debt included short-term borrowings $1.137 billion, current long-term maturities $294 million and long-term debt $15.564 billion.

Total stated debt is therefore $16.995 billion; subtracting cash and both investment lines gives $15.897 billion before lease/other adjustments. Not every investment balance necessarily has the same liquidity as cash.

The 2018 outlook calls for approximately 4% sales growth and diluted EPS of $5.40-$5.50, but operating margin declining about 30bp. Tax reform and other below-operating-line effects therefore matter to the EPS growth rate.

A consolidated ROIC series and a full three-year cash-flow/lease reconciliation were not recovered. The available financial base is sufficient to frame a margin opportunity, but not to assert that peer-level margins or historical capital returns are inevitable.
