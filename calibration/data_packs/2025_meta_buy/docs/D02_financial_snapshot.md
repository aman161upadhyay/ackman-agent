Capex includes equipment purchases plus finance-lease principal to preserve the company's free-cash-flow definition. It is not interchangeable with property purchases alone. Q3 property purchases were $18,829 million and lease principal was $545 million. For the first nine months, operating cash flow was $79,586 million, property purchases $48,308 million, lease principal $1,770 million and FCF $29,508 million, versus $38,951 million in the comparable 2024 period. Thus cash generation is strong before investment, but rising reinvestment is already reducing cash available after investment.

Net cash excluding leases was $15,614 million at September 30, calculated as cash and securities less long-term debt. This simplified measure excludes lease commitments and other liabilities. It is not a comprehensive net financial obligation calculation.

Q3 share repurchases were $3.16 billion and dividends/dividend equivalents $1.33 billion. FY2024 repurchases were $29.75 billion and dividends/dividend equivalents $5.072 billion. Buybacks must be considered alongside stock compensation: Q3 share-based compensation was $5,555 million. A repurchase dollar does not necessarily reduce share count dollar-for-dollar.

The quarter's $1.05 GAAP EPS includes the $15.93 billion noncash tax charge. The company separately gives $7.25 excluding that charge. Neither number should be annualized mechanically. ROIC under a consistent invested-capital definition is not available in these releases; no synthetic ROIC is assigned.
