The Scale AI development is included with the reporting date that verifies it; an exact original announcement date is not authenticated here. It should not be treated as a new October 30 transaction. Similarly, the October earnings call's discussion of September product launches is evidence that the launches are public by the cutoff, not an exact shipment chronology.

The earnings discussion reports Instagram at 3 billion monthly users, Threads above 150 million daily users and more than one billion monthly Meta AI users. These adoption milestones broaden potential distribution, but they do not establish a standalone price or profit stream for Meta AI. Business messaging and AI assistants are prospective monetization avenues rather than reported new operating segments.

Management also warns of Q4 RL revenue pressure from lapping Quest 3S's launch and from retailers stocking holiday inventory during Q3. This prevents mistaking Q3's 74% RL revenue increase for a stable hardware-growth run rate.

There is no announced leadership replacement, activist control change or promised asset disposal in the retrieved period. The immediate measurable catalysts are delivery against Q4 revenue guidance, further disclosure of infrastructure plans and evidence on ad returns. Their outcomes are unknown. This timeline stops at the November 3 close; it contains no subsequent earnings, spending assurances or portfolio disclosure.
