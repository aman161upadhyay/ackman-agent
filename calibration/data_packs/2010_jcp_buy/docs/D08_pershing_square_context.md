Values sum sole/shared rows once. The sizeable existing Target exposure and smaller Borders holding matter when considering additional department-store/consumer risk. Real-estate experience does not make different retail formats or customers interchangeable.

The August26 investor letter, available as a third-party archival transcription, says Yum was sold as its price approached estimated intrinsic value. It describes Landry's as a catalyst opportunity arising from a going-private offer and the outcome of negotiations. It discusses General Growth and other portfolio situations. These are dated examples of value-oriented sales and engagement, not a newly disclosed JCP thesis.

An August24 Rational Walk report attributes “over US$3 billion” AUM to the Value Investing Congress organizers. This is too imprecise and potentially narrow to reconcile firmwide NAV or investable cash; no exact AUM is asserted in meta. AUM should not be substituted with the conveniently available 13F total.

An activist can recruit expertise, seek board influence and press for accountability, but cannot guarantee that customers accept a new assortment or proposition. Existing ownership of Target demonstrates prior retail analysis, not proof of skill in a different turnaround. No authenticated pre-September24 Ackman JCP statement is available. The pack therefore evaluates public fit and capacity before public disclosure, without importing a future executive recruitment or operating plan.
