Sales declined approximately 6.9% in 2008 and 5.0% in 2009. Comparable sales fell 8.5% and 6.3%. The apparent 570-basis-point reported operating-margin decline from 2007 includes a swing from pension income of US$45 million to pension expense US$337 million.

Issuer adjusted operating income was US$1,791 million, US$1,002 million and US$961 million, with adjusted margins 9.0%, 5.4% and 5.5%. The business still has a large performance gap from its own pre-recession level, but not all reported deterioration is a merchandising problem. Issuer adjusted return on beginning invested capital fell from 15.7% to 7.8% across those years; this is the company's non-GAAP definition.

At July31, cash was US$2.003 billion, debt US$3.099 billion and inventory US$3.490 billion. Net debt is US$1.096 billion, calculated. Equity was US$4.886 billion. The cover identifies 236,443,580 shares as of September3.

First-half CFO was negative US$379 million and capex US$229 million, illustrating seasonal inventory and other funding needs. The strong full-year2009 simple FCF should not be annualized from a shrinking-capex/inventory period without normalization.

Q2 operating income was US$95 million on US$3.938 billion sales, approximately 2.4% margin. Cash and property provide runway, not a guarantee that deteriorating sales can be tolerated indefinitely. A clean maintenance-capex split and fully normalized pension cash burden remain diligence items.
