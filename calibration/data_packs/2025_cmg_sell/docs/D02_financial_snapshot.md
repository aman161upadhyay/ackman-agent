FY2024 restaurant-level margin was 26.7% versus 26.2% in 2023. FY2024 comparable sales rose 7.4%. These strong base-period results make the 2025 traffic slowdown meaningful even though consolidated revenue continues to grow.

Latest Q3 revenue was $3,003.444 million, operating income $477.172 million and diluted EPS $0.29. Diluted weighted-average shares were 1,339.522 million for the quarter and 1,350.159 million for nine months. These are weighted averages, not exact cutoff basic shares.

September cash was $698.743 million, current investments $722.531 million and long-term investments $347.694 million, total $1,768.968 million. No separate conventional funded-debt borrowing line appears in the extracted balance sheet. Large operating-lease liabilities must not be erased by calling the company completely liability-free.

Repurchases exceeded the calculated nine-month CFO-minus-capex residual, implying use of accumulated liquidity as well as current generation. This is not automatically imprudent, but buyback accretion depends on price and sustainable earnings.

A harmonized ROIC series and maintenance-versus-growth capex split are unavailable. Unit economics should not be inferred solely from consolidated margins or an assumed return metric.
