Title: Successful recovery is not the same as a stable forward forecast
Source: compiled from: https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/2022/10/07154952/2016-Annual-Report-Letter-Only.pdf; https://ir.chipotle.com/2025-02-04-CHIPOTLE-ANNOUNCES-FOURTH-QUARTER-AND-FULL-YEAR-2024-RESULTS; https://ir.chipotle.com/2025-10-29-CHIPOTLE-ANNOUNCES-THIRD-QUARTER-2025-RESULTS; https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/30/chipotle-stock-falls-after-q3-earnings-report.html
Date: 2025-10-30

The original investment expected a food-safety-related crisis to prove temporary while brand, value, restaurant economics and growth opportunities remained intact. The company subsequently grew to thousands more restaurants than at the original purchase and generated $1.511 billion calculated FCF in 2024. Those are substantial achievements, not evidence to be discarded because the latest quarter disappointed.

Today's problem is different from the original crisis. No new food-safety event is established in these documents. Current evidence concerns visit frequency, consumer budgets, cost leverage and management execution. Reusing the 2016 recovery script without distinguishing the mechanism would be poor underwriting.

The former turnaround leader is no longer CEO. Boatwright has outlined execution, marketing, innovation and digital initiatives, but the next phase requires confidence in that team's results. Past success under Niccol does not guarantee an identical trajectory under different leadership.

Existing-store growth has slowed materially. FY2024 comps rose 7.4%; Q3 2025 comps rose 0.3%, with transactions down 0.8%. October reporting says the current run rate worsened further, and full-year guidance moved from positive low-to-mid-single-digit growth to a low-single-digit decline.

The unit-development opportunity remains. Management expects 315–345 2025 openings and 350–370 in 2026, predominantly with Chipotlanes. A long runway can support value, but only if returns on incremental units and the existing estate remain attractive. New stores cannot indefinitely conceal weak mature-store productivity.

The balance sheet and cash generation are strengths. Nine-month CFO less capex was $1.219 billion, although cash-flow repurchases of $1.684 billion exceeded that residual. Capital allocation therefore also requires a view on sustainable earnings and the price paid.

Valuation has compressed following the earnings reaction. The November 3 close is about 28x FY2024 EPS. That is lower than a higher prior price but does not by itself establish a sufficiently high future IRR. Forward EPS and the traffic-recovery path remain uncertain.

The live distinction is between a great business encountering temporary pressure and a wider outcome distribution that no longer supports concentrated ownership at the current price. The documents supply evidence for both possibilities; they do not resolve the question through later investor actions.
