February 6, 2025: Hilton reported record FY2024 results. Revenue was $11.174 billion, adjusted EBITDA $3.429 billion and adjusted EPS $7.12. It returned $3.0 billion including dividends and guided to 2–3% comparable currency-neutral RevPAR growth for 2025. That initial forecast supplies a public starting benchmark against which the subsequent reduction can be judged.

August 20: PSH's H1 report continued to describe Hilton's predictability, resilience and capital-light growth favorably. This is a public statement about business quality, not evidence of a fixed minimum holding period or an unconditional valuation endorsement.

October 2025: Hilton announced Outset Collection, a new lifestyle brand, as noted in the October 22 release. New brands can expand addressable owner demand, but brand proliferation is not automatically incremental profit; contracts, development economics and differentiation determine value.

October 22: Q3 results reported RevPAR down 1.1% system-wide and 2.3% in the US. Adjusted EBITDA nevertheless reached $976 million. Management narrowed full-year RevPAR expectations to flat through +1% and expected net unit growth of 6.5–7.0%. The contrast between weak comparables and stronger system growth is central to interpreting the business.

October 22 press: Reuters framed the guidance reduction as evidence of subdued US travel demand amid consumer and tariff concerns. The company call attributed additional pressure to government travel, calendars, renovations and softer inbound demand. These are contemporaneous explanations rather than resolved causal weights.

Year-end 2025: The completed-year historical price-return series shows continued appreciation after a strong 2024. A rising share price alongside a lower RevPAR outlook raises a valuation question but is not itself evidence of investor irrationality; expected unit growth, margins and per-share capital allocation also influence value.

January 15, 2026: Historical unadjusted close was $301.86 on 2.219 million shares. The December PSH fact sheet was publicly available on this date. Full-year Hilton realized results and new-year guidance are not included in this timeline because they are not yet authenticated public information at the cutoff.

No verified CEO turnover, announced activist demand or binding change in the franchise model appears in these retrieved developments. The decision is therefore a comparison of an established business and current valuation, with unknown future operating outcomes.
