The quarter's $31 million contract-acquisition-cost figure is the difference between reported $81 million total capex/contract costs and $50 million capital expenditures. For nine months, revenue was $8.952 billion, adjusted EBITDA $2.779 billion, GAAP diluted EPS $4.84 and adjusted EPS $6.03. Nine-month capex was $133 million and combined capex/contract costs $236 million.

Shareholder returns remain a substantial use of cash. FY2023 repurchases were $2.3 billion and dividends $158 million. FY2024 capital return including dividends was $3.0 billion; dividends were $150 million and 13.3 million shares were repurchased. Quarterly dividends were $0.15 per share in the 2024 release.

Cash flow from operations and a consistent three-year FCF reconciliation were not extracted from these release tables. Neither adjusted EBITDA minus capex nor net income minus capex is labeled FCF here: cash interest, taxes, working capital and contract acquisition outlays matter. A properly measured FCF yield is consequently unavailable rather than silently approximated.

The balance-sheet trend is still informative. Net debt rose from $7.534 billion in 2022 to $10.600 billion in September 2025. Strong distributions and a capital-light fee model can coexist with rising debt. ROIC is not supplied under a consistent definition, and negative or buyback-reduced book equity would make a simplistic equity-return comparison especially misleading.
