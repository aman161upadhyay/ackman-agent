The filing excludes foreign-listed UMG, cash and most derivatives. It is therefore an incomplete economic portfolio. Agilent, present in the prior quarter's filing, is absent from this filing; the current table must not assume it remains a reported US equity position. A year-end 13F is not yet available at this cutoff.

The November 30 fund update, published December 1, reports PSH AUM of $14,156.3 million, total core-strategy AUM of $16,266.0 million, total firm AUM of $17,678.7 million including UMG co-investment vehicles, and $21,678.7 million including PSTH. These definitions add outstanding fund debt and are not equivalent to shareholder NAV. PSTH's $4 billion is separately committed SPAC capital, not free cash for ordinary fund purchases.

The same update reports ten disclosed long positions, no disclosed short issuer positions and 115% net equity/debt exposure. Macro hedges are excluded from that position count. An exact January 24 cash balance is not available in these sources.

The interim report states the objective is to “maximize its long-term compound annual rate of growth in intrinsic value per share”. It describes concentrated, principally North American public-company investing. This favors businesses whose durable economics can be underwritten over years rather than quarter-to-quarter trading.

UMG provides a public pre-cutoff connection to streaming economics, but music-rights licensing is not identical to financing video originals. No verified pre-January-24 Ackman statement about Netflix itself was recovered. A later purchase explanation is deliberately not used to supply the answer to this new-position replay.
