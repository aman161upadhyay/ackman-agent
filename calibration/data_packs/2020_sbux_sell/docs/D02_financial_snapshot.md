FY2018 cash flow includes a major transaction-related receipt associated with the Nestlé alliance. It is not a normalized recurring FCF base. Similarly, the FY2018 East China acquisition-related gain affects GAAP EPS comparability.

Non-GAAP EPS was $2.42 in FY2018 and $2.83 in FY2019, approximately 17% growth. That underlying improvement contrasts with the decline in GAAP EPS, demonstrating why neither single series should be used without its reconciliation.

FY2019 repurchases were $10,222.3 million versus $7,133.5 million in FY2018. The diluted weighted-average share count fell from 1,394.6 million to 1,233.2 million, while ending outstanding shares fell from 1,309.1 million to 1,184.6 million.

Cash at FY2019 end was $2,686.6 million. Long-term debt was $11,167.0 million. The retrieved balance sheet has no separate current long-term-debt amount for 2019; a full debt/lease reconciliation is not supplied. Simple long-term debt less cash is $8,480.4 million before other adjustments.

The capital-return program is substantial relative to current recurring cash generation and uses transaction cash/financing as well as current earnings. Buybacks reduce the EPS denominator but do not automatically create value at every purchase price.

A historical consolidated ROIC series was not recovered. Store-level pretax-return estimates in the original owner letter are not a substitute. The key forward question is normalized operating growth and sustainable cash returns, not repeating the unusually large FY2018 cash inflow.
