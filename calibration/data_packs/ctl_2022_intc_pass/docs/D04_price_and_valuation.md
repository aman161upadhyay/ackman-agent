The stock has therefore lost almost half its value from that observed high, despite the positive earnings reaction. The percentage decline measures the change in price, not whether the business has retained the same intrinsic value.

Using4.127 billion October1 shares gives approximatelyUS$119.97 billion common capitalization, calculated. Share count, price and statement dates are not identical, so this is an indicative market value rather than a precise enterprise valuation.

The quarterly dividend rate ofUS$0.365 impliesUS$1.46 annually and approximately5.0% indicated yield if maintained. The rate is not a guaranteed bond coupon. Quarterly operating cash flow is below dividends even before capital investment.

At the new full-year GAAP EPS guidanceUS$2.00, price/earnings is approximately14.5x. At adjusted EPSUS$1.95 it is14.9x. A roughly13x screen may use a trailing or different adjusted estimate; it should not be represented as the multiple on these new annual forecasts.

Neither multiple captures the gap between reported earnings and cash reinvestment. Intel forecasts negativeUS$2–4 billion adjusted free cash flow despite positive EPS. A conventional earnings multiple can look low while the owner must commit more capital to preserve the franchise.

A normalized valuation needs PC/server demand, competitive share, product margins, process yields, depreciation and maintenance versus expansion spending. The foundry opportunity requires its own external-customer returns and investment assumptions rather than a technology-sector multiple attached to gross planned capacity.

Potential government incentives, partner funding and Mobileye value can reduce financing strain. They do not establish the operating return on Intel's share of project spending. Those offsets should be modeled explicitly and not deducted twice.

No independently verified live forward-consensus table or historical-multiple distribution is supplied. The inexpensive-looking yield and price decline are observable attractions. The unresolved issue is what sustainable distributable cash will remain after the manufacturing reset, not whether the prior share-price high can be revisited.
