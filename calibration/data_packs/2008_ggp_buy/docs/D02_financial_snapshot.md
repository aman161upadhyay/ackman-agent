At September 30, consolidated cash was US$139.175 million and balance-sheet mortgages, notes and loans US$24,766.701 million. The supplemental portfolio-debt schedule, excluding specified accounting adjustments, reports US$24,569.686 million consolidated debt plus US$3,197.043 million at-share unconsolidated debt: US$27,766.729 million total. Do not add the JV debt to the balance-sheet total without identifying the basis difference.

Trailing pro-rata EBITDA was US$2,286.770 million and reported interest coverage 1.60x. Portfolio debt/that EBITDA is approximately 12.1x, calculated. High occupancy does not make this a conservatively financed REIT.

The common dividend is suspended. Issuer ROIC, maintenance FCF, buyback totals and a consistently extracted three-year capex-only series are unavailable in this snapshot. FFO adds back real-estate depreciation but does not pay maturing loan principal.
