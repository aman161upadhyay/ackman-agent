Title: Financing attempts, executive departures and a going-concern warning
Source: compiled from: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895648/000129993308003384/exhibit1.htm; https://www.businessrecord.com/general-growth-announces-earnings-delays-projects/; https://www.reviewjournal.com/news/mall-owner-to-seek-buyers-for-three-lv-malls/; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895648/000095012308014370/c47435exv99w1.htm; https://pbn.com/general-growth-dropped-from-sp-50036273/
Date: 2008-11-13

**March 2008 — equity issued.** The November 5 supplemental report identifies 22.8 million new common shares issued in March, explaining part of the fall in per-share Core FFO despite aggregate income resilience. New capital helps liquidity but reduces each existing share's participation.

**July 11 — secured refinancing.** A company release says US$875 million initial proceeds from a new secured mortgage facility repay remaining third-quarter maturities except one US$73 million property loan not yet repayable without penalty. This is evidence that refinancing was possible in summer, not that all later debt is covered.

**July 31 — development retrenchment.** Business Record's Reuters-based report describes approximately US$500 million deferred development spending over 18 months. The 2008 Core FFO forecast was cut to US$3.42 from US$3.52–US$3.58. Occupancy was then 93.2%. Operational leasing resilience and financing deterioration were already diverging.

**October 27 — governance and asset-sale changes.** The Las Vegas Review-Journal reports John Bucksbaum's departure as CEO and Robert Michaels' departure as president, with Bucksbaum retaining the chairmanship and Michaels the COO title. Fashion Show, Grand Canal Shoppes and Shoppes at Palazzo are offered for sale. The source describes margin-call pressure on insider stock and analyst skepticism that individual property sales will solve the whole balance sheet.

**November 5 — another outlook reduction.** Q3 results lower annual Core FFO guidance to US$2.85–US$2.95. Future developments are largely deferred, the common dividend suspended, and approximately US$958 million debt remains to be addressed by December 1.

**November 10–12 — survival warning.** The Monday SEC filing raises substantial doubt about continued operation, as reported in the November 12 NYT/AP article: https://www.nytimes.com/2008/11/12/business/12property.html. Extensions, asset sales and new capital remain alternatives rather than completed cures.

**November 12–13 — index deletion.** Providence Business News reports removal from the S&P 500 after November 12 trading, citing severely reduced market capitalization. The stock closed November 12 at US$0.35 according to that report. Index exclusion can create selling unrelated to property-level cash flow, but does not disprove the default risk that caused it. No bankruptcy outcome, court valuation or committed rescue financing is in this timeline.
