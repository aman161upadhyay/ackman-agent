Title: Stable mall receipts do not solve December financing needs
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895648/000095012308014370/c47435exv99w1.htm
Date: 2008-11-05

The November 5 release reports Q3 diluted EPS of negative US$0.06, FFO per fully diluted share of US$0.58 and Core FFO per fully diluted share of US$0.64. FFO declined approximately 11% to US$185.4 million from US$208.4 million. Core FFO increased in aggregate to US$205.7 million from US$200.7 million but fell per share from US$0.68 because of dilution, including 22.8 million common shares issued in March.

Core FFO excludes Master Planned Communities NOI and income-tax effects. The prior-year comparison also includes approximately US$37.1 million of Glendale Galleria litigation cost in Q3 2007. Aggregate Core FFO growth is therefore not a clean same-property growth signal.

The company states: “Although retail property revenue has remained relatively stable,” per-share FFO measures declined, principally because of impairments. Retail and Other NOI increased 1.0% to US$622.5 million. Comparable consolidated-property NOI declined 1.8% in the quarter; unconsolidated NOI at share increased approximately 8.0%. The combined comparable NOI table shows a 0.5% quarterly decline and 2.5% nine-month growth. These are substantially different from a collapse in mall operations, but the quarterly direction is weakening.

The residential condominium project Nouvelle at Natick incurred US$40.3 million impairment. Reported consolidated land-sale revenue fell to US$6.2 million from US$54.2 million. Management expects the reduced sales pace to continue into 2009. This development segment is not a dependable source of rapid debt repayment.

Guidance for 2008 Core FFO is reduced to US$2.85–US$2.95 per share, reflecting lower NOI growth, impairments and greater amortization of refinancing costs. The estimate excludes implementing strategic alternatives and future impairments.

Management reports approximately US$1.74 billion new/replacement financing funded since June 30, including a US$1.51 billion secured portfolio facility and US$225 million short-term secured loan. Nevertheless approximately US$900 million of property debt and US$58 million of corporate debt mature by December 1 and remain to be refinanced or extended. Fashion Show and Shoppes at Palazzo are being marketed, and extensions are being sought.

The board is examining asset sales, joint ventures, capital infusions and broader combinations. Most future development spending is deferred, and the dividend suspended. These actions conserve liquidity but neither establish adequate asset coverage nor secure lender consent.
