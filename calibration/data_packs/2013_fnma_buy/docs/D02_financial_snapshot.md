The 2012 annual report was filed in April2013. Consolidation rules changed in2010 and brought a large volume of securitization trusts onto the balance sheet; gross interest revenue, debt and assets should not be compared mechanically with pre-2010 reports. Traditional industrial-company EV/revenue and net-debt/EBITDA are poor measures here.

The recovery reflects improving housing conditions, lower credit costs and stronger new mortgage acquisitions. It is not equivalent to all earnings being distributable to common shareholders. Treasury dividends and senior priority dominate the capital waterfall.

Q2 2013 net interest income was US$5.667 billion and net revenue US$6.152 billion. Net income was US$10.1 billion and comprehensive income US$10.3 billion. Higher home prices reduced estimated losses and required reserves; fair-value gains also helped. Annualizing the entire quarterly profit would assume these benefits recur.

Q1 included a US$50.571 billion income-tax benefit associated with release of the deferred-tax-asset valuation allowance. This is a large accounting recognition of expected future tax benefits, not recurring annual franchise earnings and not an equivalent fresh common distribution.

At June30, net worth was US$13.2 billion. The September Treasury dividend was US$10.2 billion, leaving the contractual US$3 billion capital reserve. Treasury's senior preferred remained US$117.1 billion. Dividends do not repay its liquidation preference or extinguish warrants.

Fannie reported a Q2 net loss attributable to common holders of approximately US$159 million, US$0.03 diluted loss per share, despite its large enterprise profit. This is the central accounting clue against applying a low headline P/E to common.

Single-family serious delinquency fell from 3.29% at December2012 to 2.77% at June2013. Industrial-style free cash flow and maintenance capex are not supplied as valuation substitutes. The appropriate analysis needs normalized credit losses, guaranty fees, regulatory capital, senior claims and common dilution.
