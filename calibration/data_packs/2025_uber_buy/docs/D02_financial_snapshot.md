The margin calculations divide reported operating income by reported revenue. Growth during the pandemic recovery and acquisition changes should not be extrapolated mechanically. Uber's 2023 operating cash flow was $3,585 million, compared with $642 million in 2022. Free cash flow is operating cash flow less property-and-equipment purchases; it is not adjusted EBITDA.

For Q3 2024, revenue was $11,188 million, up 20%, and GAAP operating income $1,061 million, up from $394 million. Net income was $2,612 million and diluted EPS $1.20. However, net income includes a $1.7 billion pretax benefit from investment revaluations. The company did not earn that entire amount from moving passengers and deliveries.

Quarterly operating cash flow was $2,151 million and capex $42 million, producing $2,109 million FCF. Nine-month operating cash flow was $5,387 million and capex $198 million; analyst subtraction gives $5,189 million nine-month FCF. Insurance-reserve accruals contribute to operating cash flow and should be examined alongside cash earnings.

September cash and equivalents were $6,150 million and short-term investments $2,913 million. Long-term debt was $10,986 million. Simple debt less those unrestricted liquid assets is approximately $1.923 billion, calculated, excluding leases and restricted balances. Management expected a $2 billion debt redemption in Q4, but its completion is not assumed here.

Quarterly buybacks were $375 million under the February $7 billion authorization. No dividend or comparable ROIC series is supplied. Low physical capex does not make employee equity compensation free; quarterly stock compensation was $438 million.
