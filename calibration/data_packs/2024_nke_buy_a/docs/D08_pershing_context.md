Gross investments can exceed NAV because of financing. The table must not be treated as a complete cash allocation or ordinary 13F percentage schedule.

The report identifies Alphabet as the company added during 2023 and Lowe's as the exit. It emphasizes a concentrated, long-term approach to high-quality businesses and a willingness to assess management and operational improvements rather than simply buying a low headline multiple.

Strong consumer brands and asset-light franchise systems are well represented. Nike shares the global-brand attribute, but its need to produce successful new products, manage inventory and repair channels makes its earnings path less predictable than a recurring royalty stream.

No verified Nike-specific Ackman statement dated before this cutoff is supplied. Public principles are available; a private current thesis or an undisclosed trade is not. The next quarterly filing cannot be assumed public before its actual filing date.

The candidate therefore needs a self-contained case: a durable brand and balance sheet, fixable rather than structural underperformance, a credible operating plan and a price compensating for repair time. Even if those conditions support ownership, uncertain depth and duration of the slowdown can justify less-than-maximum conviction. Current cash, opportunity cost and portfolio concentration remain constraints rather than reasons to assume a particular action.
