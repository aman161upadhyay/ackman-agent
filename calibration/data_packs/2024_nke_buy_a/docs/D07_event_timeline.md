Title: The publicly visible de-rating before the decision date
Source: compiled from: https://investors.nike.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000320187-23-000054/q2fy24exhibit991er.htm; https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/nike-misses-quarterly-sales-estimates-weak-wholesale-business-china-demand-2023-12-21; https://investors.nike.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/24/nike-inc-reports-fiscal-2024-third-quarter-results; https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/nike-shares-skid-switch-newer-styles-squeeze-revenue-2024-03-22
Date: 2024-05-14

June 2023: the completed fiscal year showed 10% revenue growth to $51.217 billion but gross margin only 43.5%, versus 46.0% a year earlier. This established the tension between sales scale and profitability before the current guidance concerns.

December 21, 2023: Nike reported fiscal Q2 revenue of approximately $13.4 billion, up 1%. The company outlined up to $2 billion cumulative cost savings over three years. Reuters described a reduced annual sales outlook, weaker online business and more promotions, with a sharp initial share-price decline.

March 12, 2024: On released 2023 sales growth of 46.6% and guided at least 30% constant-currency growth for 2024. The challenger data make product/category competition an identifiable issue rather than a purely hypothetical future threat.

March 21, 2024: Nike reported flat quarterly revenue, digital sales down 3%, wholesale up 3%, and $340 million restructuring charges. The reported EPS decline was smaller after considering excluded charges, but operating margin remained below the prior comparable quarter.

March 21–22, 2024: management's expected low-single-digit first-half fiscal 2025 sales decline became public. Reuters connected the warning to reducing older product franchises and introducing newer styles. Shares fell approximately 8% on March 22.

March 22, 2024: PSH published its 2023 annual report. It supplies the manager's public portfolio and investment framework, not a current recommendation concerning this candidate.

May 14, 2024: Nike closed at $92.79 on 7.811 million shares, approximately 24.8% below the preceding one-year intraday high. John Donahoe remains CEO; innovation and wholesale cooperation are the stated operating levers.

The timeline deliberately ends here. A careful analyst has evidence of multiple warnings, margin pressure and a channel/product transition, but not a later earnings release or management action. Any proposed catalyst must be something plausible from this information set, not an event whose identity or timing depends on knowledge outside the cutoff.

This distinction preserves the actual uncertainty facing a prospective investor.
