Title: March results and a warning about the next product transition
Source: compiled from: https://investors.nike.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/24/nike-inc-reports-fiscal-2024-third-quarter-results; https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/nike-beats-quarterly-revenue-estimates-discounts-draw-demand-2024-03-21
Date: 2024-03-22

Nike's March 21 release covers the quarter ended February 29. Revenue was $12.429 billion compared with $12.390 billion, approximately flat. Direct revenue was approximately $5.4 billion, also approximately flat; digital declined 3% reported and 4% currency neutral. Wholesale revenue increased 3% to approximately $6.6 billion.

Converse declined 19% reported to $495 million. Nike Brand revenue grew 2%, with currency-neutral gains in North America, Greater China and APLA offset by EMEA declines. Product and geographic performance are therefore mixed, not uniformly weak or uniformly recovering.

Gross margin rose 150 basis points to 44.8%, including a 50-basis-point restructuring detriment. Pricing and lower ocean freight/logistics helped, while higher product input costs partly offset the benefits. Selling and administrative expense increased 7% to $4.226 billion, including $340 million restructuring charges.

Donahoe said: “We’re encouraged by the progress we’ve seen, as we build a multiyear cycle of new innovation, sharpen our brand storytelling and work with our wholesale partners to elevate and grow the marketplace.” This identifies innovation and channel relationships as management's stated repair levers.

Friend said: “We are taking action to build a faster, more efficient NIKE and maximize the impact of our new innovation cycle.” The statement supplies a direction, not a quantified earnings guarantee.

Reuters's March 21 article, updated March 22, reports management expecting first-half fiscal 2025 revenue to shrink by a low-single-digit percentage as it cuts back on established product franchises. This is the important forward warning available before May 14. It should not be replaced with a more severe forecast from a later earnings cycle.

The latest reported inventory reduction is encouraging for balance-sheet discipline, but does not show that new products are replacing the volume of older styles. A careful analyst should separately model a period of weaker sales, restructuring benefits and the time required for innovation to reach meaningful scale.

The release and report contain no announced chief-executive replacement. A thesis can assume operational improvements are possible under existing or future leadership, but cannot treat a particular management change as an established catalyst.
