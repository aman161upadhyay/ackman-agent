The fiscal 2023 sales increase did not translate into higher EPS, illustrating the impact of inventory, promotions, costs and mix. A broad brand franchise is not immune to margin compression.

Q3 fiscal 2024 revenue was $12,429 million, effectively flat. Gross margin increased 150 basis points to 44.8%, but EBIT declined 8% to $1,352 million. GAAP net income was $1,172 million and diluted EPS $0.77, down 3%. Excluding restructuring, company-adjusted EPS was $0.98.

Nine-month revenue was $38,756 million, up 1%; diluted EPS was $2.74 versus $2.57, and company-defined EBIT margin was 12.6% versus 13.0%. The tax rate and repurchases help explain why EPS can improve despite lower operating margin.

February cash and equivalents were $8,960 million and short-term investments $1,613 million. Inventories were $7,726 million, down 13%. Long-term debt was $8,930 million. Leases and other obligations are not included in that debt figure.

Quarterly dividends were $562 million and repurchases $866 million, retiring 7.9 million shares. Buybacks can support per-share results but do not repair demand. A consistent independently recovered ROIC series and latest-quarter FCF reconciliation are unavailable. No future fiscal-year cash flow or earnings result is used to complete the table.
