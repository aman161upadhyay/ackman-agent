Revenue recovered 13.2% in 2010, calculated from the reported totals. For like-for-like 2008 comparison, the annual report separately supplies pro-forma revenue of C$5,349.2 million including DM&E throughout the year. Using that denominator changes the apparent 2009 contraction. Adjusted 2009 operating ratio was 81.7%, versus reported 81.1%; property gains and a shortline lease termination affected comparability.

H1 operating cash less property additions was negative C$4.3 million, calculated, before C$91.4 million of cash dividends. This conventional cash-flow subtraction differs from CP's defined “free cash,” which also considers investing flows, dividends and specified adjustments. Q2 conventional free cash flow alone was negative C$6.1 million. Neither figure should be annualized through a seasonal railway.

June 30 cash was C$267.8 million; current debt C$278.8 million and long-term debt C$3,918.8 million. Calculated gross debt is C$4,197.6 million and debt less cash C$3,929.8 million, excluding pension liabilities of C$1,019.9 million. Annual CFO/capex series, buyback totals and a verified issuer ROIC series are not available in this extracted snapshot; EPS is not a substitute for them.
