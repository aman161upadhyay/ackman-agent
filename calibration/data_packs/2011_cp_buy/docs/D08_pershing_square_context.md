Barron's Jonathan Laing, May 23, 2011, put Pershing Square funds under management at US$10.3 billion, versus US$6.7 billion in August 2008. This is the latest authenticated firmwide AUM figure in this source set; it is not September's exact NAV or a measure of immediately available cash. A concentrated new investment must be assessed against substantial existing exposures.

CNBC's Margaret Popper, September 14, reports the June 30 disclosed equity portfolio at US$6.4 billion, with five positions approximately 84% of value:

| Holding | Approximate weight of disclosed portfolio |
|---|---:|
| J.C. Penney | 21% |
| General Growth Properties | 19% |
| Fortune Brands | 17% |
| Citigroup | 15% |
| Kraft | 12% |

These are rounded 13F-style portfolio weights reported by CNBC, not percentages of firm NAV. They omit cash, shorts, many derivatives and non-reportable securities. No audited cash balance is available. Market Folly's August 30 account of the investor letter says Ackman deployed US$600 million during recent volatility; the source does not establish how much uncommitted capital remains.

The publicly expressed approach is concentration, intrinsic-value analysis and action to improve businesses. The New York Post, June 17, reproduces this May 25 investor-letter statement: “We believe that these larger businesses generally offer greater opportunities for the kinds of corporate change that we often pursue.” The point is an opportunity to influence an enterprise, not merely to trade a price rebound.

Market Folly quotes his contemporary Citigroup analysis: “Based on this disclosure, we believe that Citi is adequately capitalized to withstand the losses that may result from adverse outcomes from the Greek and other Eurozone debt crises.” That reasoning explicitly considers an adverse scenario rather than only expected earnings. It is sector context, not a statement about CP.

CNBC's profile describes earlier McDonald's restructuring proposals, General Growth real-estate involvement and costly Target/Borders experiences. Those examples show both willingness to pursue governance change and the danger of treating asset value as sufficient protection. There is no authenticated pre-cutoff Ackman statement specifically endorsing CP in this source set; none is supplied as a fabricated quotation. Exact recent exits and a full cash reconciliation remain unavailable.
