Title: From Allergan partnership to Salix commitment
Source: compiled from: https://www.cnbc.com/2015/01/07/cnbc-excerpts-cnbc-exclusive-pershing-square-capital-management-founder-ceo-bill-ackman-speaks-with-cnbcs-squawk-box-today.html; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/885590/000088559015000015/0000885590-15-000015.txt; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1009356/000119312515057077/d878661dex991.htm; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/885590/000119312515059044/d879266dex991.htm
Date: 2015-02-25

**2014 — attempted Allergan combination.** Valeant and Pershing Square pursued Allergan through a public acquisition campaign. Allergan challenged both the offer and Valeant's model. Actavis ultimately agreed to buy Allergan instead. The result permits a profitable target-stock investment without validating every operational forecast made by the unsuccessful bidder.

**January 7 — legal-strategy reflection.** CNBC's interview transcript quotes Ackman discussing a mistake in launching a tender offer after advisers reviewed communications. He says the opposition could sue and use the dispute for adverse publicity. This is an outstanding litigation/reputational issue, not a court determination that Pershing's conduct was illegal.

**January 8 — annual guidance.** Valeant raised 2015 revenue guidance to US$9.2–US$9.3 billion and Cash EPS to US$10.10–US$10.40. Source: https://www.rttnews.com/2439139/valeant-pharmaceuticals-international-vrx-is-soaring-after-guidance-increase.aspx. The outlook predates the Salix agreement and must not be described as combined-company guidance.

**February 22 — Salix agreement.** Boards approve US$158 per share in cash, approximately US$14.5 billion enterprise value. The announcement says bank financing is fully committed and closing is expected in Q2 subject to customary conditions and tender requirements. It projects over US$500 million annual run-rate cost synergies within six months and over 20% Cash EPS accretion in 2016. Inventory reduction means only modest 2015 accretion.

**February 23 — earnings and financing detail.** Management discusses 16% Q4 same-store organic growth and approximately 5.6x initial combined leverage. Dendreon consideration is described as US$415 million including a pipeline product/tax attributes; management sees an underutilized infrastructure and manufacturing savings. These plans add multiple simultaneous integration demands.

**February 25 — original annual filing.** The 10-K provides a substantially more complete basis for reviewing product sales, acquisition accounting, contingencies, debt and cash-flow adjustments than headline Cash EPS alone. It is available before the cutoff.

The next identifiable tests are completion of the financing amendment, Salix closing, wholesaler-inventory normalization, product approvals and delivery against organic-growth/deleveraging assumptions. These are uncertainties to monitor, not accomplished catalysts. No later price decline or investigative outcome belongs in this timeline.
