A low-teens acquisition multiple can generate EPS accretion when funded with cheaper debt and tax savings, but that arithmetic does not establish shareholder value. The relevant price includes assumed debt, future integration cost, required product investment and an appropriate risk premium. Repeat acquisitions cannot be valued as costless future growth.
