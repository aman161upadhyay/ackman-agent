Reported revenue growth is approximately 66% in 2013 and 43% in 2014, principally acquisition-driven. The 10-K attributes US$2,279.9 million incremental 2014 product sales to acquired businesses. Consolidated size growth is not the same as the return on acquisition capital.

Cash EPS for 2014 is US$8.34 versus US$6.24 in 2013. The bridge begins with US$913.5 million attributable GAAP income and adds net adjustments of US$1,936.3 million. Major adjustments include US$1,599.0 million amortization/impairment and other specified charges and US$388.0 million restructuring/integration/acquisition costs; gains, taxes and other items partly offset them. This measure is neither GAAP EPS nor cash flow.

Year-end common shares are 334.4 million, versus 333.0 million in 2013 and 303.9 million in 2012. The annual diluted weighted average is 341.5 million. Management reports net debt of approximately US$15.3 billion and net leverage about 3.5x adjusted pro-forma EBITDA. That ratio predates Salix.

The 10-K says CFO includes US$397.5 million proceeds connected with the Allergan investment/partnership. Simple CFO less PP&E therefore overstates a clean recurring operating baseline. No dividends were declared in 2012–2014. A consistently defined acquisition-inclusive ROIC and detailed buyback series are not extracted; adjusted EPS growth should not substitute for either.
