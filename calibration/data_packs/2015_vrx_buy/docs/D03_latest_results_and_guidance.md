Title: Strong Q4 growth followed by a large financing commitment
Source: compiled from: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/885590/000119312515057094/d878365dex991.htm; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/885590/000119312515059044/d879266dex991.htm; https://getfilings.com/sec-filings/150108/Valeant-Pharmaceuticals-International-Inc_8-K/d847143dex991.htm
Date: 2015-02-23

Valeant released results on February 22 and discussed them February 23. Q4 revenue was US$2.280 billion, up 10%, despite a US$113 million negative foreign-exchange effect. Management reports 16% same-store organic growth, 8% Bausch + Lomb organic growth, GAAP EPS of US$1.56 and Cash EPS of US$2.58. Cash EPS excludes the Allergan investment gain and rose approximately 20%.

Q4 GAAP operating cash flow of US$815.6 million includes transaction-related benefits. Adjusted operating cash flow is US$624.2 million, excluding US$286.7 million net Allergan investment gain but adding back specified restructuring, legal and working-capital items. Neither number is a pure recurring-owner-earnings measure without examining the bridge.

TheStreet's February 23 analyst roundup reports FactSet consensus of US$2.23 billion revenue and US$2.55 adjusted EPS, making the published results modest beats on those measures: https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/valeant-surges-on-salix-acquisition-what-wall-streets-saying-13054596. The same source reports previous company revenue guidance of US$2.2 billion and EPS above US$2.55.

For Q1 2015 management expects Cash EPS of at least US$2.30 and same-store growth of 10–15%. The January 8 annual forecast was US$9.2–US$9.3 billion revenue and US$10.10–US$10.40 Cash EPS. The February release says a new full-year outlook reflecting Salix, Dendreon and operating outperformance will come with Q1 results. The old annual range is a pre-Salix baseline, not an updated combined-company promise.

The Salix call projects roughly 5.6x net debt/pro-forma adjusted EBITDA immediately after the transaction, partly because reducing Salix wholesaler inventory depresses near-term EBITDA. Management targets below 4x by the second half of 2016 and forecasts over US$7.5 billion pro-forma EBITDA and US$4 billion free cash flow in 2016 before mandatory repayments.

An expected approximately 5% combined-company tax rate also supports accretion. These are forward management assumptions, not realized cash flows. A credit amendment is expected within two weeks; a US$7.5 billion backstop would then fall away. Financing is committed, but capital allocation, integration and deleveraging remain execution-dependent.
