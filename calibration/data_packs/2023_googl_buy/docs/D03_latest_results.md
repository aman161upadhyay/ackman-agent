Title: Q4 earnings, cost restructuring and the AI investment agenda
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204423000013/googexhibit991q42022.htm
Date: 2023-02-02

Q4 consolidated revenue of $76,048 million grew only 1%, or 7% at constant currency. Operating income fell to $18,160 million from $21,885 million, and margin fell to 24% from 29%. Net income was $13,624 million and EPS $1.05, versus $20,642 million and $1.53 a year earlier.

The advertising slowdown is visible inside the aggregate. Search/other revenue declined to $42,604 million from $43,301 million. YouTube ads declined to $7,963 million from $8,633 million, while network ads declined to $8,475 million from $9,305 million. These figures predate any measurable commercial impact from the new Bard product. The current advertising weakness cannot be attributed entirely to a newly announced chatbot.

Cloud revenue rose to $7,315 million from $5,541 million, approximately 32% growth calculated from the table. Its operating loss narrowed to $480 million from $890 million. Other Bets lost $1,631 million, more than its $1,450 million loss a year earlier. Neither segment is a current offset to Services' profit on these reported definitions.

Pichai says the company is well positioned as AI reaches an inflection point and describes a journey to “re-engineer our cost structure in a durable way”. Porat says there is significant work underway on costs to support investment in high-growth priorities. The statements combine reinvestment with efficiency, rather than promise an immediate elimination of research spending.

January's approximately 12,000-role workforce reduction is expected to produce $1.9-$2.3 billion of severance/related charges, mostly in Q1. Office reductions are expected to add approximately $0.5 billion of Q1 exit costs. These are charges and anticipated actions, not already realized annual savings.

Changing server and certain network-equipment useful lives to six years is expected to reduce 2023 depreciation by approximately $3.4 billion for existing assets. That raises accounting income without an equivalent immediate cash saving. A forward EPS analysis should separate this estimate change from genuine efficiency.

DeepMind will move from Other Bets to unallocated corporate costs beginning January. Historical segment comparisons must respect that reporting change. The release does not provide numerical full-year revenue or EPS guidance.
