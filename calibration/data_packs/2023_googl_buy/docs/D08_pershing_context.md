Title: Concentrated portfolio and public investment context

Date: 2023-02-09

The latest pre-cutoff quarterly 13F was filed November 14, 2022 for September 30. It showed a concentrated US-listed equity portfolio: Lowe's, Chipotle, Restaurant Brands, Hilton, Canadian Pacific and Howard Hughes. Approximate weights within the reported $7.88 billion US equity total were 24.7%, 21.1%, 16.3%, 15.4%, 12.9% and 9.6% respectively. These weights are filing-portfolio weights, not current NAV allocations.

The 13F excludes UMG's foreign-listed shares and the manager's macro derivatives, as well as cash. It must not be treated as either total AUM or the full economic portfolio. An updated year-end filing is not yet public at this replay cutoff.

The February 9 investor presentation gives PSH AUM of $12,916 million and equity of $10,580 million as of February 7. Its definition includes fund bond proceeds. This is the listed fund, not a verified manager-wide total. A same-day unrestricted cash balance and precise current security weights were not recovered.

Netflix is a relevant recent exit already public. In his April 20, 2022 letter, Ackman wrote: “We require a high degree of predictability in the businesses in which we invest due to the highly concentrated nature of our portfolio.” The letter distinguishes admiration for management from the ability to forecast a changed business model. This is a pre-cutoff investment standard, not a statement about Alphabet.

That prior experience poses a useful discipline question. A new technology narrative may create an opportunity if the core economics remain understandable and conservatively valued. Alternatively, it may widen the range of outcomes beyond what is acceptable for a large holding. A lower price does not independently answer which applies.

The public portfolio is dominated by durable consumer franchises, recurring fee businesses and long-lived assets. Those holdings provide context for quality, cash generation and capital allocation as underwriting priorities. No verified pre-February-10 Ackman statement specifically endorsing Alphabet was recovered, and none is supplied from a later investor letter.

The appropriate context is therefore a concentrated investor with finite capital and an explicit predictability requirement, not an assumption that the fund must own every high-quality technology platform or that the latest stock-price decline establishes the manager's eventual response.
