Amounts are $ millions except EPS. The annual report supplies a consistent 2023–25 series. Company-defined FCF deducts distributions to noncontrolling holders as well as capex; CFO minus physical spending alone would overstate the cash attributable to common owners.

FY2025 revenue increased 8% and operating profit 16%. Reported operating profit includes disposition gains, severance and other items; the annual report notes $273 million gains on dispositions and $157 million employee-severance charges. Adjusted profitability should use the full company reconciliation, not only selective adjustments.

FY2025 adjusted operating margin was 50.4%, compared with reported 42.2% in the release's more precise presentation. The difference reflects meaningful amortization and other adjustments. Calling adjusted profit “cash earnings” without examining those items would be misleading.

Year-end cash/equivalents were $1.745 billion. Short-term debt was $718 million and long-term debt $12.370 billion, total $13.088 billion. Calculated net debt was $11.343 billion before other enterprise-value adjustments. Diluted weighted-average shares were 305.1 million.

The company returned $6.2 billion in 2025, including $5.0 billion repurchases and $1.2 billion dividends. It describes that as 113% of adjusted FCF, which is not identical to the $5.135 billion unadjusted FCF in the table.

Latest Q4 revenue was $3.916 billion, up 9%. Complete quarterly cash conversion is not independently reconstructed here. A consistent ROIC series and buyback history for all three years remain missing.

The cash evidence supports a capital-efficient enterprise, but acquisition amortization, minority economics, restructuring and segment mix must remain visible when translating reported earnings into owners' cash.
