A reliable historical comparison requires the correct price, adjusted earnings forecast and observation date, consistently measured across periods. Apparent discounts are not meaningful if the business perimeter or accounting denominator changes. The economic question is whether this price compensates for workflow disruption, issuance cyclicality and transition costs while allowing durable benchmark/data franchises to compound. A lower multiple alone does not establish that outcome.
