Title: Moody's and MSCI separate benchmark economics from generic software
Source: compiled from: https://s203.q4cdn.com/694693571/files/doc_financials/2025/Annual/MOODYS-CORP_AR_BOOKMARK_2026_V1-Reduced.pdf; https://ir.msci.com/news-releases/news-release-details/msci-reports-financial-results-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/64040/000006404026000007/a4q2025earningsrelease.htm
Date: 2026-02-26

Moody's is the closest public ratings/data comparison; MSCI is a useful index and analytics comparison. Neither matches all SPGI divisions, so corporate multiple comparisons require attention to mix and earnings adjustments.

Moody's 2025 report gives revenue approximately $7.7 billion, up 9%, and adjusted operating margin 51.1%, up 300 basis points. Reported EPS was $13.67 and adjusted EPS $14.94, up 21% and 20% respectively. Those are annual company figures, not the preliminary search snippet's mislabeled EPS series.

MSCI's January28 Q4 release reports revenue $822.5 million, up 10.6%, with organic growth 10.2%. Recurring subscription revenue increased 7.5% and asset-based fees 20.7%. Operating margin was 56.4%, adjusted EBITDA margin 62.2%, and adjusted EPS $4.66, up approximately11%.

SPGI's FY2025 adjusted operating margin was 50.4%, while reported margin was 42.2%. Directly comparing it to MSCI's EBITDA margin would be wrong because depreciation, amortization and other adjustments differ. The peer evidence nevertheless demonstrates high margins and recurring/benchmark economics.

The shared moat is not simply software code. Recognized ratings, adopted benchmarks, trusted datasets and embedded customer processes can create high switching and coordination costs. AI may alter the interface without automatically recreating all institutional acceptance or data rights.

The shared risk is that customers demand lower prices or different licensing as AI expands access. Benchmark licensing and proprietary inputs may retain value while desktop or seat-based products face pressure. Moody's and MSCI are not immune merely because they have familiar brand names.

Current operating growth is counterevidence to a claim of already-observed wholesale displacement. It does not settle long-run economics. Issuance cycles, market-sensitive asset fees and client budgets can weaken even without technology disruption.

Exact March31 peer forward EPS, multiples and harmonized FCF yields were not authenticated. No precise relative-value ranking is supplied. The useful benchmark is the combination of recurring revenue, profit margins and distinct contractual/IP economics, which should be compared with the actual threatened SPGI products rather than with generic software-sector sentiment.
