Reuters' February10 report, syndicated by US News, describes SPGI's forecast below Wall Street expectations amid growing unease over AI disruption in software and services. It notes that financial-data and analytics businesses were swept into the broader technology selloff.

Reuters' February4 account describes nearly $1 trillion erased from software and services stocks as investors debated the implications of Anthropic's advances. The market concern is that AI agents can perform research, analysis or workflow tasks previously sold as dedicated software or professional information services.

The Journal's February10 report gives a concrete earnings comparison: adjusted guidance $19.40–19.65 versus $20.02 consensus. Shares fell 9.7% to $401.08, and Moody's fell 6.8%. This combines company-specific guidance disappointment with a broader narrative, rather than proving either factor explains the entire move.

The serious bear mechanism is loss of pricing power or seats in financial workflows, cheaper access to substitute data, and changed distribution economics as AI becomes the user's interface. Even if underlying data remains useful, a platform could retain less of the economics if intermediaries control the customer relationship.
