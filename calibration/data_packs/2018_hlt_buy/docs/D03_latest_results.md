Title: Latest available earnings — room growth, fee growth and buybacks
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1585689/000158568918000120/a2018-q2earningsrelease.htm
Date: 2018-07-25

Q2 adjusted EBITDA was $555 million, up 10% and above the company's guidance range. Net income was $217 million, up 44%; diluted EPS was $0.71, and EPS adjusted for special items $0.70. The comparative adjusted EPS was $0.51.

Currency-neutral system-wide comparable RevPAR grew 4.0%, with the US up 3.5% and international markets up 5.9%. Both occupancy and average daily rate contributed. First-half RevPAR growth was 3.9%.

Management/franchise fee revenue grew 11% in Q2 and 12% in the first half. The release attributes growth to comparable RevPAR, increased license fees and new properties. This is important because the company's growth mechanism is not confined to same-hotel pricing or occupancy.

Hilton opened 123 hotels totaling 17,100 rooms in Q2. Net additions were 15,800 rooms, up 18% from the prior-year quarter. Management projects approximately 6.5% net-unit growth for the year.

The development pipeline reached 362,000 rooms in more than 2,370 hotels across 105 countries/territories. Approximately 186,000 rooms were under construction and 194,000 outside the US. A pipeline is not equivalent to an immediately earning asset, and not every signed project is assured to open on its original schedule.

Nassetta says: “This growth coupled with continued net unit growth resulted in Adjusted EBITDA growth of 10 percent, exceeding our expectations.” The source connects operating performance to both demand and distribution-system expansion.

Hilton repurchased 18.5 million shares in Q2 for approximately $1.3 billion, at an average $72.01, including shares from HNA and Blackstone exits. It returned nearly $1.6 billion through July in buybacks and dividends. The quarter's dividend was $0.15 a share.

Capital returns must be read alongside financing: April's $1.5 billion 5.125% notes helped fund the HNA repurchase and term-debt repayment. Repurchases are not all funded by current-period free cash flow.

The July outlook calls for full-year RevPAR growth of 3%-4%, fee revenue growth of 9%-11% and adjusted EBITDA growth of 8%-10%. October 24 results had not occurred at the October 15 cutoff and are excluded.
