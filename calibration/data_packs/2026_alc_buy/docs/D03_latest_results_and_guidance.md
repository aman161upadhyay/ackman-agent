Title: May5 — launch-driven growth with weak implantable momentum
Source: compiled from: https://s1.q4cdn.com/963204942/files/doc_earnings/2026/q1/earnings-result/Q1-2026-Press-Release.pdf; https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/swiss-eye-care-firm-alcons-quarterly-revenue-rises-driven-by-product-launches-2026-05-06
Date: 2026-05-06

Alcon reported Q1 sales$2.685billion, up10% reported and6% constant currency. Surgical grew10% reported/6% currency-neutral to$1.460billion; Vision Care grew9%/6% to$1.225billion. Management highlighted Unity and Tryptyr launch momentum.

Implantables revenue was$438million, up4% reported but only1% constant currency. Consumables were$769million, up8%/4%, and equipment/other$253million, up27%/23%. The largest relative growth came from equipment, while the premium-lens/implantable question remains live.

Contact lenses grew7% reported and4% currency-neutral to$738million. Ocular health grew13%/10% to$487million, supported by dry-eye products. These different categories should not be assumed to carry identical incremental margins.

Reported gross margin was56.8%; core gross margin63.0%. Reported operating margin was10.9% and core21.2%. Higher R&D and launch-related selling/marketing expense, plus incremental tariffs, weighed on margins; operating leverage and manufacturing efficiency provided offsets.

Reported diluted EPS was$0.39 and core$0.85. CFO was$418million and FCF$279million. These figures provide an existing cash-earnings base, but the core-to-reported reconciliation remains economically meaningful.

The company maintained constant-currency sales-growth guidance of5–7%, projected core-margin improvement of70–170basis points, and raised core EPS-growth guidance to10–13% constant currency. The release does not provide an exact USD EPS range in the extracted table.

Net tariff expense after mitigation is estimated at$100–150million for2026. The reduction from February expectations is intended to be reinvested in the business, not automatically retained entirely as extra profit. Capex is expected in the mid-single digits as a share of sales.

The board authorized$1.5billion repurchases over three years. Authorization is not completed execution. A CHF0.28 dividend was approved; no USD yield is invented without the relevant exchange rate.

Reuters reports sales below the$2.71billion LSEG consensus, despite the10% headline increase. That modest sales miss and weak implantables help explain why a growing business can disappoint the market. No second-quarter result or later clinical status belongs in this snapshot.

Guidance is a management forecast, not an observed outcome. The review should model slower adoption and persistent spending as downside scenarios rather than extrapolate the launch quarter mechanically.
