The February11 presentation gives February9 gross core-fund AUM$19.748billion and core equity$16.077billion. PSH gross assets were$17.872billion and equity$14.202billion. These dated observations establish scale, not current June cash.

The April30 announcement of the combined PSUS/manager IPO reports$5billion aggregate gross proceeds. Fund and manager capital belong to different entities. It would be wrong to assume the whole offering is available to PSH, or to add it mechanically to old gross AUM as undeployed buying power.

Exact June deployment, vehicle-specific cash and current NAV weights were not independently recovered. Publicly discussed franchises include Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Brookfield, Uber, Universal Music, Restaurant Brands, Howard Hughes and Fannie/Freddie; Microsoft ownership was publicly discussed May15.

Those holdings illustrate a preference for durable economic roles, recurring cash and long-term reinvestment. They do not prove that any new healthcare candidate qualifies. Industry labels and the apparent stability of medical demand cannot substitute for evidence on pricing, competitive position, reinvestment and regulation.

The stated framework favors simple, predictable FCF, strong management, durable entry barriers, a sound balance sheet and limited capital-markets dependence. Alcon's installed workflows and consumables could fit that framework, while developmental clinical risk needs to remain separate.

The core company already generates substantial FCF and has multiple operating categories. A base investment forecast can therefore be built without assuming AURN001 approval. If the valuation requires that approval, the nature of the opportunity changes toward development-stage risk rather than an established compounder.

Concentration also requires assessing overlap in policy, consumer spending and currency factors. Vision care may diversify product-market demand relative to technology or restaurant franchises, but tariffs, manufacturing, healthcare access and innovation risk remain real.

No verified pre-June30 Alcon-specific Ackman thesis statement is supplied. The decision must be reasoned from current public operating evidence and prior principles. Permanent capital can tolerate a measured launch cycle, but should not be used to excuse an unbounded recovery timetable or an unpriced clinical dependency.
