The FY2025 difference between CFO less purchases and defined FCF reflects property-sale treatment; the table preserves the published FCF rather than forcing exact equality with gross purchases. FY2025 sales grew5% reported and4% constant currency, while core EPS barely increased in reported dollars and core margin declined.

The company returned$848million during2025, including$682million repurchases and$166million dividends. Repurchases retired approximately8.4million shares. This is an observed capital return, separate from the new authorization announced inMay2026.

At March31, cash/equivalents were$1.578billion. Financial debt was$577million current plus$4.157billion noncurrent, total$4.734billion. Calculated net debt was$3.156billion. Q1 diluted weighted-average shares were490.2million and reported net income$189million.

Q1 FCF increased through operating generation, but launches, R&D, tariffs and working capital affect quarterly conversion. A growing equipment base can require upfront spending before consumables mature.

A consistent ROIC series, maintenance/growth capex split and full three-year capital-return table were not extracted. The operating and cash evidence nevertheless establishes a substantial existing business independent of developmental clinical success, while preserving the distinction between core and reported profitability.
