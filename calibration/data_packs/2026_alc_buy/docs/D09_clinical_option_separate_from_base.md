Title: AURN001 — a pivotal-stage option, not required core earnings

Date: 2026-05-05

Aurion's April8 announcement states that the first patients have been dosed in ASTRA, a pivotal Phase3 study evaluating AURN001 for corneal edema secondary to corneal endothelial dysfunction. The relevant clinical proposition is restoring corneal clarity and vision through cell therapy, not a routine extension of an already approved US commercial product.

The announcement describes a randomized, double-masked study comparing treatment with placebo, enrolling at12 US sites. Its primary endpoint is the percentage of participants gaining at least15 letters, or three lines, of best-corrected visual acuity at month6. Secondary endpoints include changes in corneal thickness and visual acuity.

First-patient dosing is evidence of trial initiation, not efficacy, safety, completed recruitment, regulatory approval or commercial adoption. Later registry pages can display updated status and estimated completion dates; those later updates are not imported into this June freeze.

The base company already generated$1.733billion defined FCF in2025 and$279million inQ1. It sells surgical equipment, consumables, implantables, contact lenses and ocular-health products. A valuation can therefore start with an established operating business rather than an assumed future therapy launch.

That separation is essential. A base forecast should include ongoing R&D and the costs of supporting development, while assigning no unapproved sales unless clearly identified as an upside scenario. It should not count a potential therapy's revenue and simultaneously remove all associated clinical spending from normalized profit.

A separate option analysis would require probability of technical/regulatory success, addressable patient demand, treatment economics, capacity, reimbursement, ownership economics, launch timing and remaining investment. The source set does not support a defensible numerical expected value for those inputs.

Clinical failure would eliminate some potential upside and could leave sunk costs. It need not destroy the existing consumables/contact-lens franchise. Conversely, even a successful trial would not guarantee a rapid profitable launch.

The investment test is whether core recurring cash generation and a reasonable margin path justify the current price without the option. If not, describing the position as an uncomplicated predictable compounder would conceal its dependence on developmental risk. No later results or approval are assumed.
