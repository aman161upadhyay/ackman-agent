Title: Concentrated equity duration and already disclosed protection

Date: 2023-07-03

The June 30 update reports ten long issuer positions, no issuer shorts and 116% net issuer equity/debt exposure. Direct/indirect hedges, cash and expense items are excluded from those counts and exposures. “No shorts” in this table therefore does not prove no macro protection.

PSH AUM was $13.081 billion, core strategy AUM $14.9766 billion and total firm AUM including UMG co-investment vehicles $16.1545 billion. The footnotes add back $1.8 billion and €500 million bond principal and do not deduct accrued performance fees.

These are not interchangeable with equity NAV or dry powder. A premium expressed as a fraction of gross AUM can understate its size relative to shareholder capital. Co-investment capital is also not unrestricted cash for every vehicle.

PSH returned 10.0% net and 10.9% gross through June. Those performance figures are not current position weights or proof that the portfolio has little downside.

The February presentation and annual report describe concentrated franchise ownership, including consumer, hospitality, media, retail, transport and real-estate exposures. Businesses with durable future earnings can still be sensitive to discount rates through valuation and financing channels.

The March annual report already discloses long-rate option protection. The August decision is therefore not clean first inception. It can be interpreted as whether to retain, add, reshape or close protection given current yields and option value.

The hedge may serve a portfolio purpose even when a standalone trader would have monetized an earlier gain. Conversely, retaining protection has opportunity cost and can lose value if rates fall or time passes.

Exact August equity weights, aggregate portfolio duration, cash, current option terms and contract-level P&L were not recovered. No mechanically optimal hedge ratio is derived.

The relevant portfolio test is whether long-rate stress materially harms expected equity value and whether the option payoff supplies useful offsetting liquidity at an affordable cost. A broad macro narrative does not establish that match, and the fund's previous hedge successes should not substitute for underwriting the present structure.
