Title: Information sequence through August 2
Source: compiled from: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20230726a.htm; https://www.bea.gov/sites/default/files/2023-07/gdp2q23_adv.pdf; https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy1662; https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy1671; https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/fitch-cuts-us-governments-aaa-credit-rating-by-one-notch-2023-08-01
Date: 2023-08-02

**March 29:** The manager's 2022 annual report publicly documents interest-rate option protection and year-end sensitivity data. This establishes that the later public long-rate thesis is not necessarily an August initiation.

**July 3:** The June month-end portfolio update reports 116% net issuer exposure and AUM definitions that include PSH debt. Its zero issuer shorts do not include all hedge categories.

**July 12:** June CPI is released, showing substantial headline disinflation and lower monthly core inflation while year-over-year core remains elevated. Later inflation releases are not yet available.

**July 26:** The Fed raises its target range to 5.25%-5.50% and retains a data-dependent stance. It notes inflation remains elevated and recognizes the role of cumulative tightening and policy lags.

**July 27:** The advance Q2 GDP estimate is 2.4% annualized. This is the then-current release vintage; later revisions must not replace it in the decision set.

**July 28:** The June personal-income/outlays release adds further disinflation evidence, with core PCE still above the Fed objective.

**July 31:** Treasury raises its July-September net marketable borrowing estimate to $1.007 trillion, reflecting cash-balance and fiscal-flow assumptions. The 30-year benchmark yield is 4.02%.

**August 1:** Fitch downgrades US sovereign credit to AA+ from AAA. Treasury disputes the decision. The 30-year benchmark yield is 4.11%.

**August 2:** Treasury announces a $103 billion refunding package with $19 billion approximate new cash. Daily H.15 yields are 4.08% at ten years and 4.17% at thirty years.

The pack permits end-of-day information and does not claim those yields were executable before their measurement. The manager's same-day directional social-media statement is withheld to avoid giving the decision label; the later Reuters report verifying it belongs in the key.

No October peak, realized cover, later war or subsequent economic evidence appears here. The test is a contemporaneous decision about an existing protection overlay with incomplete contract terms, not a hindsight exercise that starts at the known low and ends at the known high.
