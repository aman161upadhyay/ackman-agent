Cash and equivalents were $9.034 billion at year-end 2025 versus $7.805 billion a year earlier. Short- plus long-term debt was $14.463 billion versus $15.583 billion. Calculated net debt using those definitions was $5.429 billion. Q1 cash was approximately $12.26 billion and gross debt $14.4 billion, substantially affected by a nonrecurring cash receipt.

The $2.8 billion Warner-related termination payment was recognized in interest and other income, boosting Q1 EPS and cash flow but not recurring operating income. FCF of $5.094 billion less that receipt is approximately $2.294 billion before tax/timing adjustments; this is an illustrative subtraction, not a company-defined adjusted FCF measure.

Content cash expenditure is primarily embedded in operating cash flow rather than the property-capex line. A small physical-capex number must not be mistaken for minimal economic reinvestment. The April letter expects annual cash content spending around 1.1 times amortization. Content cash-versus-expense timing is central to the earnings-to-FCF comparison.

Netflix repurchased 13.5 million shares for $1.3 billion during Q1, with $6.8 billion authorization remaining. Share count and per-share comparisons must use split-consistent data. No consistent ROIC series is included.
