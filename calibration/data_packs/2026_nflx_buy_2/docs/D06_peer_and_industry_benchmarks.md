Title: Streaming competitors and the value of scale
Source: compiled from: https://investors.thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/news-details/2026/The-Walt-Disney-Company-Reports-First-Quarter-Earnings-for-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx; https://www.wbd.com/news/warner-bros-discovery-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528026000137/ex991_q126.htm
Date: 2026-04-16

Disney and Warner Bros. Discovery provide public benchmarks for subscription streaming, but neither is a pure comparable. Disney also owns experiences and sports operations; Warner includes studios and linear networks. Netflix's consolidated margin cannot be compared directly to either company's total corporate margin as if their economics were identical.

| Public observation | Netflix | Disney | Warner Bros. Discovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relevant release | April 16, 2026 | February 2, 2026 | February 26, 2026 |
| Period | Q1 2026 | Fiscal Q1 2026 | Q4 2025 |
| Streaming profitability | $3.96bn consolidated operating income; 32.3% margin | Entertainment SVOD OI $450m; 8.4% margin | Streaming adjusted EBITDA $393m |
| Relevant growth | Revenue +16% | SVOD revenue +11% | Streaming subscribers 131.6m, +3.5m sequentially |

Disney's SVOD operating income rose $189 million from $261 million, or 72%, and management attributed growth to subscription and advertising revenue, partly offset by programming, production and marketing costs. This demonstrates improving competitor economics, rather than an assumption that rivals will remain permanently loss-making.

Warner's total quarterly revenue was $9.5 billion, down 7% excluding currency, with total adjusted EBITDA $2.2 billion, down 20%. Streaming EBITDA fell 7% excluding currency. Those consolidated pressures include legacy businesses and should not be attributed automatically to HBO's consumer proposition.

Netflix's more than 325 million year-end memberships are much larger than Warner's 131.6 million streaming subscribers, but definitions differ. Scale can spread content expense across a larger paying audience and give greater flexibility to fund diverse programming. It does not establish that any particular title earns its cost or that competitors cannot retain valuable niches.

Advertising adds another competitive arena, including YouTube and social platforms. Netflix can combine premium long-form inventory with subscription revenue, but advertisers can choose many other sources of attention. Its prospective $3 billion ad revenue is still small relative to its subscription-funded business.

Cutoff peer forward multiples and harmonized FCF yields were not authenticated, so no false relative-value ranking is supplied. The measurable distinction is Netflix's demonstrated operating margin and cash conversion versus competitors' improving but different profit metrics. An analyst should distinguish that evidence from a prediction that all competition has ended.
