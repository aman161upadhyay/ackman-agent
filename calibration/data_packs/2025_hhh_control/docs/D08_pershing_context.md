Pershing already has substantial HHH ownership; this is not a blank-slate screen of an unfamiliar company. Its publicly announced exploration of alternatives is relevant evidence of potential influence, though an independent committee and other shareholders remain necessary counterparties.

The latest recovered firm AUM is$18.4988 billion at September30, including debt proceeds and multiple investment vehicles. PSH AUM was$15.1353 billion. These balances are not interchangeable with unrestricted manager cash or committed capital for an acquisition.

At December2023, the annual report shows HHH at11.72% of PSH NAV. Other major weights were Universal Music24.88% directly plus affiliate exposure, Alphabet14.06%, Chipotle13.71%, Restaurant Brands approximately13.50%, Hilton12.15% and Canadian Pacific8.70%. The dated table illustrates concentration and opportunity cost, not current January10 weights.

The public portfolio also gained Brookfield and Nike during2024. Ordinary13F weights exclude foreign holdings and some economic exposures; they should not be treated as complete PSH NAV allocations. Current fund cash and exact vehicle-level financing capacity are not recovered.

Pershing's stated framework emphasizes durable franchises, capable managers, long-term ownership and the ability to help resolve identifiable underperformance. HHH shares the long-duration asset and potential influence characteristics, but is more capital intensive and interest-rate sensitive than an asset-light brand franchisor.

For a large existing owner, a possible strategic transaction can have a different return profile from purchasing a small passive stake. Governance and capital-allocation changes may affect how future land and property cash is reinvested. Those benefits must be incremental and economically grounded, rather than assumed because the owner is well known.

The publicly available strategic alternatives include a possible take-private. They do not yet establish a particular diversified holding-company or insurance-acquisition program. Such a plan cannot be inserted into this pre-announcement pack as fact.

Sizing should therefore distinguish the value of existing fund shares, any new capital committed by another Pershing vehicle, and eventual combined ownership. Company ownership percentages and transaction dollars are not automatically fund NAV weights. That distinction is essential to evaluating whether a strategic arrangement creates attractive per-share value without overstating available capital.
