Title: Interest rates, long cash cycles and minority-owner transaction risk

The January9 Associated Press report carried by the Los Angeles Times says the average30-year mortgage rate rose to6.93%, its fourth consecutive weekly increase and highest level since July. Higher mortgage costs can reduce homebuyer affordability, even when low inventories support demand for selected new communities.

HHH's latest land sales are strong, but that does not remove interest-rate exposure. Builders' willingness to buy acreage depends on their ability to sell homes profitably. Office, retail and apartment values also depend on financing and capitalization rates; rising rates can lower estimated asset values before reported property NOI deteriorates.

The2023 Seaport impairment is a concrete reminder that long-term project forecasts can disappoint. The company reported a$555 million after-tax impairment in that quarter. Although Seaport is now separated, the episode remains relevant when evaluating capital allocation, forecasting discipline and the temptation to label every unsuccessful investment a temporary mark.

The September balance sheet has approximately$5.3 billion debt and$400.7 million cash. Project-level lender commitments are restricted to development purposes. They should not be treated as unrestricted cash available for a take-private, unrelated acquisitions or shareholder distributions.

There is also a governance question. The August8 company release says the largest shareholder is exploring alternatives, possibly acquiring the remaining shares, and the board has formed an independent special committee. A transaction could change ownership or unlock value, but minorities need fair terms and a sound process.

No binding transaction price, financing commitment or final board recommendation is public in the sources before this cutoff. The possibility of a deal should not be modeled as a guaranteed near-term exit at a premium.

The constructive case is that scarce communities, rental assets and future condo proceeds create more value than the market price implies, with a sophisticated owner helping allocation. The bear case is that cash remains tied up in development, debt and weak projects for longer than expected. A credible thesis needs to explain both the asset cushion and the path from asset value to per-share returns.
