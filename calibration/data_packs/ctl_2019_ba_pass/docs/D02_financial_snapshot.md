FY2018 property/equipment additions are approximatelyUS$1.722 billion. CFO less those additions is approximatelyUS$13.600 billion, calculated. This is a useful historical cash measure, not an estimate of cash after an extended interruption in deliveries.

The company repurchased26.1 million shares forUS$9.0 billion during2018. Cash and marketable securities totaledUS$8.6 billion at year-end, consisting ofUS$7.7 billion cash andUS$0.9 billion marketable securities. Total consolidated debt was approximatelyUS$13.8 billion. Net debt after these liquid assets is aboutUS$5.2 billion, calculated.

The release gives586.2 million weighted-average diluted shares for2018. That is an annual earnings denominator, not the exact March15 common share count, particularly after repurchases. Any market capitalization using it must be labeled indicative.

January2019 guidance anticipatesUS$109.5–111.5 billion revenue, GAAP EPSUS$21.90–22.10, core EPSUS$19.90–20.10 andUS$17.0–17.5 billion operating cash flow. The forecasts depend on production and delivery assumptions established before the present event.

Commercial backlog is approximatelyUS$412.3 billion within theUS$490 billion consolidated backlog. An order book cannot by itself pay suppliers while deliveries are suspended. The timing and contract terms of progress payments versus final delivery payments need explicit modeling.

Historical accounting profitability and a modest net-debt figure indicate capacity to absorb some stress. They do not establish a maximum liability or a safe duration. Program economics, inventory, supplier commitments, customer remedies and possible legal costs are separate obligations from balance-sheet financial debt.
