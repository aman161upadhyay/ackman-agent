Title: Safety, certification and cash timing cannot be reduced to one estimate
Source: compiled from: https://www.zeebiz.com/agencies/boeing-prepares-for-max-737-jets-sitting-outside-factory-as-deliveries-halted-90208; https://investors.boeing.com/investors/news/press-release-details/2019/Boeing-Reports-Record-2018-Results-and-Provides-2019-Guidance/default.aspx
Date: 2019-03-15

Reuters reports MAX deliveries have stopped while production continues. The immediate mechanical consequence is increasing finished inventory and a delay in customer payments. The commercial backlog remains substantial, but the company must convert it into aircraft that customers and regulators can accept.

Two fatal accidents involving a relatively new model have created a safety investigation, not just a marketing problem. At this cutoff, the evidence does not establish a complete engineering explanation, the final remedy or approval dates across all relevant jurisdictions.

Keeping production running may preserve supplier continuity and avoid costly stop/start disruptions. It also consumes cash and physical storage space. Reducing production may protect liquidity but create its own industrial and contractual costs. Neither option is costless.

The January results release had emphasized expected record deliveries, higher2019 profits and cash flow. Those forecasts relied on planned commercial deliveries, with other businesses also contributing. They show why investors valued the franchise highly, but are not a forecast incorporating the current grounding.

The bull case is a technically fixable interruption, eventual deliveries from a deep backlog, and a duopoly with high entry barriers. If confidence returns quickly, delayed cash may be recovered rather than permanently lost. Existing defense and service operations provide additional value.

The bear case is broader than a quarter of deferred deliveries. Possible redesign, training, certification differences, legal claims and lost customer confidence may change costs and schedules. An activist board seat cannot itself supply a regulator's approval or an independent accident finding.

It would also be wrong to assume an unlimited liability simply because the facts are uncertain. The honest gap is that a reliable range has not yet been established. The investor needs stress cases rather than an unsupported binary prediction that either nothing is wrong or the franchise is destroyed.

Key monitoring items are the investigation evidence, regulator requirements, customer acceptance, production decisions and cash conversion. No subsequent production halt, final settlements or return-to-service date is included. The current decision must be made with an unusually wide range of possible near-term outcomes.
