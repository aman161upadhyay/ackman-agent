Historical unadjusted March15 close isUS$378.99. The preceding approximatelyone-year window recordsUS$446.01 high andUS$292.47 low. The close is approximately15.0% below that observed high; this is not a claim that the exact same percentage was lost between two weekly closes.

Session volume is26.70 million shares, well above the calculated20-session average10.19 million. Elevated trading reflects the sudden change in uncertainty. Liquidity in a large issuer does not make the business loss distribution small.

At2018 GAAP EPSUS$17.85, the stock trades approximately21.2x historical earnings. At core EPSUS$16.01, the multiple is23.7x. These denominators reflect a record operating year before the interruption.

January's2019 GAAP EPS guidanceUS$21.90–22.10 implies approximately17.1–17.3x; its core rangeUS$19.90–20.10 implies18.9–19.0x. Quoting only the lower forward multiple silently assumes pre-event delivery and profitability forecasts remain valid.

Using586.2 million annual weighted-average diluted shares gives indicative equity valueUS$222.2 billion. Adding year-end net debtUS$5.2 billion yields approximatelyUS$227.4 billion enterprise value before other adjustments. An exact current share count and complete enterprise-liability bridge are not recovered.

Historical CFO less capexUS$13.6 billion is approximately6.1% of this indicative equity value. It cannot be treated as an assured continuing cash yield while aircraft are built but not delivered.

A scenario model needs the duration of grounding, production/inventory response, engineering/training costs, customer compensation, legal exposure and subsequent delivery recovery. A short interruption and a more complex remedy can have very different outcomes even if the long-term duopoly survives.

Asset and franchise quality can support residual value, and defense/services can be valued separately. But no reliable contemporaneous aggregate cost ceiling or final return-to-service timetable is available. Applying a normal multiple to unaffected earnings and calling the price decline a margin of safety would skip the main underwriting question.

The valuation attraction is the possibility of a temporary problem in a scarce franchise. The uncertainty is whether cash losses and confidence effects are temporary, bounded and acceptable at the price.
