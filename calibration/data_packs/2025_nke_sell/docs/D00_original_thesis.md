Title: The 2024 brand-repair thesis and its public March account
Source: https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/2025/03/14183709/Pershing-Square-Holdings-Ltd.-2024-Annual-Report-1.pdf
Date: 2025-03-14

The March 2025 report is a post-purchase account of the spring 2024 underwriting, not a contemporaneous May letter. It describes Nike as “one of the world’s most valuable and iconic brands with a dominant market position” and attributes a long growth record to share gains, pricing and innovation, supported by health/wellness and casualization.

The original repair case addressed three strategic errors: prioritizing direct sales over wholesale partners, reorganizing around gender rather than sport, and overproducing mature lifestyle franchises instead of commercializing new products. The report says prior management began reversing these choices in early 2024.

Its explicit rationale was: “We were confident that the new strategy would either lead to improving fundamentals if the team could execute, or leadership change if they failed, and we initiated an investment in the company in the spring of 2024.” Elliott Hill's later appointment was not already known at initial purchase.

The report also acknowledges learning in June 2024 that it had underestimated near-term revenue declines and operating deleverage required for repair. That admission is already public by this snapshot and belongs in an honest original-thesis record.

Hill's appointment was announced in September and became effective in October 2024. Pershing expected the veteran to reinvigorate culture, sport-focused innovation and brand marketing, and rebuild wholesale relationships. Inventory cleanup, marketing, sports partnerships and retailer incentives would depress near-term margins.

The medium-term expectation was “high-single-digit revenue growth and margins that are more than double current levels.” That forecast was a major source of upside and must be tested against current evidence rather than treated as an established normalized earnings level.

In early 2025 the position was converted to deep-in-the-money calls, retaining upside exposure while releasing capital. The report describes multi-year duration and a low break-even price. This is a financing/exposure structure, not proof that the brand repair was complete or that capital could not be lost.

Exact original NAV-at-cost weight is not authenticated here. This investment is separate from Pershing's earlier profitable Nike ownership. A current review should assess brand relevance, innovation, wholesale recovery, sustainable margins and turnaround duration, with no assumption that prior peaks are automatically recoverable.
