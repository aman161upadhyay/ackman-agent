Title: Structural separation, property pressure and the investor-day plan
Source: compiled from: https://bam.brookfield.com/sites/brookfield-bam/files/2023-02/2023-q1-bam-investor-presentation.pdf; https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/brookfield-defaults-on-161-million-debt-for-office-buildings; https://bn.brookfield.com/sites/brookfield-bn-v2/files/brookfield-bn/events-news/investor-presentations/2023-bn-investor-day.pdf; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001085/000117184324000675/exh_991.htm
Date: 2024-04-10

December 2022: Brookfield distributed a 25% interest in the asset-management business through listed BAM, retaining 75% at BN. The transaction creates a public valuation reference for part of BN while leaving investors with two different securities and economic exposures.

February 2023: the asset-manager presentation illustrates that 25%/75% structure and distinguishes listed BAM from the underlying manager. An investor cannot simply treat BN as owning 75% of BAM's quoted market capitalization without adjusting for the fact that the listed security represents only the distributed interest.

April 18, 2023: Bloomberg reported a $161.4 million office-property mortgage default by Brookfield funds. This is a concrete episode supporting real-estate concern. It is not evidence that the holding company defaulted on its own bonds.

September 2023: investor day set out $74 per-share plan value and a $163 target by 2028. Asset management, insurance and reinvestment were major growth levers. Public asset values and management-valued private assets require different confidence levels; the five-year objective is a management scenario.

During 2023: Brookfield raised $93 billion and monetized more than $30 billion in assets. It repurchased more than $600 million of shares. The latter represents management allocating capital to the corporate discount rather than relying only on new acquisitions.

February 8, 2024: annual results reported $4.806 billion DE, $2.66 per share before realizations and a quarterly dividend increase to $0.08. Management expected at least $1 billion of repurchases in 2024 and larger insurance earnings after the anticipated AEL transaction.

March 18, 2024: the annual report provides the audited historical context, including the five-year financial record. The raw series must be adjusted conceptually for the 2022 special distribution before comparing growth.

April 10, 2024: the market price remains well below management's last published plan value. That observed gap is a starting point for analysis, not a promised catalyst. No later corporate simplification, index event, investment disclosure or revised valuation is included in the decision information.
