Title: Growth in recurring earnings versus lower realizations
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001085/000117184324000675/exh_991.htm
Date: 2024-02-08

Brookfield's latest results are the February 8 release for Q4 and 2023. Total annual DE was $4.806 billion, down from $5.229 billion; before-realization DE per share increased 12% on the adjusted comparable ownership basis. Investors must decide which measure best captures sustainable earnings rather than choosing the larger growth number without reconciliation.

President Nick Goodman said the asset manager had “very strong fundraising momentum,” insurance had a “transformational year,” and operating businesses demonstrated resilience. He also said management expected to acquire “at least a further $1 billion” of BN shares during 2024.

Asset management raised $93 billion of capital in the year. Approximately $50 billion associated with anticipated American Equity Life closing would bring the total referenced capital to $143 billion. The pending transaction is not treated as completed at the release date. Fee-related earnings increased 6%, and fee-bearing capital reached $457 billion.

Insurance annual operating earnings were $740 million. Management put year-end annualized earnings at $940 million and expected more than $100 billion of assets and $1.3 billion annualized earnings following AEL. Those latter numbers are forward-looking run-rate expectations, not 2023 reported cash distributions.

Core real-estate same-store NOI grew 7%. Brookfield reported leasing over 15 million square feet across its office assets and core retail tenant sales per square foot 21% above 2019. These operating observations coexist with refinancing and valuation risk; a strong core portfolio does not establish that every property is healthy.

Realizations contributed $583 million to DE, including $570 million of net realized carry. Accumulated unrealized carried interest was $10.2 billion. Management reported more than $30 billion of asset monetizations, substantially all above IFRS carrying values. That supports selected asset marks but does not independently validate every remaining valuation.

The board increased the dividend 14%. Management reiterated a 15%+ long-term per-share return target. No company-wide quarterly revenue or EPS guidance range comparable to a consumer company was supplied. The relevant forward framework is recurring earnings growth, capital deployment and realization timing, all of which remain subject to market conditions.
