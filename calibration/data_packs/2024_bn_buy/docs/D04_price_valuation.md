The one-year intraday range is approximately $28.84–$43.17. The close is approximately 9.2% below that high. Trading volume was approximately 3.400 million contemporaneous shares, versus a window average approximately 2.472 million. The raw archive normalizes shares and volume; price and earnings here are put on the same contemporaneous basis.

At $39.18, price/2023 total DE of $3.03 is 12.9 times; price/DE before realizations of $2.66 is 14.7 times. Price/accounting EPS of $0.61 is 64.2 times. All three are trailing analyst calculations, and their wide divergence illustrates why the earnings definition matters.

Using approximately 1,588.2 million annual diluted shares and equivalents gives illustrative capitalization of $62.23 billion. This is not exact April common capitalization. A clean EV/EBITDA ratio is not supplied because consolidated debt, minority interests and affiliate operating earnings cannot be mixed casually.

The September 2023 investor day estimated $74 per share plan value, or $120 billion net asset value. Public holdings contributed $60 billion: $40 billion asset management, $9 billion renewables, $8 billion infrastructure and $3 billion business partners. Private holdings contributed $78 billion: $14 billion direct fund investments, $32 billion carried interest, $14 billion core property, $10 billion transitional/development property and $8 billion insurance. Debt and preferred capital deducted $18 billion.

The April close is approximately 47.1% below that $74 plan value, calculated. But the plan value is management's estimate using June 2023 inputs, not an independently executable liquidation value. Carry and insurance multiples are especially assumption-sensitive.

Investor day targeted $163 plan value by 2028, implying 17% annual compounding from $74. That is a plan, not a guaranteed stock return. Matching forward consensus DE, peer multiples, historical valuation range and S&P forward P/E were not recovered.

The critical margin of safety is therefore the conservatism of the asset assumptions and the durability of distributable earnings, not merely the headline percentage discount.
