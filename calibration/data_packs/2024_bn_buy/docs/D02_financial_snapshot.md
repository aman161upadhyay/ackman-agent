The annual financial record's 2022 $2.68 before-realizations figure is historical, before adjusting away the distributed 25% manager interest. The February results release provides a comparable adjusted 2022 figure of $2.38. Against that adjusted base, 2023's $2.66 represents 12% per-share growth. Both numbers are retained with their definitions.

Consolidated revenue was $95,924 million in 2023 versus $92,769 million in 2022, approximately 3.4% growth, calculated. Consolidated net income was $5,105 million, but only $1,130 million was attributable to Brookfield shareholders before preferred dividends. Treating the full consolidated income as BN common earnings would substantially overstate profitability.

Latest-quarter total DE was $1,312 million or $0.83 per share, compared with $1,498 million or $0.94. Before realizations, DE was $1,209 million or $0.76, versus an adjusted $0.65. The contrast separates underlying operating growth from lumpy monetizations.

At December 31, corporate borrowings were $12,160 million and nonrecourse borrowings $221,550 million. Consolidated cash was $11,222 million, but cannot all be netted against parent debt as freely available corporate cash. Management described $38 billion cash, financial assets and undrawn facilities at BN and affiliates within $122 billion total deployable capital.

Brookfield repurchased over $600 million of stock in 2023 and forecast at least $1 billion in 2024. The quarterly dividend increased to $0.08. A comparable parent-only FCF, maintenance-capex and ROIC series is unavailable; DE is management's non-IFRS measure, not a universally defined substitute for those quantities.
