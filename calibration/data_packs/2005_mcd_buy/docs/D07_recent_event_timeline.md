Title: Operating gains and Chipotle plans accompany property speculation
Source: compiled from: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/63908/000119312505145941/dex991.htm; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/63908/000006390805000058/exhibit99.htm; https://www.theledger.com/story/news/2005/09/17/briefing/26173805007/; https://www.foxnews.com/story/mcdonalds-limited-impact-from-gas-prices
Date: 2005-09-22

**March2005 — annual filing.** FY2004 results document renewed sales/profit growth and strong cash generation after earlier operational difficulties. The property ownership percentages and separate franchise/company margins are public.

**July21 — Q2 earnings.** Revenue rises8% and operating income5%. EPSUS$0.42 includesUS$0.09 repatriation-tax expense. The company repurchases approximatelyUS$600 million, about20 million shares, during the quarter.

**July29 — peer strategic plan.** Wendy's announces a Tim Hortons minority IPO plan, capital returns and operating initiatives. This provides a public example of unlocking a different brand's economics but is not a completed McDonald's transaction.

**September9 — August sales.** Global comparable sales rise3.4%, with positive US, Europe and Asia/Pacific/Middle East/Africa results. The US posts29 consecutive positive months. Hurricane effects are only beginning.

**September16–17 — public stake rumors.** The Ledger reports speculation that Pershing Square bought4.9%. Neither side confirms or denies. The article's hypotheticalUS$2 billion common-stock cost is an assumption, not authenticated cash invested or economic instrument disclosure.

**September21 — analyst meeting.** McDonald's announces an intended minority Chipotle IPO and raises the annual dividend22% toUS$0.67. Management reiterates growth and incremental-return objectives. It rejects a new structure for core property assets, and the stock falls according to Reuters.

**September22 — continued meeting.** Executives emphasize menu innovation, remodeling, convenience and value. They say gasoline-price effects have been limited. Shares rebound as investors assess operating prospects and capital-return plans.

The publicly available agenda includes organic improvement, a planned minority IPO and meaningful distributions. It does not include an accepted core-property restructuring, a completed corporate restaurant IPO or a future negotiated refranchising compromise.

By September30, investors have both operating evidence and a visible stakeholder objection to simplistic monetization. A catalyst analysis should distinguish an announced company action from an external investor's unconfirmed interest or an unaccepted structural idea. No November presentation details or later campaign resolution appear here.

The shareholder return depends on both operating durability and the price paid.
