Reported 2024 FCF of $38.219 billion divided by that illustrative equity value produces approximately a 2.0% trailing cash-flow yield. The low yield reflects heavy investment and should not be relabeled an owner-earnings yield after unverified maintenance-capex adjustments.

A verified April 7 consensus forward EPS estimate, forward P/E, comparable EV/EBITDA and a point-in-time twenty-year forward-multiple series are not available in the recovered materials. Consequently, “twenty-year-low multiple” is not established as a factual premise. Historical trailing multiples are also distorted by loss periods and major changes in business mix.

FactSet's April 4 report places the S&P 500 forward twelve-month P/E at 19.4, below its five-year average of 19.9 and above its ten-year average of 18.3. Comparing that with Amazon's trailing 31.7 times is not like-for-like. The investment case requires a separately justified growth or margin advantage, rather than treating a drawdown as proof of cheapness.
