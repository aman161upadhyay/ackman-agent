Praxair's calendarQ1 and APD's fiscalQ2 both end March31 2013, making this a date-aligned operating comparison. Adjusted definitions still require reconciliation.

| Operating margin used | 15.7% | 21.6% adjusted |

Praxair reported operating profit is US$600 million and EPS US$1.30. Its adjustments remove a US$23 million Venezuela currency-devaluation charge. Comparing its adjusted21.6% margin with APD's clean current-quarter measure gives an approximately590-basis-point observed gap, not a verified achievable target.

Praxair says comparable sales rose4% after currency, energy pass-through and working-day effects; reported growth is2%. It also faces weaker Europe and moderated North America. Its higher profitability therefore cannot be dismissed solely as benefiting from an entirely different global cycle.

Q1 Praxair CFO is US$472 million and capex US$466 million, primarily new plants under long-term contracts. Its NuCO2 acquisition adds approximatelyUS$1.098 billion acquisition spending. Even a stronger peer requires substantial investment; high margins do not automatically mean immediate distributable cash.

Praxair reports after-tax return on capital13.3% under its own methodology. APD's FY2012 adjusted return on capital employed is11.5%, not necessarily the same definition or period. A standardized asset, tax and lease bridge is needed before treating the difference as a precise ROIC target.

Other global competitors include Air Liquide and Linde, with different currencies, business mixes and geographic exposure. Clean matched quarter multiples and fully reconciled return metrics for all are not provided. No fabricated market-average valuation is used.

The comparison supports a focused diligence program: distribution density, local pricing, overhead, project selection, customer contracts and business mix. It does not establish that selling every nongas asset or replacing management will automatically produce Praxair economics. The capital intensity and implementation risks remain part of the security's valuation.
