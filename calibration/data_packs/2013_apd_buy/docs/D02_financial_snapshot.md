The apparent EPS choice matters: total net income includes discontinued operations. FY2012 adjusted continuing EPS was US$5.40 versus US$4.66 GAAP continuing. Do not compare US$5.44 total GAAP to the US$5.40 continuing adjusted figure and infer almost no adjustments.

FY2012 restructuring/cost-reduction charges were US$327.4 million, partly offset by an US$85.9 million gain on a previously held equity interest. Adjusted operating income was US$1.534 billion, 16.0% margin, versus reported13.3%. FY2011 adjusted margin was16.1%; the underlying gap is not just one year's restructuring expense.

Cash plant additions exclude acquisitions and other capital deployment. FY2012 issuer GAAP capital expenditures were US$2.560 billion; its broader non-GAAP measure was US$2.778 billion including capital-lease spending and other items. Simple CFO less plant additions is not cash left after all growth investment.

Share repurchases were US$649.2 million in2011 and US$53.1 million in2012. At March31 2013 cash was US$401.6 million. Debt comprised US$1,239.8 million short-term, US$96.7 million current long-term and US$4,644.7 million long-term, totaling US$5,981.2 million. Net debt is US$5,579.6 million, calculated.

Issuer adjusted return on capital employed was11.5% in2012 versus12.5% in2011. Clean maintenance/growth capex and a standardized ROIC bridge remain gaps; growing assets is not necessarily value creation.
