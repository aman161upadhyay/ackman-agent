Title: Investment spending and cost discipline precede the next earnings test
Source: compiled from: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2969/000119312512476878/d409668d10k.htm; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2969/000119312513166085/d526189dex991.htm; https://investors.airproducts.com/news-releases/news-release-details/air-products-extends-prismr-hydrogen-generator-product-line
Date: 2013-06-24

**Fiscal2012 — acquisition and restructuring year.** Air Products acquires Indura, expands its industrial-gas footprint and incurs significant restructuring charges. The annual financials distinguish GAAP operating margin13.3% from adjusted16.0%. Capital spending including acquisitions and capital leases is unusually high.

**November2012 — audited annual filing.** Investors can inspect segment mix, capital deployment and a long record of dividends. The business is profitable but its adjusted margin is essentially unchanged from2011 despite growth investment. No later disposed-business perimeter is used in this snapshot.

**Early2013 — portfolio and cost actions.** The company continues its announced exit from polyurethane intermediates and pursues productivity. This can reduce revenue while improving focus; a shrinking sales line is not always evidence of deteriorating customer demand.

**April23 — fiscalQ2 results.** Sales rise6% but underlying sales fall2%. Adjusted/continuing EPS isUS$1.37, with15.7% operating margin. Management describes difficult demand in several regions and electronics, while highlighting cost performance and a backlog aboveUS$3 billion.

**April23 — outlook and dividend.** Annual adjusted EPS guidance isUS$5.45–US$5.60 and Q3 US$1.33–US$1.38. The quarterly dividend has increased11% toUS$0.71, extending more than30 years of increases. Dividends are a policy choice supported by cash, not a substitute for attractive capital returns.

**April24 — peer report.** Praxair reports21.6% adjusted operating margin despite its own demand challenges. This provides contemporaneous evidence of a profitability difference worth investigating.

**June24 — product extension.** Air Products announces expanded PRISM hydrogen-generator capacity for on-site customers. It describes leveraging proprietary reformer/purification technology and a global service network. The release is evidence of technical capability, not an earnings forecast for the entire enterprise.

The next quarterly update is still pending at July15. Important tests include underlying volumes, new project start-ups, cash conversion and acquisition returns. No subsequent takeover defense, public shareholder stake, named replacement CEO or new margin target is an established event in these documents.

A possible engagement thesis should be built from the company's disclosed problems and implementable changes, not from knowledge of who will later run the business or which assets will later leave it.
