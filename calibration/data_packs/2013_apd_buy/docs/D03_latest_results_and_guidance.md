Title: Acquisition-led sales growth masks weak underlying demand
Source: compiled from: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2969/000119312513166085/d526189dex991.htm; https://www.fool.com/investing/general/2013/04/24/air-products--chemicals-increases-sales-but-misse.aspx
Date: 2013-04-24

FiscalQ2, ended March31, is the latest published quarter at this cutoff. Air Products reports US$2.484 billion sales, up6% year over year, and US$289 million attributable continuing income, US$1.37 diluted continuing EPS.

The underlying sales comparison is negative2%, affected by the decision to exit polyurethane intermediates. Acquisitions contribute6%. Reported revenue growth therefore does not establish a volume recovery in the existing business.

Operating income is approximately US$390 million and margin15.7%, down30 basis points. The company attributes pressure to acquisitions and pension expense, partly offset by productivity. Sequential underlying sales decline3%, partly because of planned customer maintenance.

Merchant Gases sales rise14% to US$1.003 billion, largely from Indura, with operating income US$168 million. Underlying sales fall1% as positive pricing is outweighed by lower European and helium volumes. Distribution and energy costs also affect performance.

Tonnage sales rise3% to US$809 million and operating income is US$123 million. New plants and energy pass-through support revenue, while lower polyurethane volume and customer outages restrain growth. Electronics/Materials sales fall3% to US$549 million and operating income falls9% to US$78 million amid weak electronics demand and price pressure.

Equipment/Energy sales increase12% to US$124 million, with US$21 million operating income, supported by LNG activity. This project-driven improvement cannot simply be applied to all company sales.

The company guides Q3 adjusted continuing EPS to US$1.33–US$1.38 and FY2013 to US$5.45–US$5.60. Expected capital spending is approximately US$1.9–US$2.1 billion under its broader non-GAAP definition.

April24 Motley Fool coverage, citing S&P Capital IQ, gives Q2 revenue consensus US$2.60 billion and EPS consensus US$1.37. Its next-quarter EPS estimate is US$1.48 and next-year estimate US$5.71. These are dated observations, not verified July consensus.

The summary initially describes lower non-GAAP EPS against a US$1.39 prior-year number that includes discontinued earnings; the original company release instead reports5% growth in continuing EPS. The pack preserves that distinction rather than reproducing an inconsistent headline comparison. The next results must test volumes, acquisition returns and cash productivity, not just EPS adjustments.
