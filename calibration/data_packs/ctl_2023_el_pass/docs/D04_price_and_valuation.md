The brand portfolio can justify a premium to an undifferentiated consumer-products business. The premium still needs conservative sustainable margins, cash conversion and returns on acquisitions. An old historical high is not an independent estimate of intrinsic value.

A scenario model should vary travel-retail recovery, channel inventory, organic growth, gross margin, marketing expense and tax geography. It should also include the acquisition's incremental cash earnings and financing costs without double-counting brand value.
