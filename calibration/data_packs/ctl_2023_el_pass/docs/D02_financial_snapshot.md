Reported quarterly sales fall12%, with organic sales down8%. The distinction reflects currency and other reporting effects. Adjusted EPS is still sharply lower after designated items, so removing acquisition/restructuring costs does not eliminate the operating deterioration.

The effective tax rate rises to44.6% from18.5%, influenced by the geography of earnings. EPS therefore falls for more than one reason. A normalization needs both operating margins and an appropriate tax assumption.

At March31 cash isUS$5.531 billion. Current debtUS$2.243 billion and long-term debtUS$5.128 billion totalUS$7.371 billion; the simple net amount isUS$1.840 billion. These are pre-closing figures, not a May5 net-debt estimate.

ApproximatelyUS$2.25 billion cash is paid at the Tom Ford closing. The release's transaction and financing details must be incorporated into a current enterprise-value bridge; using all March cash as surplus after closing would overstate financial flexibility.

Nine-month CFO less capex isUS$365 million, calculated. This period includes working-capital movements, so it is not a permanent annual cash figure. It nevertheless emphasizes the current gap between historical earnings expectations and cash conversion.

Full-year reported net sales are now expected to fall10–12%. Adjusted diluted EPS guidance isUS$3.29–3.39, compared with the priorUS$4.87–5.02 range reported inFebruary.

The board declaresUS$0.66 quarterly dividend, payable inJune. That commitment is an announced distribution, not evidence that future earnings have already recovered. Financial strength, acquisition obligations and future brand spending need to be considered together.
