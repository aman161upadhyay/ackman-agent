Revenue grows41% reported and32% excluding Sun Art consolidation. Those are different growth measures; neither should be confused with the growth rate of distributable cash to an ADS owner.

The March quarter includes anRMB18.228 billion antitrust fine. Quarterly reported operating loss isRMB7.663 billion; excluding that one-time amount, operating income would beRMB10.565 billion. The fine's accounting removal may help normalize one quarter, but does not remove uncertainty about future operating restrictions.

Quarterly net loss attributable to ordinary holders isRMB5.479 billion. Full-year diluted earnings per ADS areRMB54.70, approximatelyUS$8.35 at the release's translation rate. Adjusted full-year earnings per ADS are approximatelyUS$9.94. Ordinary-share EPS is one-eighth the ADS figure; using the wrong denominator creates a major valuation error.

Bank borrowings total approximatelyRMB41.941 billion and senior notesRMB107.212 billion, before lease and other obligations. Cash plus short-term investments exceeds those financial debts substantially. This is not an imminent-balance-sheet-insolvency case.

Management expects fiscal2022 revenue aboveRMB930 billion and says it plans to use incremental profit and additional capital to support merchants and invest in new businesses/strategic areas. Revenue guidance does not promise proportional near-term EPS growth.

The established commerce engine can finance considerable investment. The shareholder's task is to judge its future margin, capital allocation and ability to distribute value under applicable rules. The FCF definition, investment securities and legal access to operating cash need reconciliation before treating all reported cash as immediately available to foreign shareholders.
