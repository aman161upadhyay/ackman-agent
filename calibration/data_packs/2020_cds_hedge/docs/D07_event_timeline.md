Title: Public-information timeline through the first international repricing
Source: compiled from: https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/84735/cdc_84735_DS1.pdf; https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/coronaviruse/situation-reports/20200224-sitrep-35-covid-19.pdf; https://www.nytimes.com/2020/02/24/business/stock-market-coronavirus.html; https://www.cnbc.com/2020/02/24/global-stocks-roiled-as-italy-and-south-korea-sound-coronavirus-alarm.html
Date: 2020-02-24

**January 30:** CDC announces the first US person-to-person transmission in a close-contact household setting. Its telebriefing also emphasizes low general-public risk in that context. This is evidence of transmissibility, not evidence that a later US shutdown is already known.

**January 31 portfolio measurement:** The manager's public monthly update subsequently reports no CDS exposure at month end and strategy NAV of approximately $7.019 billion. It establishes a recent public baseline, not the exact book at inception of any subsequent trade.

**Early February:** Public attention focuses on the outbreak's Chinese economic impact and disruptions to activity. The retrieved February 24 press later describes investors as having previously looked past much of that threat. This is a dated contemporary characterization, not a hindsight claim that all markets were complacent.

**Week before February 24:** The S&P reaches a record on Wednesday, according to the February 24 New York Times account. The close proximity of a market high matters when assessing the range of possible drawdowns, but does not determine a fair credit spread.

**February 24 public-health update:** WHO reports 79,331 confirmed cases globally, 2,069 outside China and 29 affected countries outside China. Its global risk assessment is high. The report's measurement time is 10am CET, so it is not a final worldwide end-of-day tally.

**February 24 market session:** International clusters in Italy, South Korea and elsewhere drive broad risk repricing. The S&P falls 3.35%, the Dow 3.56% and Nasdaq 3.71%. Equity implied volatility rises substantially.

**Cutoff:** The pack permits end-of-day February 24 reporting. It does not claim these closing prices were available at an earlier intraday execution. Exact inception timing and CDS terms are unknown.

No March disclosure of the manager's hedge, television comments, policy rescue, monetization date or realized proceeds appears in the model-facing pack. Those are answer-key matters. The information set leaves a real decision: whether emerging international contagion and available risk prices justify a defined-cost protection overlay while preserving long-run equity ownership.
