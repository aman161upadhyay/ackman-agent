Title: Concentrated ownership and the need for an independently testable repair thesis
Source: compiled from: https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/2022/10/07155000/2016-Interim-Financial-Statement-Letter-Only.pdf; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058090/000105809016000078/cmg-20160630xex99_1.htm
Date: 2016-08-23

The first source is an archival copy of the 2016 interim manager letter, with performance through August 23, not a new 2022 operating account. Its historical date governs the information boundary. The letter describes a difficult performance period: PSH net return was negative 21.1% through June and negative 15.5% through August 23, following a negative 20.5% 2015.

The public discussion includes Valeant and the difficulties of reassessing its operating performance. This context matters for risk discipline: a large prior loss does not make another turnaround attractive, and a lower share price does not prove that a business problem is temporary.

Precise current manager-wide AUM, cash and name-by-name NAV weights were not recovered from a source whose publication before September 6 could be established unambiguously. An August month-end performance PDF is not used merely because its measurement date precedes the cutoff; publication timing must also qualify.

The manager's approach involves concentrated company ownership and, where appropriate, engagement with management and boards. Such engagement can help change incentives, oversight and capital allocation, but cannot directly force consumers to return. The business case must therefore stand independently of an activist's reputation or access.

For a restaurant repair, the distinction between a recoverable operating problem and a permanently damaged customer proposition is especially important. Chipotle's reported unit margins, traffic, safety costs and cash reserve provide observable checkpoints. A plan should specify what evidence would disconfirm recovery rather than assume influence guarantees it.

An investor considering a substantial stake also needs liquidity for further operational investment and the patience to tolerate volatile quarterly results. The company's first-half repurchases demonstrate that capital allocation can materially change the cash cushion while the operating outcome remains uncertain.

No pre-cutoff investor purchase, stake size or same-day direction statement is supplied here. That information would reveal the answer in a new-investment simulation. The same-day Schedule 13D is reserved for the hidden answer key, where it provides authentic language on brand, value and possible governance/operating engagement.

Accordingly, this context is useful but incomplete for precise sizing. The analyst should not fabricate current fund capacity or assume a specific governance outcome. The test is whether the durable franchise and repair path warrant concentrated capital at a verified executable price.
