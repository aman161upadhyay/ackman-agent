FY2015 net income was $475.6 million, up 6.8%. That full-year result obscures the late-year break: Q4 comparable sales fell 14.6%, and quarterly EPS fell to $2.17. Using the full-year EPS as an undamaged steady-state result would therefore require a normalization judgment.

In Q2 2016, operating income was $40.895 million, only 4.1% of revenue, versus $227.416 million and 19.0% a year earlier. Net income was $25.596 million versus $140.204 million. First-half operating loss was $5.709 million and net loss $0.836 million.

First-half operating cash flow was $179.665 million and capital purchases $126.712 million. The difference is $52.953 million, an explicitly calculated CFO-minus-capex proxy. Comparable 2015 first-half figures were $401.130 million and $114.395 million, or $286.735 million by the same calculation.

Repurchases used $700.036 million in the first half of 2016, far exceeding the simple FCF proxy. Liquidity was supplied in part by investment sales/maturities. Cash of $189.960 million, current investments $80.184 million and long-term investments $376.364 million total $646.508 million at June 30.

The balance sheet has no conventional debt, but restaurant leases and other operating obligations remain. No dividend is announced. A complete multi-year FCF, capex and ROIC table is not available from the retrieved extracts and is not invented.

A recovery investment requires a bridge from lost transactions to restaurant contribution, corporate costs, taxes, capital spending and diluted shares. Applying an old EPS multiple without that bridge risks confusing recoverable volume with permanently higher safety costs.
