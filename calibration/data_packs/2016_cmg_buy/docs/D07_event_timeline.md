Title: Food-safety crisis and operating response
Source: compiled from: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058090/000105809016000058/cmg-20151231x10k.htm; https://newsroom.chipotle.com/2016-02-02-chipotle-mexican-grill-inc-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2015-results-cdc-investigation-over-chipotle-welcomes-customers-back-to-restaurants; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058090/000105809016000078/cmg-20160630xex99_1.htm
Date: 2016-09-06

**2014:** Chipotle grew revenue 27.8%, comparable sales 16.8% and restaurant operating margin to 27.2%. These results provide the pre-crisis operating reference. They are not current guidance.

**2015, before the late-year crisis:** The company continued adding restaurants and generated high unit volumes. Average FY2015 restaurant sales of $2.424 million and full-year EPS $15.10 reflect substantial operations before the sharp fourth-quarter decline.

**Late 2015:** Food-borne illness incidents became a major public issue and traffic deteriorated. The subsequent annual report identifies food safety and customer acceptance as principal business risks. It would be inappropriate to treat the crisis as limited to a small accounting charge.

**Q4 2015:** Comparable restaurant sales fell 14.6%, revenue fell 6.8% to $997.5 million and EPS fell to $2.17. The change is visible before the 2016 financial collapse.

**February 2, 2016:** The company announced FY2015 results and stated the CDC investigation was over. Its message welcomes customers back and emphasizes safety changes. This establishes a dated operating response, not complete reputational repair.

**Q1 2016:** Comparable sales declined 29.7%, according to the July release's quarterly historical table. This provides the base for management's later claim of modest improvement.

**Q2 2016:** Comparable sales were down 23.6%, transactions down 19.3% and restaurant margin 15.5%. The company returned to quarterly profitability, but first-half net income remained a small loss. New-unit openings continued.

**July 2016:** Chiptopia launched as a frequency program. At the July 21 release, management reports early engagement and a 200-300bp improvement in July comparable-sales trends. The program is still new; its sustained, unsubsidized economics are not established.

**September 6 cutoff:** Ells and Moran remain co-CEOs in the public operating sources. No later board agreement, CEO search or external recruitment is included. The analyst must assess whether the existing concept can recover and whether operational/governance intervention is needed, without importing a future management team or future traffic statistics.
