Title: Restaurant model, pre-crisis economics and governance
Source: compiled from: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058090/000105809016000058/cmg-20151231x10k.htm; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058090/000105809016000078/cmg-20160630xex99_1.htm
Date: 2016-07-21

Chipotle operates fast-casual restaurants selling a focused menu of customizable burritos, bowls, tacos and salads. The proposition combines quickly served meals with fresh ingredients and the Food With Integrity sourcing identity. The operating system is company-run rather than a franchise royalty model: shareholders bear restaurant labor, food, occupancy and capital costs directly.

At June 30, the reported restaurant count was 2,124. The company also describes 27 Chipotle restaurants outside the US, 15 ShopHouse restaurants and an investment in an entity operating four Pizzeria Locale restaurants. The core economic exposure remains the Chipotle concept.

The pre-crisis evidence is strong but must be dated correctly. FY2014 restaurant-level operating margin was 27.2%; FY2015's 26.1% already contains the late-year crisis. Q2 2015 margin was 28.0%. Restaurant-level profit excludes corporate expenses, depreciation and pre-opening costs, so it is not the company's consolidated operating margin.

Average restaurant sales were $2.472 million in 2014 and $2.424 million in 2015; the June 2016 trailing average had fallen to $2.067 million. Mature-unit economics depend heavily on transactions because occupancy and much of restaurant labor do not decline in proportion to sales.

Steve Ells is founder, chairman and co-CEO. Monty Moran is also co-CEO. Their July statements emphasize food safety, empowered restaurant teams and restoring trust. No new CEO appointment or board-refresh agreement has been announced in the sources through this snapshot.

The July balance sheet shows no conventional interest-bearing debt. Cash plus short- and long-term investments totaled $646.508 million at June 30. That liquidity helps fund repair, but has declined sharply alongside repurchases and weak operations.

At June 30, issued shares of 35.826 million less 6.676 million treasury shares imply approximately 29.150 million outstanding. Q2 weighted-average diluted shares were 29.340 million. These are pre-split figures.

A historical ROIC series or independently verified 25%+ ROIC metric was not recovered; the pack does not substitute restaurant margin for ROIC. The key moat question is whether the focused concept, price/value and restaurant execution can retain relevance after the safety crisis, not whether favorable old margins automatically return.
