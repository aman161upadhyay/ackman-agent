The most recent independently recovered firm AUM figure is $18.4988 billion at September 30, including PSVII and bond proceeds. PSH AUM was $15.1353 billion. Neither is a January15 cash balance or the NAV denominator for an exact current position weight.

The public portfolio includes Alphabet, Universal Music, Hilton, Chipotle, Restaurant Brands, Canadian Pacific Kansas City, Howard Hughes, Brookfield and Nike, alongside Fannie/Freddie. Ordinary 13F tables omit OTC positions and foreign-listed holdings, so those tables cannot establish current GSE exposure or complete portfolio weights.

For a dated scale reference, the March2024 annual report's December2023 PSH NAV weights were Universal Music 24.88% directly plus affiliate exposure, Alphabet 14.06%, Chipotle 13.71%, Restaurant Brands approximately13.50%, Hilton12.15%, Howard Hughes11.72% and Canadian Pacific8.70%. These stale fund weights are not January2025 allocations. Current cash and exact GSE economic weight are unavailable.

The same annual report identifies Alphabet as the2023 addition and Lowe's as the exit. Its strategy emphasizes high-quality businesses, capable managers and long-term concentrated ownership. The GSE position differs because realization depends on government policy rather than ordinary corporate execution.

On December30 Ackman wrote: “We have owned Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac common stock for more than a decade. Today, they trade at or around our average cost. As such, they have not been great investments to date.”

He also wrote that there was a credible path to removal from conservatorship “in the next two years.” The statement reflects renewed conviction in a possible policy route, not evidence of a new purchase or a guaranteed deadline.

The manager's choice is therefore an existing-position reassessment after a significant rally. Opportunity cost and sizing matter even if the long-run thesis remains attractive. Exact incremental transactions are not publicly established by the sources here; no ADD execution is inferred from favorable rhetoric.

The public warning to limit exposure to what an investor can lose should remain part of the decision framework. High conviction in a conditional asymmetric payoff is not the same as low downside risk.
