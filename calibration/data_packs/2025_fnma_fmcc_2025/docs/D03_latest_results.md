Title: Latest operating results before the policy decision window
Source: compiled from: https://www.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/q32024release.pdf; https://www.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/q32024_financial_supplement.pdf; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1026214/000102621424000098/a20243qerexhibit991.htm
Date: 2024-10-31

Fannie reported Q3 results October 31, with $4.044 billion net income and $90.530 billion net worth. The release says it supported $274 billion of housing activity through the first nine months of 2024. These figures illustrate continuing operating scale while conservatorship remains in place.

CEO Priscilla Almodovar said: “Our net worth increased to $90.5 billion and, since the start of the year, we’ve reduced our minimum regulatory capital shortfall by $17 billion.” A declining shortfall is progress, not a statement that every regulatory buffer has been satisfied.

Compared with Q2, Fannie's net income declined $440 million. Fair-value gains fell to $52 million from $447 million, while net interest income was relatively stable. Credit-loss provisioning and fair values can make quarterly earnings variable even when the guarantee book remains large.

Freddie reported October 30. Q3 net income was $3.105 billion and net revenues $5.838 billion. Net worth rose to $56.390 billion, compared with approximately $44.7 billion a year earlier. The company said it helped 415,000 households during the quarter.

Freddie's income increase of $420 million year over year partly reflects lower noninterest expense because the prior period included a $313 million additional accrual. An analyst should not annualize the entire year-over-year increase as a permanent growth rate.

Freddie's senior preferred liquidation preference was $125.871 billion at September 30 and was scheduled to increase with retained net worth under the governing terms. Net worth accumulation is therefore not equivalent to erasing the senior claim. The same conceptual distinction applies to Fannie.

Neither earnings release supplies an approved plan for IPO, relisting or release from conservatorship. Neither company forecasts a distributable common dividend or confirms that Treasury will deem its senior preferred repaid. Those matters depend on contracts, regulation and government decisions beyond the operating teams' quarterly execution.

As of January 15, these are still the latest published earnings. No Q4 2024 results, January 16 investor deck or subsequent government action is imported into the financial baseline. A favorable policy scenario must be labeled as a scenario layered on top of the observed earnings.
