Title: June results expose a deeper transition
Source: compiled from: https://investors.nike.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000320187-24-000028/q4fy24exhibit991er.htm; https://www.nbcnews.com/business/consumer/nike-expects-sales-declines-attempts-comeback-rcna159412
Date: 2024-06-28

Nike reported fiscal Q4 and annual results on June 27 after the market close. Q4 revenue declined 2% reported to $12.6 billion and was flat in constant currency. Nike Direct revenue fell 8% to $5.1 billion; digital fell 10%, and owned stores fell 2%. Wholesale revenue increased 5% reported and 8% currency neutral to $7.1 billion.

Converse revenue declined 18% to $480 million. Nike Brand revenue was $12.1 billion, down 1% reported, with currency-neutral gains outside North America partly offsetting North American weakness. This is broader than a single delayed shipment or isolated foreign-exchange issue.

Gross margin rose 110 basis points to 44.7%, helped by strategic pricing, lower ocean freight and logistics costs, and lower warehousing expense. Lower Nike Direct margins and currency partly offset the benefit. Selling and administrative expenses declined 7% to $4.1 billion. Earnings therefore look healthier than the current sales trajectory.

Donahoe said: “We are taking our near-term challenges head-on, while making continued progress in the areas that matter most to NIKE's future – serving the athlete through performance innovation, moving at the pace of the consumer and growing the complete marketplace.”

Friend said: “While we are encouraged by our progress, our fourth quarter results highlighted challenges that have led us to update our Fiscal '25 outlook.” That statement accompanies a material change in the forward sales baseline, not just caution about a single quarter.

CNBC's June 28 report, carried by NBC News, says Nike now expects fiscal 2025 sales down mid-single digits, compared with analyst expectations of a 0.9% increase. First-half revenue is expected down high single digits, versus the earlier low-single-digit decline outlook. The report also describes expected Q1 revenue down approximately 10%.

The repair plan prioritizes performance innovation and a more balanced marketplace, but the revenue bridge is uncertain. Analysts must not capitalize fiscal 2024 EPS as if the new guidance had no profit consequences. Equally, the release does not establish permanent loss of Nike's brand or balance-sheet capacity. Both the severity and duration of the reset require underwriting.
