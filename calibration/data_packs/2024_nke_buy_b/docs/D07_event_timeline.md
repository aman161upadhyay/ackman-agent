Title: Successive warnings and the June reset
Source: compiled from: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/nike-beats-quarterly-revenue-estimates-discounts-draw-demand-2024-03-21; https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/nike-shares-skid-switch-newer-styles-squeeze-revenue-2024-03-22; https://investors.nike.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/24/nike-inc-reports-fiscal-2024-third-quarter-results; https://investors.nike.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000320187-24-000028/q4fy24exhibit991er.htm
Date: 2024-06-28

Fiscal 2023, reported in June 2023: revenue grew 10% to $51.217 billion, but gross margin was 43.5%, below fiscal 2022's 46.0%. This supplied a high sales base but weaker profitability as inventory and marketplace adjustments affected the business.

March 21, 2024: Nike's fiscal Q3 release reported $12.429 billion revenue, approximately flat, with Direct revenue approximately flat and digital down 3% reported. Wholesale revenue grew 3%. Inventory fell 13%, indicating progress on excess stock without a corresponding restoration of strong demand growth.

March 21–22, 2024: Reuters reported management expecting first-half fiscal 2025 revenue to decline by a low-single-digit percentage as Nike reduced older product franchises. Shares fell approximately 8% on March 22. This warning already established that a product transition could depress sales before the June report.

During fiscal 2024: Nike undertook organizational restructuring. The June release records $379 million of annual restructuring charges, mainly associated with streamlining the organization. Cost reductions can improve efficiency, but they are not a substitute for a successful product cycle.

June 27, 2024: full-year revenue was nearly flat at $51.362 billion. Q4 Direct revenue declined 8%, digital fell 10% and Converse declined 18%. Gross margin and EPS improved, illustrating the divergence between near-term profitability and weakening sales momentum.

June 27 earnings discussion, reported June 28: fiscal 2025 revenue outlook changed to a mid-single-digit decline; the first-half decline outlook deepened to high single digits. Management emphasized innovation and a balanced marketplace. No completed operational turnaround is established.

June 28, 2024: shares closed at $75.37 after an approximately 20% fall, on nearly 130 million shares. The market is repricing both earnings and confidence in the recovery timetable.

The timeline ends on that trading day. There is no announced CEO replacement, activist campaign by a new outside owner, or subsequent fiscal-quarter evidence in the pack. Any thesis requiring a specific future executive or a particular future product hit is conditional, not a fact available at this cutoff.
