Title: Pre-listing valuation anchors and missing equity terms
Source: compiled from: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210303005864/en/Vivendi-The-Good-Results-for-2020-Confirm-the-Resilience-of-the-Groups-Main-Businesses; https://www.vivendi.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/20210518_VIV_PR_Update-on-the-proposed-distribution-of-UMG-shares.pdf
Date: 2021-05-18

UMG has no public share-price, daily trading volume or 52-week range at June 3. A later Amsterdam opening quote cannot serve as an entry price in this pre-listing simulation.

The March Vivendi results identify the Tencent-led consortium's 20% investment at a €30 billion enterprise-value reference. This is a documented private transaction anchor, not proof that a new buyer can transact at the same terms.

At €30 billion EV, the ratio to 2020 UMG revenue of €7.432 billion is 4.04x. The ratio to 2020 EBITA of €1.329 billion is 22.6x. Those are explicitly EV/revenue and EV/EBITA calculations, not P/E or EV/EBITDA.

The May announcement says Vivendi is considering selling another 10% to an American investor or offering 5%-10% publicly before distributing 60%. It does not disclose a binding counterparty price in the information used here.

A 10% enterprise-value slice is not automatically the equity consideration for a 10% stake. Net debt, intercompany balances, transaction costs and separation arrangements affect equity value. Exact terms require more than a headline valuation.

The financial history shows improving EBITA margin, from 15.0% in 2018 to 17.9% in 2020. An underwriting model can test further operating leverage, but must allow for artist economics, standalone costs, marketing and content investment rather than assume pure royalty drop-through.

The proposed separation may make the music franchise easier for investors to value independently. A governance/listing catalyst can narrow a conglomerate discount without altering the underlying business, but it can also introduce shareholder-distribution selling, tax complexity and execution risk.

A verified pre-cutoff forward P/E, standalone FCF yield, net-debt table, consensus EPS series and comparable-company forward multiples were not recovered. No such figures are inferred from a later prospectus.

The valuation task is consequently enterprise-level and conditional: determine whether sustainable cash growth and the rights platform justify the negotiated price once actual equity terms are available. The existing transaction anchor provides discipline, not a guaranteed fair value or an instruction to accept any premium for scarcity.
