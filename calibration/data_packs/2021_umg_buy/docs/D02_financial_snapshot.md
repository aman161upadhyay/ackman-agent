Figures are millions of euros. These are UMG business disclosures from Vivendi releases available before June 3, not Vivendi consolidated totals and not September prospectus data.

| UMG metric | 2018 | 2019 | 2020 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 6,023 | 7,159 | 7,432 |
| EBITA | 902 | 1,124 | 1,329 |
| EBITA margin, calculated | 15.0% | 15.7% | 17.9% |

Actual revenue growth was 18.9% in 2019 and 3.8% in 2020. Constant-currency/perimeter growth was 14.0% and 4.7% respectively. EBITA grew 24.6% and 18.3% on an actual basis, with mix and cost control contributing.

EBITA is not EBITDA: amortization treatment differs, and neither equals net earnings or free cash flow. Applying an EBITDA multiple to this table without an explicit reconciliation would be incorrect.

The May 18 update supplies Q1 2021 standalone summary figures: revenue €1,809 million versus €1,769 million, EBIT €322 million versus €248 million, pretax earnings €164 million versus €144 million, and shareholder-attributable earnings €135 million versus €89 million.

Q1 EBIT margin calculated on reported revenue is 17.8%, up from 14.0%. Actual revenue growth is only 2.2%, versus 9.4% at constant currency/perimeter. Exchange rates materially affect the translation.

Net cash from operating activities before income tax was an outflow of €69 million in Q1. The release attributes improvement to operating performance and lower content spend. The measure is explicitly before tax and cannot be labeled ordinary after-tax FCF.

A complete pre-cutoff standalone CFO, capex, net debt, share count, diluted EPS and consolidated ROIC series was not independently recovered. The pack does not import the later listing prospectus to fill those fields.

The available evidence supports growing operating earning power and margin expansion. It leaves an important underwriting requirement: determine sustainable artist/content investment, working capital, taxes, standalone costs and separation debt before turning EBITA into equity cash yield or assigning an exact per-share intrinsic value.
