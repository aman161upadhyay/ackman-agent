Title: First-quarter growth, repertoire and cash timing
Source: compiled from: https://www.vivendi.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/20210422_VIV_PR_Vivendi-revenues-Q1-2021.pdf; https://www.vivendi.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/20210518_VIV_PR_Update-on-the-proposed-distribution-of-UMG-shares.pdf
Date: 2021-05-18

Vivendi's April 22 revenue release and May 18 UMG update are the latest retrieved operating disclosures before the cutoff. They report Q1 UMG revenue of €1,809 million, up 9.4% at constant currency/perimeter and 2.2% in actual euros.

Recorded-music revenue grew 10.8% at constant currency/perimeter. Subscription/streaming revenue increased 19.6%, despite comparison with a prior-year digital royalty claim. The underlying recurring growth is therefore stronger than the consolidated reported-euro headline.

Physical sales grew 14.8%, supported by new releases and catalog. Downloads declined 16.0%. These format differences matter: the company is participating in a transition rather than growing every legacy revenue stream.

Best sellers named in the release include new King & Prince and Justin Bieber releases and continued sales from The Weeknd, Ariana Grande and Pop Smoke. A diversified repertoire helps reduce dependence on one release but does not eliminate hit risk or contractual renewal risk.

Publishing revenue increased 6.9% at constant currency/perimeter, with subscription/streaming growth contributing. Publishing rights and recorded masters have different contracts and economics; their growth should not be mechanically merged into one royalty rate.

Merchandising/other revenue fell 10.0%, reflecting continued touring disruption, partly offset by retail and direct-to-consumer activity. The pandemic has therefore not been uniformly favorable to all music monetization.

EBIT reached €322 million versus €248 million, up 35.8% at constant currency/perimeter. Vivendi attributes the improvement to revenue growth and cost control. Q1 shareholder-attributable earnings were €135 million.

Cash flow before income tax was negative €69 million, despite positive operating profit. Content spending and working-capital timing matter. “Capital-light” does not mean reported profit immediately becomes distributable cash every quarter.

The May update also proposes governance for the prospective public company and discusses a possible additional stake sale. It does not provide a completed negotiated transaction, a guaranteed public opening price or a reliable standalone EPS forecast.

The investor can observe attractive streaming growth and operating leverage, while still needing a conservative conversion from current EBIT to normalized cash earnings. No later half-year figures, listing-day valuation or successful transaction are used to settle that question.
