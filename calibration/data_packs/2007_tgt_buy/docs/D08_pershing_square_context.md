Sole/shared rows are summed once. Values are the filing's long-equity table, not fund NAV. They omit cash, shorts, many derivatives and confidential positions. A March28 Seeking Alpha summary calls this a US$1.6-plus-billion hedge fund but appears to rely on the same filing total. That number is not independently verified firmwide AUM and is not used as such.

McDonald's supplies relevant experience in franchise economics, property and capital allocation. The bookseller holdings add familiarity with retail operating margins and site economics. These are precedents for the type of analysis, not proof that Target's land can be monetized without hurting the retailer.

On April2, NYT DealBook describes Ackman's pressure for Ceridian to spin off Comdata, while reporting third-party interest in the whole company. The distinction between a proposed separation and an actual buyer outcome illustrates activist dependence on management, boards and other stakeholders.

This public portfolio is already concentrated. Taking another large retail/asset-value position would require considering correlation with existing consumer businesses, liquidity and the resources demanded by concurrent campaigns. Ordinary common-stock purchases and a leveraged derivative exposure have fundamentally different tolerances for timing and drawdowns; no particular new vehicle is presumed to exist.

No authenticated pre-April20 Ackman Target thesis statement or target-specific purchase disclosure is available. This is an ex ante assessment of fit using prior public behavior and current issuer facts, not a reconstruction that silently borrows a future campaign's land structure or price target. Exact available cash, current NAV weights and overall gross exposure remain unknown.
