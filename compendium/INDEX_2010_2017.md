## Corrections and explicit limits

- **Justice began in 2011**, even though the Burger King target was selected in 2012; QSR remains held in the 2026 primary reports.
- **Platform exited in 2019**, not the task's proposed 2017 endpoint.
- **Howard Hughes executive chairmanship began May 2025**, and is followed through 2026; the $900m control transaction was funded by Holdco, not PSH alone.
- **Chipotle was fully exited during 2025**; 2026 reports verify the exit rather than ongoing ownership.
- **Allergan's ~$2.6bn** was a contemporary projected partnership gain; Pershing's allocation, stock consideration and later $193.75m settlement expense require separate accounting.
- **Preferred entry date is unresolved** across the 2017/2018 boundary. Original series, weights and later continuous ownership are unverified.
- “**Pershing Square 3.0 (2018)**” was not authenticated as an exact signed-letter title/date; the 2017 annual letter published March 2018 and the 2018 letter dated March 19, 2019 document the organizational/investment refocus.
- This is an evidence-grounded research pass, **not a complete point-in-time calibration pack**: several original entry decks/letters, exact execution dates, audited P&Ls and contemporary VIC/COBF/Reddit/Seeking Alpha discussions remain missing. Unknowns and later retrospective descriptions are marked in the individual files.
