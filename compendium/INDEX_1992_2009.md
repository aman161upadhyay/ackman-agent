# Ackman trade compendium — Gotham 1992–2003 / Pershing 2004–2009 entries

## Reading this index

This is an **evidence-weighted historical inventory**, not a claim to reconstruct every private Gotham trade. Each linked dossier contains the eight schema sections, dated source links, original words when recoverable, and explicit unknowns. Detailed cases and thin but verified holdings are deliberately distinguished. The source links for each row are in its linked dossier.

- **Dates:** entry means first purchase, not first 13D or 13F. Where only an observation or approximate year is known, the table says so and front matter remains `unknown`. A quarter-end exit is a **bound**, not an invented sale month. Years in uncertain filenames are research locators; particularly Rockefeller 1994, Hallwood 1995, Pre-Paid 2001 and Sears Roebuck 2004 are not verified initial-purchase dates. Austrian Post's filename uses its verified exit year.
- **Sizes:** “observed” is not lifetime peak. 13F dollar values are quarter-end **market values, not cost**. Percentages of the reported 13F book are not fund NAV. Swaps/options economic exposure differs from voting ownership; 13F option values are not premiums. Historical share counts are as filed unless stated otherwise.
- **Results:** `unknown` follows the schema's overriding “where unknown, write unknown” instruction, rather than inventing `flat` because the example enum omits an unknown outcome. `win` sometimes describes a well-supported successful catalyst with exact P&L unavailable; see dossier. Target's `big_loss` specifically flags the PS IV derivatives episode, not a measured 90% lifetime loss on every Target common share. First Union/Golf's `big_loss` flags the failed illiquid episode, not a quantified FUR common-stock loss.
- **Historical status:** known completed episodes can have an unknown exact exit date; where disposition itself is not verified, `status: unknown` follows the global unknown rule rather than assuming a sale. Later re-entries are separated rather than assumed continuously held. Do not sum predecessor/successor and derivative exposures as independent profits.
- **Calibration:** unknown driver slots mean **do not grade a guessed rationale as ground truth**. Ownership verification alone is not an original investment thesis.

## Cross-period and unresolved coverage

- **Corrections Corporation of America belongs to 2009**, despite the separate worker's historical `2010_corrections_corp` filename. The [original December 3, 2009 letter](https://pdfcoffee.com/pershing-square-third-quarter-investor-letter-pdf-free.html) explicitly says initiated Q3 2009, approximately 9.5%, and refers to *Prisons' Dilemma*. See [the shared dossier](trades/2010_corrections_corp.md); avoid a duplicate trade.
- **GGP hedges:** original [Q2 2009 letter, pages 6–7](https://www.scribd.com/document/20150688/Pershing-Square-Q2-2009-Investor-Letter) confirms an unnamed largest short as a GGP valuation hedge, plus another small REIT short. No unsupported issuer name is assigned to the unnamed hedge. Realty Income's later disclosed standalone short has its own dossier.
- **Broader CDS portfolio:** the same letter says notional exposure peaked at 2.8x capital January 2007 and fell to approximately 36% by end-August 2009. These are portfolio totals, not MBIA notional or capital-at-risk percentages. Individual unnamed credits remain unresolved.
- **1992–94 Gotham:** no complete audited security-by-security ledger recovered. Alexander's bankruptcy teaching example and references to investments considered are not promoted into proved Gotham holdings. No fabricated 1992 “first trade” fills this gap.
- **Original-source gaps:** Pre-Paid report text; Rockefeller entry quote; original Hallwood acquisition thesis; Farmer Mac contract-level payout; many passive holdings' original pitches; full final realized cash-flow ledgers. *Confidence Game* publisher sample and contemporary reviews were used, not falsely claimed full-book examination.
- **Social sources:** contemporaneous press, SEC opposition and one dated Seeking Alpha counterview were located. No unauthenticated Reddit/X/VIC/COBF material is claimed as contemporary evidence. Forum silence is not evidence of consensus.
