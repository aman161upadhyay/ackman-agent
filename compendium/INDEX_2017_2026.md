# Pershing Square / Bill Ackman: 2017–2026 trade index

**Research cutoff: 2026-10-25.** Forty-three new trade/decision files from this pass, plus the earlier-period worker's 2017 GSE preferred case. Three are explicitly incomplete macro-exposure records, not fabricated complete trade histories. This is a sourced research compendium, **not a claim of a complete execution ledger or a fully calibration-ready dataset**. Unknown executions and incomplete pre-entry packs are explicitly identified below. Each linked file follows the shared front-matter schema and eight-section order.

## Reading rules

- **Entry is first economic purchase, not 13F filing date.** Where only a quarter is known, the file states the window in prose and uses `unknown` rather than inventing a month.
- **Updates are not new original positions.** HHH's control purchase is a new financing/control checkpoint; CMG, QSR and GSE files update earlier positions and link their originals. SEG initially arrived by distribution; rights subscriptions are a separate new-capital decision.
- **Performance objects differ:** stock total return, realized fund return, calendar-year gross NAV contribution, and gross proceeds are not interchangeable. Mark-to-market winner does not mean realized final profit.
- **Vehicle matters:** PSCM/advised funds, PSH, PSTH, PSI/Holdco and PSUS differ. Q2 2026 parent 13F consolidation expanded. It is not PSH NAV, does not include all OTC GSEs/swaps/foreign securities, and includes direct sponsor holdings.
- Later manager explanations are labeled **retrospective**. A source's publication date does not make its hindsight content a valid pre-entry data pack. Files explicitly identify incomplete blind-replay snapshots rather than fill them with later results.
- `flat` is provisional for a withdrawn/non-executed IPO or cash-trust redemption, **not** a verified zero economic loss. `2020_cds_2`'s closed label is a historical-episode convention with unknown final execution, not proof of an exact exit. Domino's directional outcome is provisional; actual realized P&L is unknown.

### Evidence and calibration limitations

- Deep recent files include dated pre-entry operating data where retrieved (Nike, BN, Hertz, Uber, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Netflix II, Visa/MA/SPGI, ICE/Alcon). Daily price series, actual weighted fills and contemporaneous independent bear valuations remain incomplete in many. **No invented purchase ranges.**
- Many older files have a clearly labeled incomplete entry pack. They are research/thesis records, **not yet safe blind replay inputs**.
- Direct Firecrawl X verified: **Uber 2025-02-07, Hertz 2025-04-17, Microsoft 2026-05-15**. Companion pass authenticated multiple Fannie/Freddie posts and direct opposing Schiff replies. Alphabet follow-up indexed/press-corroborated but original scrape failed. Nike/BN/AMZN-specific original X thesis URLs remain gaps.
- Requested Amazon **H1 2025 original investor-call quote** was not recovered. February 2026 retrospective reasoning and August 2026 capex discussion are labeled later, not mislabeled as the original call.
- Exact first/final transaction days unavailable in many older holdings, Q2 2026 entries and Q3 ICE/Alcon. SEC quarter-end marks are never called acquisition cost.
- Contemporary Reddit/VIC/COBF/Seeking Alpha archive coverage remains sparse. Press plus directly linked X counterarguments are useful but not representative sentiment samples.
- Existing `archive/letters/raw_psh_...` files encountered in this pass contained **44-byte 429 error text**, not reports. They were not used as evidence. Full primary PDFs were retrieved separately where possible.
- Source conflicts retained: Nike I duration/return, Nike II footer, CP exit month, Alphabet retrospective entry date. This is deliberate evidence hygiene.
