## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
As of May 2011 disclosure, Family Dollar was a discount retailer and a potential acquisition candidate. The contemporaneous description was a **passive** stake; do not confuse Ackman's 2011 position with his separate later Dollar Tree/Family Dollar campaign. First quarter 2011 ownership was reported after confidential treatment ended. [1,3]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words
“It’s a good business, it’s done very well for a long time” and “It’s an attractive LBO transaction.” — May 25, 2011 Ira Sohn remarks, reported May 26. He also described it as “very reasonably priced.” Original video/deck not recovered; quotes are contemporaneous Bloomberg-derived reporting. [1]

## What Pershing Square did
Initially bought approximately 5.7 million shares for a reported $295 million in Q1 2011. Publicly disclosed at Sohn in May; June reporting put exposure at approximately 10.9 million shares/8.9%, including derivative-related exposure. Eliminated the position during Q2 2012. [2–4]

## What happened after
P&L unknown. `flat` is an **unverified classification placeholder**. The later eventual sale of Family Dollar is not the realized outcome of this already-exited investment. [4]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Good business:** direct contemporary statement. [1]
- **Valuation:** explicitly called reasonably priced. [1]
