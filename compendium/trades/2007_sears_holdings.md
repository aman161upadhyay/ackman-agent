## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

By October 2007 Sears was a controlled combined Sears/Kmart retailer. Reuters reported approximately 5M shares bought by Pershing and a 7.5% two-day stock rise on speculation about asset disposals. **Those analysts' restructuring arguments are not an original Ackman thesis.** Initial purchase date and cost remain unknown; the requested 2005 starting date was not independently established. [Reuters, 2007-10-08](https://www.reuters.com/article/world/ackman-buy-seen-good-for-sears-regardless-of-sales-idUSN04394475/).

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

No original entry pitch recovered. His Q3 2008 letter explains the policy exception: “When the price is right, the business is high quality, the management is excellent, and there are no changes to be made, we are willing to make a passive investment.” The surrounding discussion identifies Sears, Canadian Tire and Austrian Post as controlled companies; this general explanation is **not** a quantified Sears appraisal. [Letter, reproduced 2008-11-27](https://advisoranalyst.com/2008/11/27/bill-ackman-pershing-square-q3-2008-letter.html/).

## What Pershing Square did

The March 2008 13F reports 2,717,769 plus 4,028,799 shares, $688.757M total market value. Most were sold in Q3; September's remaining 501,000 shares were valued at $46.843M. Ackman said the remainder was approximately 1.5% of fund capital at the letter date and had fallen below the price at which they wished to keep selling. These measures use different dates and denominators. [13F, 2008-05-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336528/000117266108000313/pscm1q08.txt); [September 13F, 2008-11-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336528/000117266108000803/pers3q08.txt); [Q3 letter, reproduced 2008-11-27](https://advisoranalyst.com/2008/11/27/bill-ackman-pershing-square-q3-2008-letter.html/).

## What happened after

The Q1 2009 holdings digest records elimination. No exact last-sale date or full cash-flow record recovered. The Q3 letter discusses both redeployment and tax-loss realization for a group of exits; it does not assign a precise Sears total loss. [Market Folly, 2009-07-08](https://www.marketfolly.com/2009/07/bill-ackmans-pershing-square-has-large.html); [Q3 letter, reproduced 2008-11-27, above.]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

The important learning is explicit: control impeded influence and Ackman said he would generally avoid controlled companies going forward. This is separate from the earlier Sears Roebuck strategic-sale rationale. [Q3 letter, reproduced 2008-11-27, above.]

## Public discussion

Reuters records bullish analyst asset-disposal speculation even amid falling sales. It is useful contemporaneous context, not proof of eventual investment success. [Reuters, 2007-10-08, above.]
