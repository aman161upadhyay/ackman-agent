## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

This later holding is distinct from the [2005 activist Tim Hortons separation](2005_wendys.md), most of which was exited in November 2006. The March 2008 filing establishes 7,144,099 Wendy's shares, $164.742M market value. Exact new first-purchase date and re-entry financial underwriting unknown. A completed earlier spin-off is not a fresh 2008 catalyst. [Primary 13F, 2008-05-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336528/000117266108000313/pscm1q08.txt).

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

Unknown: no verified company-specific re-entry pitch recovered. Do not recycle the July 2005 letter as proof of a 2008 thesis.

## What Pershing Square did

By September 2008 the filing reports 21,578,936 plus 34,040,812 shares of **Wendy's-Arby's**, 55,619,748 combined, worth $292.560M. These successor shares cannot be compared one-for-one to pre-combination Wendy's units as if all growth were market purchases. By March 2009 the filing reports 32,324,580 successor shares, $162.593M. [13F, 2008-11-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336528/000117266108000803/pers3q08.txt); [13F, 2009-05-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336528/000117266109000911/0001172661-09-000911.txt).

## What happened after

Contemporary coverage says all sold by mid-May 2009, after the Q1 reporting date. Exact realized P&L unknown; no transfer of the earlier Wendy's/Tim Hortons return to this later trade. [Market Folly, 2009-07-08](https://www.marketfolly.com/2009/07/bill-ackmans-pershing-square-has-large.html).
