## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Conservative cutoff 2026-03-31; exact Q2 purchase day unknown.** February 10 results reported FY2025 revenue $15.336B (+8%), Q4 revenue $3.916B (+9%), 2026 organic constant-currency growth guidance 6%-8% and adjusted EPS guidance $19.40-$19.65. The company disclosed $5B buybacks and $1.2B dividends for 2025. [1] This provides the pre-entry guidance benchmark; do not import the eventual July Mobility spin completion into this March pack. AI substitution of data/desktop workflows was a risk to investigate; the August manager's profit decomposition is not a pre-entry public Ackman thesis. [2] Exact price path and fill range: unknown.

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

August letter: “over 80% of profits” comes from Ratings, Indices and Platts, rather than the desktop product most associated with AI fears. It says Capital IQ is “less than 7% of total revenue.” [2]

Detailed reasoning: ratings form an effective duopoly with Moody's and confer financing-cost savings; recurring surveillance complements issuance fees; refinancing, M&A, infrastructure and AI capex support issuance. Indices earns a royalty on market appreciation and passive flows, anchored by the S&P 500 trademark. Platts owns difficult-to-replace energy/commodity benchmarks. Proprietary/curated datasets and embedded regulated workflows are more durable than generic desktop access; LLM connectors can expand distribution, and enterprise/value-based contracts reduce seat-count vulnerability. Buybacks plus low/mid-teens EPS growth and a 19x entry valuation create prospective attractive returns. All are **August explanations**, with estimates explicitly attributed to the manager. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Earnings mix:** dominant benchmarks, not exposed desktop revenue, govern enterprise value. [2]
- **IP:** ratings/indices/commodity standards are hard to recreate with generated text. [2]
- **AI exposure:** quantify the threatened subsegment instead of extrapolating to all profits. [2]
- **Data opportunity:** reliable structured datasets may become more valuable in LLM workflows. [2]
- **Price/returns:** growth and capital return at a de-rated multiple support the expected outcome. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Classic toll/benchmark business; also a familiar-company re-entry when a new narrative creates price dislocation. It tests willingness to separate economically small disruption risk from the core moat. [2]

## Public discussion

Reuters identified SPGI among the six additions. [4] The August letter describes February's Claude Cowork/guidance-related selloff and rebuts blanket “AI replaces financial data” reasoning. The bear case remains lost pricing power or data redistribution economics, weak debt issuance and benchmark regulation; a moat does not remove cyclical risk. [2]
