## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff July 31, 2021 for funds' initial August acquisition; binding commitments preceded cash settlement.** June 4 PSTH public proposal already described a 10% UMG purchase and planned Vivendi separation. That business/transaction information was public before fund purchase. [1] It did not establish that PSTH shareholders ultimately would own the stake: the obligation moved after SEC concerns. [2] Full pre-commitment UMG earnings/consensus snapshot not independently assembled. September 21 listing price is not the original private entry price.

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The 2021 report explains the long-term owner advantage in winning the negotiated stake and a preference for businesses not requiring an exit. [2] Core drivers: largest music-label/catalog platform; streaming extends paid monetization globally, including emerging markets; valuable IP can be licensed repeatedly with little reproduction capital; subscriptions are inexpensive relative to consumer value; pricing/new formats/new platforms improve monetization; operating leverage and cash generation. Lucian Grainge's execution is explicit. [2,3]

By February 2026, the emphasis was Streaming 2.0 repricing, new tiers and AI licensing/productivity. These are **later holding reasons**, not all 2021 public catalysts. [3]

## What Pershing Square did

PSTH proposed acquisition June 2021; reassigned to funds in July. Initial 7.1% purchase in August, subsequently completed 10% across the investor group. September 21 Euronext trading began. [1,2,4] Average negotiated price **€18.27**, not the public IPO price. [3] March 13, 2025 sold $1.4B at €26.55. April 7, 2026 proposed a roughly $64B transaction via SPARC and US listing; **UMG board rejected May 29**. Sold remaining approximately 80.6M shares overnight June 3; June 4 issuer repurchase formed part of the block. [3,5,6]

## What happened after

February 2026 presentation gives interim PSH return 102% gross/72% net through February 9; these are **not** final June exit returns. [3] June sale reporting describes a profitable overall investment despite final block below original average share cost; earlier sales/distributions/dividends matter. Exact final audited fund-level P&L unknown. [6] H1 2026 gross PSH contribution -2.6 points and full exit confirmed by interim report. [7]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **IP royalty:** participation in music consumption without building distribution platforms. [2]
- **Streaming/pricing:** penetration plus improved label economics. [2,3]
- **Cash/margins:** capital-light catalog monetization and scale. [2]
- **Management/alignment:** Grainge and patient capital. [2]
- **Structure:** US listing/governance sought to improve realization, but board consent was required. [5]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Quality royalty purchased privately before listing; later moved into structural activism. UMG profit must not be credited to PSTH holders, whose deal failed. [1,2]

## Public discussion

2026 board rejection is primary counterevidence to treating the proposed takeover as a completed catalyst. Reuters records the sale and UMG repurchase. [5,6] Risks include platform bargaining, royalty accounting, catalog returns, AI copyright/value allocation and controlling-holder governance. [3,5]
