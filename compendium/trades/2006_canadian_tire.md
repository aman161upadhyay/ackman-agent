## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

May 2006 reporting describes a retailer, financial-services business, property portfolio and Mark's Work Wearhouse. Public Class A shares were largely nonvoting; Martha Billes and her son controlled 61% of voting shares. This control constraint was observable **before** the thesis failed to force change. Analysts' estimates of C$3.6B-plus for cards and C$3B-plus for property are analysts' estimates, not independently verified Ackman numbers. The article says buying began earlier that year, without a first-purchase month. [Globe, 2006-05-25](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/report-on-business/hedgie-puts-canadian-tire-in-his-sights/article18163783/).

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

“Great company, great management, cheap stock,” Ackman told the reporter. The report attributes the parts-worth-more-than-whole argument to his charity presentation; the C$129 estimate is attributed to observers, not a recovered original slide. [Globe, 2006-05-25](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/report-on-business/hedgie-puts-canadian-tire-in-his-sights/article18163783/).

## What Pershing Square did

Pershing held a reportedly approximately 5% stake by May 2006 and urged sales of the card portfolio and noncore property. In May 2007 the CEO said Pershing was supportive and increasingly accepted the existing model; Ackman declined comment. Do not turn the CEO's account into an Ackman quote. [Globe, 2006-05-25, above; 2007-05-11](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/report-on-business/pershing-canadian-tire-relations-good/article962372/).

## What happened after

Ackman's Q3 2008 letter confirms full sale in that quarter, alongside Austrian Post and Cadbury. September is the **quarter-end exit bound**, not an exact sale month. The letter discusses attractive replacements, tax losses and constraints from controlling holders collectively; no security-level P&L recovered. [Letter, reproduced 2008-11-27](https://advisoranalyst.com/2008/11/27/bill-ackman-pershing-square-q3-2008-letter.html/).

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Quality/price:** directly quoted May 2006.
- **Sum of parts:** contemporary report of his presentation, not a later REIT story.
- **Monetization:** advocacy corroborated by May 2007 coverage. [Globe, 2006-05-25 and 2007-05-11, above.]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Asset separation fits the early retail playbook; a nonvoting minority could not compel the controlling family. Ackman's 2008 stated preference to avoid controlled companies is a documented process lesson. [Q3 letter, reproduced 2008-11-27, above.]

## Public discussion

Contemporary skeptics said management valued the interconnected businesses and had no interest in breakup. Analyst Perry Caicco said much hidden value was already priced. This is a real counterargument to the catalyst, not proof the company lacked value. [Globe, 2006-05-25, above.]
