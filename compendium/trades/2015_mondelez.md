## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
At March 30, 2015 entry, Mondelez was the global snacks business resulting from Kraft's 2012 split. Exact entry-date reported revenue, valuation and earnings multiple were not recovered; the **$27 billion 2015 revenue** and 13% margin discussed in the later annual letter are not automatically entry-date known numbers. Do not import the later CEO transition into the initial case. [1]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words
“Branded snacks and candy businesses are wonderful businesses because of their high category margins, large economic moats, high returns on capital, and attractive long-term global growth potential.” — March 24, 2016 report. [1]

The letter argues that Nabisco, LU Biscuit and Cadbury had not been fully integrated/optimized, leaving Mondelez with the lowest margins among packaged-food peers. This is an operational-efficiency case; a sale to Kraft Heinz should not be asserted as the sole original thesis without a separate source. [1]

## What Pershing Square did
Started buying March 30, 2015; at year-end held approximately 105 million shares/equivalents, approximately 6.6%. Continued to support the margin opportunity through 2017 and Dirk Van de Put's CEO transition. Exited during Q3 2018; Reuters reported redeployment toward other opportunities including Starbucks and Hilton. Exact final exit day/month unknown. [1–3]

## What happened after
The 2015 letter cited a company 17–18% margin target for 2018, not an achieved entry result. The 2017 letter still saw an opportunity at less than 18 times consensus 2018 EPS, despite U.S. grocery concerns and CEO-transition uncertainty. Its **−3.5-point 2017 PSH performance contribution** is not a lifetime trade return. Cumulative realized result unknown; `flat` is **unverified**, not break-even. [1–3]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Category quality:** the quoted category economics are the franchise case. [1]
- **Margin gap:** under-earning compared with peers supplied upside beyond revenue growth. [1]
- **Integration:** manufacturing/procurement and overhead optimization were specific levers. [1]
- **Geography/snacking:** 2017 commentary distinguished international snacks from pressured U.S. center-of-plate food companies. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: quality franchise plus fixable peer gap; the exit illustrates opportunity cost rather than needing every thesis target to be reached. No verified Mondelez-specific process rule was recovered. [1,3]

## Public discussion
Reuters' November 2018 exit story documented the move to other opportunities. Pershing's 2017 letter explicitly addresses bearish grocery/CEO narratives, but those passages are the manager's rebuttal rather than independent forum consensus. Original contemporary social/forum threads remain gaps. [2–3]
