## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**2019 initiation; exact date unknown.** No complete pre-entry price/earnings snapshot retrieved. The recurring-consumables thesis below is a post-entry manager explanation and cannot silently become an entry-date public quote. [1]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The 2019 report underwrites leading analytical instruments, an installed base generating recurring service/consumables revenues, laboratory workflows that resist switching and Mike McMullen's execution. Post-Keysight focus and operating improvements supported margin/EPS growth. [1] Direct contemporaneous Ackman quotation and X post unknown.

## What Pershing Square did

Initiated in 2019, publicly visible by the November reporting cycle. Held through COVID recovery. Sold **after June 30 and before the August 24, 2021 interim report**, in part to fund the UMG commitment. Exact final day unknown. It did **not** remain until 2022. [2,3]

## What happened after

2021 report lists Agilent as a +3.8 percentage-point PSH gross NAV contributor and confirms exit. This is a year contribution, not the lifetime percentage return. Dollar profit/peak position not reliably established. [3]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Installed base:** consumables/services improve recurring quality. [1]
- **Switching costs:** regulated laboratory processes and equipment familiarity. [1]
- **Management:** operational improvement and capital allocation. [1]
- **Funding choice:** successful position sold to fund a more attractive opportunity. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Fits simple recurring economic value despite technical products; no need to forecast individual scientific discoveries. Sale for capital allocation is distinct from thesis failure. [1,2]

## Public discussion

Reuters explicitly links Agilent liquidation to UMG financing and separately discusses the still-searching PSTH. Avoid merging the private funds' UMG acquisition with a consummated PSTH deal. [2]
