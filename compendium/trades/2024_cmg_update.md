## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Update only, not a new 2024 initiation.** Cross-reference [2016 Chipotle initiation](2016_chipotle.md). Separate dated blind replay packs are needed for each trim and the November 2025 exit; exact first trim decision day and executions have not been recovered. Accordingly this file's point-in-time financial snapshot is **unknown**, not populated with the later exit narrative. [1,2]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The February 2026 presentation says “the wide range of potential future outcomes led us to sell our remaining shares.” [1] Retrospective reasons: Brian Niccol's departure and the ensuing management transition; slowing/negative same-store sales; under-adjustment of required returns/assumptions after leadership changed; high initial-2025 valuation. Those are reasons for ending the position, not an abandonment of belief that Chipotle is a good business. [1]

## What Pershing Square did

Reduced over years, with 83% of initial shares sold during 2017-24 according to the presentation. Q4 2024 common-share count fell 14% to 24.7M, reported February 14, 2025. [1,2] Final exit **November 12, 2025**, not an open 2026 position. [1] Precise 2024 trim-by-trim transactions and prices remain gaps.

## What happened after

2024 gross PSH NAV contribution +4.4 percentage points; 2025 -4.6 points. Lifetime investment is a substantial win, despite the final year's decline. [3,4] Presentation charts explicitly warn that underlying share-price returns differ from fund realized returns; do not multiply the final share count by the original chart cost to claim lifetime P&L. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Value realized:** earlier turnaround created large gains and permitted reductions. [1]
- **Leadership:** Niccol's departure altered underwriting quality. [1]
- **Sales:** slowing comps reduce confidence in margin/earnings projections. [1]
- **Valuation:** a good business can fail the required-return test at a demanding multiple. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Important distinction between a successful original activist case and a later sale required by changed evidence. Avoid classifying the entire holding as a loss because the exit-year mark fell. [1,4]

## Public discussion

Reuters documented trimming while adding Nike; November coverage described the exit and business deterioration. [2,5] No original Ackman X exit post verified. The presentation PDF's extracted numerals around starting P/E are corrupted; exact multiple not asserted without reliable verification. [1]
