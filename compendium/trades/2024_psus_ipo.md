## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**No completed purchase; cutoff for withdrawal decision: 2024-07-30.** A proposed US listed closed-end vehicle was attempting to raise capital; July reporting described lowered demand/size. These are subscription/fund-structure decisions, not buying an operating business. Full dated proposed fee schedule and SEC prospectus risk-factor extraction remain gaps. “No investment completed” is not a missing stock return. [1,2]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

On withdrawal, Ackman said the issue was why invest at IPO rather than buy later, motivating reconsideration of PSUS's structure. CNBC reproduces: “This question has inspired us to reevaluate PSUS's structure to make the IPO investment decision a straightforward one.” [1] Do not infer he claimed that failed demand itself was a trading profit. His later reports explain permanent capital's importance and scalable brand distribution. [3]

## What Pershing Square did

Proposed a US closed-end fund, cut size expectations and **withdrew July 31, 2024**. Reuters reports earlier target $25B and revised approximately $2B plan; those are indicated fundraises, not actual AUM received. [1,2] Separate June 2024 sale of 10% of the **management-company Holdco** for $1.05B did close; it was not the withdrawn PSUS IPO. [3]

## What happened after

2026 combined offering of PSUS and manager PSI subsequently completed; see `2026_pershing_square_ipo.md`. The 2024 event's `flat` classification is only a schema accommodation for no consummated public trade; economic launch costs are unknown, not proven zero. [2,4]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Permanent capital:** a listed closed-end fund eliminates redemption pressure. [3]
- **Scale/access:** distribute the existing strategy to a broader US investor base. [1,3]
- **Fees:** proposed no-incentive-fee structure differentiated the offering. [1]
- **Discipline/structure:** cancellation acknowledged the disadvantage of subscribing before a possible aftermarket discount. [1,2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Capital-raising design rather than security selection; informative about willingness to withdraw and redesign a high-profile plan. [1,2]

## Public discussion

Reuters/CNBC emphasized demand shortfall and large reductions in ambition; Ackman emphasized a better structure. Preserve both accounts rather than treating either as the sole proven cause. [1,2]
