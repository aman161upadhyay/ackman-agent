## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff 2020-11-30; first purchase December, day unknown.** A dated inflation/options-surface pack has not been assembled. March 2021 mark-to-market gains and 2022 realized inflation cannot be inserted as pre-entry inputs. This position began in **2020**, not merely 2021. [1,2]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

March 2021 report describes large-notional swaptions with a potential payoff many times capital at risk. The manager worried monetary/fiscal support and recovery would produce higher inflation/rates than markets priced, impairing long-duration equity valuations. [1,2] It is an options payoff analysis, not simply selling an equivalent notional of Treasury bonds.

## What Pershing Square did

Bought December 2020-early February 2021; PSH cost $157M / 1.4% assets, marked $493M / 4.2% at the letter's reporting date. [1] Sold expiring portions in January 2022 for $1.25B across the funds, and remaining 2020/21 options before March 29 for $195M, approximately **$1.45B proceeds on $188M all-fund premium**. [2] These amounts must not be mixed with PSH-only initial cost. Bought new longer-dated initially OTM options during 2022; those are replacement exposures, not additional proceeds from the original episode. [2]

## What happened after

2021 PSH gross NAV contribution +7.7 points, early-2022 through March 22 +7.3 points. [2] Later hedges had mixed results: 2023 report separately records positive US swaptions and losses on Japan swaptions and energy protection. [3] The 2023 long-Treasury short belongs in `2023_treasury_short.md`, not a new independent profit added to this original $1.45B.

2024 letter states no **significant new** hedges were purchased because volatility made insurance expensive; existing hedges still detracted approximately 1.9 points. Do not rewrite this as “there were no hedges.” [4]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Variant inflation:** views must differ from the price, not merely predict positive inflation. [1,2]
- **Portfolio duration:** protect valuation sensitivity while retaining quality equities. [2]
- **Convexity:** premium sized small relative to potential payoff. [1]
- **Rolling:** new strikes/expiries change exposure and require separate accounting. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Demonstrates episodic macro asymmetry, not continuous insurance purchase. The 2024 rule is both a variant view **and** a sufficiently asymmetric instrument; otherwise wait. [4]

## Public discussion

Manager letters distinguish paid premium, notional and proceeds. Public descriptions often conflate these and combine multiple vintages. The later losing overlays are essential counterevidence against “all Ackman hedges win.” [1-4]
