## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

The June 2009 filing establishes a sizable stock investment by quarter-end; an August 17 digest labels it new that quarter. Exact first purchase month is unknown. This is **not the 2017 ADP activist campaign**. The detailed original rationale is available in the later Q2 letter, public by September, and is not mislabeled an entry-day memo. [Primary 13F, 2009-08-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336528/000117266109001387/0001172661-09-001387.txt); [digest, 2009-08-17](https://www.marketfolly.com/2009/08/pershing-square-new-positions-in.html).

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

“ADP is a high-quality business that trades at a meaningful discount to its intrinsic value due to the near-term effects of the economic downturn.” He separates payroll processing, client-fund float and dealer services, concluding: “As the U.S. economy stabilizes, we believe the headwinds facing ADP will become tailwinds.” [Original Q2 2009 letter, pages 4–5, public by 2009-09-24](https://www.scribd.com/document/20150688/Pershing-Square-Q2-2009-Investor-Letter).

## What Pershing Square did

June holdings total 7,258,302 shares, $257.234M market value, approximately 11.34% of the **13F reported book**, not fund capital. Contemporary quarter digests record trimming 15.7% in Q3 and approximately 98% in Q4. No board nominations or management-replacement proposal was verified for this episode. [Primary June filing, above; Market Folly, 2009-11-17](https://www.marketfolly.com/2009/11/bill-ackmans-pershing-square-boosts.html); [2010-02-17](https://www.marketfolly.com/2010/02/bill-ackmans-pershing-square-dumps.html).

## What happened after

A small residual was still reported at December 2009. Total realized return, final sale date and a quantified thesis bridge remain unknown; a 98% trim is not a zero holding. [Q4 digest, 2010-02-17, above.]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Temporary headwinds:** unemployment, low interest rates/wage deflation and automotive distress.
- **Normalization:** operating leverage as the economy stabilized.
- **Cash generation:** low capital outlays meant FCF above earnings.
- **Quality:** dominant franchise, pricing power and AAA balance sheet in Ackman's description.
- **Growth/return:** global payroll, beyond-payroll cross-sell, approximately $5B buybacks and $2B dividends over four years, as stated in the letter. [Original Q2 2009 letter, pages 4–5, above.]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

The original letter praises shareholder-friendly management. It would be a hindsight error to transfer his 2017 criticism of ADP management into the 2009 answer key. [Original Q2 2009 letter, above.]

## Public discussion

Contemporary trackers noted both ADP and McDonald's as new blue-chip holdings and later aggressive trims; these observations are not independent company valuations. [Market Folly, 2009-08-17 and 2010-02-17, above.]
