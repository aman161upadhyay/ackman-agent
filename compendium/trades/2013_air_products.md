## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
By July 31, 2013, Pershing had disclosed 9.8% of an approximately $22 billion industrial-gas company, a position valued around $2.2 billion. Peers included Praxair, Linde and Air Liquide. The public filing left open a proxy solicitation; Seifi Ghasemi's eventual appointment was **not yet an accomplished fact**. Exact initial trailing financials and share basis are unknown. [1,5]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words
“Seifi is the ideal leader to transform Air Products” — March 24, 2015 report, after his July 1, 2014 appointment. Pershing described a target to move EBIT margins from approximately 16% to 22.5%, comparable to Praxair, with half the 650-basis-point improvement from overhead and half from productivity. This is a dated 2014–2015 campaign update, not an authenticated original 2013 pitch. [2]

“APD’s business is extremely high-quality, reasonably priced and run by outstanding management.” — March 28, 2017 annual letter. [3]

## What Pershing Square did
July 2013 disclosed the large position and willingness to seek change. Ghasemi became chairman/CEO July 1, 2014; Pershing supported his turnaround. During 2016, the company sold/spun non-core materials operations, including Versum. The 2017 report confirms Pershing exited during 2017; exact final month unknown. [1–4]

## What happened after
Pershing reported fiscal-2016 operating margins of 23.1%, up 400 basis points that year, and 14% EPS growth, explicitly noting this fiscal year included businesses subsequently divested/spun. These figures should not be compared unadjusted to later continuing-only margins. The company's October 27, 2016 release independently reports 23.1% adjusted operating margin. [3,6]

The 2017 letter says all listed exited positions other than Valeant generated gains, including Air Products. The 24% APD shareholder return **in 2016**, including Versum/dividends, is neither cumulative position return nor PSH contribution. [3–4]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Franchise quality:** industrial gases were viewed as a high-quality continuing core. [3]
- **Peer margins:** explicit target benchmarked Praxair. [2]
- **Leadership:** Ghasemi was the specific operator backed to deliver change. [2]
- **Accountability:** decentralized structure and regional pay alignment were reported operating mechanisms. [3]
- **Focus:** non-core disposal/spin released capital and clarified the core. [3]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: CP-like franchise/peer-gap/operator case; the report explicitly says the thesis did **not** require an improving economy. Do not recast it as solely a macro industrial-recovery bet. [3]

## Public discussion
NYT and CNBC-derived reporting covered the record-sized position on July 31, 2013. The company's earnings releases offer primary operating evidence but are management's adjusted measures. No authenticated contemporaneous forum/Twitter discussion or original Pershing entry deck was recovered. [1,5–6]
