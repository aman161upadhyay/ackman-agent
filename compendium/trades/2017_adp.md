## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff 2017-05-09.** ADP was an established payroll/HCM provider, but the investor's detailed margin diagnosis was not public until the subsequent campaign. A dated pre-May financial/consensus snapshot has not been retrieved. **Unknown; not calibration-ready.** September proxy materials establish the May 10 first purchase, not that their later arguments were publicly known then. [1]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The March 2018 letter summarizes campaign goals: “better its Enterprise market offering,” “accelerate growth” and “improve margins.” [2] He argued Enterprise share losses/product investment and inefficiency explained part of a structural gap; Employer Services margins could rise from roughly 19% to 35%+, driving approximately $10 FY2022 EPS in the campaign scenario. This was a forecast, not a realized result. [2] Payroll's sticky recurring relationships made improvement attractive without requiring a risky new product category.

## What Pershing Square did

First bought May 10; disclosed August 4 roughly 8% economic stake; public proxy materials September 5. Asked for board/leadership change, ultimately ran three candidates. Lost the **November 7, 2017** contest. [1,3] Continued ownership and dialogue after losing rather than immediately liquidating. Fully exited **July 2019**, announced August 5—not 2020. [4]

## What happened after

Reporting estimates approximately $1.2B profit and distinguishes proxy defeat from investment outcome. Exact fund-by-fund return/cost unknown. [5] The 2018 letter credits the campaign with increased accountability and commitments; that is the activist's causal interpretation, not an independently proven counterfactual. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Business quality:** recurring, mission-critical payroll/HCM supports durability. [1,2]
- **Product/growth:** repair Enterprise competitiveness and bookings. [2]
- **Margins:** compare achievable operating model rather than accept historical costs. [2]
- **Governance:** board pressure sought faster execution. [1,2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Classic quality-business/operating-gap activism; public confrontation and inability to secure seats limit control over the catalyst. A proxy result alone is not the outcome label. [2,5]

## Public discussion

ADP defended its existing strategy and criticized the proposal; Reuters documented the bitter contest and defeat. [3] The legitimate bear countercase is that peer margins reflect different product/client mixes and that modernization costs cannot simply be eliminated. Primary proxy claims require evaluation, not automatic acceptance. [1,3]
