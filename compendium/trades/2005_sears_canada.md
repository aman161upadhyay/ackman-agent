## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**Entry in early 2005, exact date unknown.** The June 2006 application places Pershing's initial investment in early 2005. It is a later litigation document, not an entry-time financial pack. First-purchase valuation, expected catalysts and pre-entry stock performance remain unknown. The controller's December 2005 offer cannot be assumed to have been foreseen when the shares were bought. [1]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words
No verified initial investment quote. The 2006 shareholder application supplies Pershing's **later minority-rights argument** jointly with Hawkeye and Knott, not an individually attributed verbatim Ackman statement. Preserve that distinction. [1]

## What Pershing Square did
Pershing resisted Sears Holdings' privatization effort and participated in the June 5, 2006 regulatory application. The application dates initial public announcement of the C$16.86 offer to December 5, 2005. Sears Holdings contested Pershing's position, differentiating directly owned shares and derivatives/economic exposure and criticizing purchases near C$18. [1,3]

On April 23, 2010, Pershing announced sale of its entire 18,660,880 shares, 17.57%, to Sears Holdings through the TSX at C$30, scheduled April 27. [2]

## What happened after
The announced 2010 sale price was materially above the original controller offer. Gross proceeds are approximately C$559.8M (18,660,880 × C$30), **not profit**. Unknown costs, distributions and changes in stake prevent a defensible aggregate IRR. Do not mix Sears Canada's later fate with Pershing's closed 2010 holding. [1,2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Price:** application and opposition concern buyout adequacy, not merely opposition to Lampert personally. [1]
- **Minority rights:** treatment of blockholders and voting conditions were central to the complaint. [1]
- **Holdout:** the continued stake followed by a negotiated higher-price exit is documented; initial underwriting remains missing. [1,2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Analytical comparison: minority leverage in a controlled company differs from replacing a dispersed-company board. Ackman's Q3 2008 letter says controlling shareholders at Sears Holdings, Canadian Tire and Austrian Post constrained activism; do not automatically apply that discussion to the different Sears Canada security. [3,4]

## Public discussion
Sears Holdings' contemporaneous release gives a forceful contrary view of Pershing's accumulation/economic-interest claims. WSJ's April 12, 2006 report frames the hedge-fund confrontation. No authenticated era forum recovered. [3,5]
