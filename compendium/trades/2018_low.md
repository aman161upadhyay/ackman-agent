## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**First 2018 purchase date unknown.** A dated pre-entry financial/valuation pack remains incomplete; do not backdate the May CEO appointment or later successful margin repair into an earlier purchase window. CNBC's May 23 disclosure is public evidence of the stake, not proof of the first execution date. [1]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The 2018 report calls Lowe's a high-quality business under a new high-caliber team committed to narrowing its gap with Home Depot. [2] Specific drivers: merchandising and inventory/supply-chain fixes, service to professionals, expense/productivity improvements, and strong cash generation. Marvin Ellison's experience made operational repair credible. Ackman supported the new team; he did **not** personally install Ellison through a proxy victory. [1,2]

## What Pershing Square did

Disclosed approximately $1B position May 23, 2018. Held through the pandemic and subsequent improvement. March 2022 report describes about 15% of equity portfolio. [1,3] Fully exited **during 2023**, exact final day unknown; public confirmation February 7, 2024. It was **not** held through 2026. [4,5]

## What happened after

Reuters reports $1.3B net profit across Pershing funds. The 2023 report explains that prospective sales/earnings under macro uncertainty were less predictable than desired, without rejecting Ellison's quality. Exact entry-to-exit gross/net percentages not independently recovered. [4,5]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Duopoly/demand:** recurring repair/renovation rather than a single new-housing cycle. [2]
- **Management:** confidence in Ellison's execution. [2]
- **Operating gap:** specific fixable functions, not merely “cheap versus peer.” [2]
- **Cash return:** buybacks compound EPS as operations improve. [2,3]
- **Exit discipline:** uncertainty and opportunity cost can end a successful case. [5]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Classic high-quality laggard/credible-manager improvement, followed by selling when prospective return visibility weakened. [2,5]

## Public discussion

CNBC distinguished Ackman's new holding from D.E. Shaw's separate activist involvement. [1] February 2024 exit reporting fixes a common chronology error: disclosure in 2024 does not make it a 2024 purchase or an open 2026 position. [4]
