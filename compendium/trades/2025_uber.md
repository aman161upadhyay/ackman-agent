## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff: 2025-01-06.** Uber's October 31, 2024 release reported quarterly gross bookings +16% reported / +20% constant currency, operating income $1.1B, adjusted EBITDA $1.7B and FCF $2.1B. The business already demonstrated positive operating cash generation before Ackman bought; it was not a speculative zero-profit platform bet. [1] Autonomous operators were an identifiable strategic risk, but Ackman's March 2025 numerical AV analysis was not public before January entry. The February 2025 earnings release is also excluded. Pre-entry daily stock range and an independently sourced December bear consensus: **unknown**.

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The **2025-02-07** X post, scraped with Firecrawl:

> “Beginning in early January, we began acquiring a position in @Uber. Today, we own 30.3 million shares.”

> “Since he joined the company in 2017, Dara Khosrowshahi CEO has done a superb job in transforming the company into a highly profitable and cash-generative growth machine.”

> “We believe that Uber is one of the best managed and highest quality businesses in the world.” [2]

The 2024 annual report's **post-entry** detailed argument: scale, data and dense network reduce waits and prices; earners obtain higher utilization; bookings grow through users, frequency, products and geography; fixed expense control creates operating leverage and anticipated 30%+ earnings growth. AV fears disproportionately discounted the US rideshare exposure. Safety, regulation, manufacturing and operations constrain rollout; variable weekly demand makes a fixed AV fleet poorly matched to demand. A hybrid marketplace uses AV baseload plus flexible human supply. Partnering with Uber can increase utilization enough to justify its fee and avoid duplicating its distribution/infrastructure. Lower AV costs could expand rideshare penetration, but he did not require a dramatic penetration gain for the base case. [3]

## What happened after

2025 gross PSH NAV contribution +2.7 percentage points. [8] H1 2026 letter records share price down 12% through June 30 and about 35% earnings growth expected in 2026, but ongoing AV concern and an increasingly strained Waymo relationship. It marks valuation at 19x manager-estimated earnings, not the January 2025 entry multiple. [9] Latest verified position remains open; Q3 2026 ownership changes are not yet reportable as of the archive cutoff.

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Management:** transformation from erratic leadership to profitable execution under Dara. [2]
- **Marketplace:** density, low waits and earner utilization sustain network advantages. [3]
- **FCF/growth:** existing cash generation plus operating leverage supports compounding. [1,3]
- **AV complementarity:** peak/off-peak mismatch supports distribution partnerships, not an AV manufacturing bet. [3]
- **Price:** feared disintermediation created an unusually low valuation for anticipated growth. [3,4]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Fits a scalable, capital-light consumer/service platform with strong management. The analytical burden is proving that a feared technology improves rather than destroys the moat; uncertainty over the ultimate AV structure remains material. [3,9]

## Public discussion

The verified announcement thread includes a February 7 counterclaim from @farzyness that Uber is “Blockbuster in the age of self-driving technology,” plus Dara's positive response to Ackman's ownership. This is a useful **disclosure-time**, not pre-entry, contrast. [2] The later Waymo concern shows the bear case did not disappear simply because Ackman invested. [9]
