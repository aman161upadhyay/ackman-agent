## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Update only.** Cross-reference [original Justice/Burger King investment](2011_justice_burger_king.md). No new 2024 purchase cutoff has been verified, so a new-entry point-in-time pack is not manufactured. The requested “2024 exit?” is contradicted by later primary holdings and letters. [1-3]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

August 2026 letter expects QSR to return to a capital-light structure and sees durable earnings growth plus capital returns. It cites improvements at Burger King and international operations, and an earnings multiple below peers. ## What Pershing Square did

Continued ownership through 2024 and 2025 reports, rather than a confirmed exit. [2,3] Q1 2026 tracker reports approximately 22.65M common shares. June 30 consolidated 13F reports **25,821,284** / $1,872,301,303, approximately 14% more shares under the enlarged reporting perimeter. [4,5] Schedule 13D snapshots can differ from 13F counts due to beneficial-ownership definitions and dates; no invented reconciliation.

## What happened after

2025 gross PSH NAV contribution +0.5 points. [3] H1 2026 letter reports Burger King first-half same-store sales +7%, International +6%, operating-profit growth expectation 8% and estimated valuation 17x earnings; +0.7 gross NAV points H1 and +0.9 through August 11. These are post-period observations/estimates, not old entry facts. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Franchising:** capital-light recurring royalties underpin predictable earnings. [1]
- **Burger King repair:** remodels/brand execution support peer outperformance. [1,2]
- **International:** unit and comparable-sales growth extend the runway. [1]
- **Capital return:** buybacks, leverage and discount can supplement operating growth. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Fits a long-held franchise cash-flow model. Temporary ownership of restaurants during remodeling is not equivalent to permanently abandoning asset-light economics. [1,2]

## Public discussion

The important evidentiary correction is that **QSR was not exited in 2024** in the sources inspected. Trackers corroborate continuing ownership, but manager perimeter changes mean Q2 share additions should not all be labeled same-fund discretionary purchases. [4,5] Risks remain consumer spending, franchisee economics and capital intensity during turnaround. [1,2]
