## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
Fortune combined spirits, golf and home/security businesses. By October 2010 Pershing had disclosed 10.9%, making it the largest shareholder. Do not place the December breakup announcement into the September entry information set. Entry valuation and segment financials unknown in retrieved sources. [1,5]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words
“This is great news for the company and great news for the shareholders.” — Ackman, December 7, 2010 response to the planned split. This confirms his reaction, not a complete original sum-of-parts valuation. Original breakup deck not authenticated; drivers below are sourced campaign paraphrases. [1]

## What Pershing Square did
- September–October 2010: accumulated shares; October disclosed 10.9%. [1,5]
- December 2010: company announced intended separation of its three businesses. [1]
- May 2011: company agreed to sell Acushnet golf to a Fila-led group for $1.23 billion cash. [3]
- August 2011: Pershing raised ownership to 13.5%. [2]
- 2011: resulting spirits exposure continued as Beam; home/security exposure became FBHS. FBHS was eliminated by Q2 2012. [4,6]

## What happened after
The corporate-action objective was realized; `win` refers to that result, not a verified combined investment IRR. Beam's later acquisition is covered separately to avoid counting the same parent cost basis twice. Combined FO/Beam/FBHS P&L unknown. [1–4,6]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Separation:** conglomerate breakup created independently valued businesses. [1]
- **Spirits focus:** the retained spirits company had recognizable brands including Jim Beam and Maker's Mark. [3]
- **Monetization:** Acushnet was sold rather than spun; FBHS became a separate quoted business. [3–4]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: a structural value-unlocking campaign rather than a distressed operating rescue. The evidence does not justify attributing every board decision solely to Pershing. [1–3]

## Public discussion
NYT DealBook covered the December announcement and May golf sale contemporaneously; Reuters tracked the stake increase. Original VIC/COBF/Seeking Alpha discussion and a verified original Pershing deck remain gaps. [1–3]
