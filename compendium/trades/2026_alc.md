## Ackman's thesis in his own words

August letter describes surgical vision as a “classic ‘razor / razor-blade’ model” and AURN001 as an “underappreciated call option.” [1] Full stated reasoning: leading global ophthalmology franchise; approximately 30,000 installed surgical equipment units generate recurring consumables; scale funds commercial/R&D capability; aging populations, income growth and healthcare access support volumes. Current approximately 20% operating margin was below an anticipated 25%+ structural potential; launch costs, tariffs, FX and temporary clinical R&D masked potential. Contact-lens manufacturing maturation and operating leverage support mid-teens EPS growth. Valuation fell from high-20s to about 18x. AURN001 clinical/regulatory upside is additional, uncertain and should not substitute for the core case; $1.5B authorized repurchases support per-share returns. All figures are August manager assessments. [1]
