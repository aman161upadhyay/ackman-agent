## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**2018 first execution date unknown.** No reliable pre-entry financial/price pack assembled. Otis/Carrier separation announced later in 2018 cannot be a completed event in an earlier purchase pack. The 2019 Raytheon transaction plainly belongs after entry. [1,2]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The 2018 annual report underwrites the value of strong aviation, elevator and HVAC franchises when separately managed/valued, with recurring service/aftermarket revenues and operating improvement. [1] No independently verified long original quote or X post recovered; the June 2019 opposition to Raytheon is a **changed-thesis** statement, not the original purchase announcement. [2]

## What Pershing Square did

Built a 2018 position, supported separation into aerospace, Otis and Carrier. Objected to June 2019 Raytheon merger proposal as inconsistent with unlocking a focused aerospace valuation. Sold remaining shares July 2019; publicly reported August 5. [1-3] Exact initial and final fills, peak weight and dollar return unknown.

## What happened after

Exited before the eventual 2020 separation/merger completion. Manager report describes a profitable investment; it should not receive credit/blame for the post-exit RTX/Carrier/Otis returns. [4]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Quality:** installed bases and aftermarket service support durable cash flows. [1]
- **Structure:** standalone businesses can be valued and run more efficiently. [1]
- **Catalyst:** breakup, not a generic aerospace-cycle bet. [1]
- **Exit:** new conglomerate merger changed the original expected outcome. [2,3]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Classic breakup activism; willingness to sell when management chooses a different transaction rather than rationalize permanent ownership. [1-3]

## Public discussion

CNBC/WSJ reporting covered opposition to Raytheon; merger sponsors argued scale/technology complementarity. Both accounts postdate entry. [2] No independently verified Reddit/VIC discussion.
