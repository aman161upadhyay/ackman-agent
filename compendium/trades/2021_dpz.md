## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff 2021-02-28; first day in March unknown.** A full dated earnings/price pack remains incomplete; subsequent driver shortages and 2022 pricing changes are not March entry facts. Reporting establishes buying after a selloff, not exact fill prices. [1]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

Manager reports emphasize the durable franchisor model, strong technology and advertising scale, international store growth and attractive unit economics. Fortressing increases delivery speed/density and customer service while capital-light royalties compound. [2,3] Direct verified entry-day quote/X unavailable.

The August 2022 exit explanation: valuation had rebounded above 28x its estimate of forward earnings in a volatile market, so cash was better available for other investments. That was not a declaration that Domino's was broken. [3]

## What Pershing Square did

Bought approximately 2M shares in March 2021, funded in part by selling Starbucks; announced May 12 nearly 6% stake. [1,4] Still held roughly 2M at June 30, 2022; fully sold in **July 2022**, before August interim publication—not 2024. [3,4]

## What happened after

Shares rose over 50% through end-2021; declined 29% through March 22, 2022. Driver shortages, reduced delivery hours and labor/food inflation slowed comps; pricing and staffing measures later supported recovery. [2,3] Reporting cites entry near the $330 selloff and shares about $400 in August 2022, suggesting a modest positive price round trip; **actual weighted realized profit is unknown**, so the `win` classification is provisional, not a verified net-return calculation. [4]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Franchise:** recurring capital-light royalties. [2]
- **Scale:** digital/advertising/procurement support competitive value. [2]
- **Density:** fortressing improves service. [2]
- **Runway:** international unit growth. [2]
- **Exit:** high multiple versus alternatives after recovery. [3]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Classic franchise compounder, but a shorter holding than intended due to relative prospective returns. A 2021 mark-to-market win and 2022 drawdown are not separate investments. [2,3]

## Public discussion

Reuters announced the stake and Starbucks switch; August coverage corrected trackers that still showed the June holding by reporting the subsequent sale. [1,4] This is a useful example of quarter-end holdings lagging current ownership.
