## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff 2025-11-23.** Meta's October 29 prepared remarks reported quarterly operating income $20.5B and 40% operating margin, and warned 2026 capex dollar growth would be notably larger than 2025. The same earnings materials disclosed a one-time noncash tax charge of $15.93B; GAAP earnings should not be confused with underlying ad-business cash generation. [1,2] This was public before November 24. The bear question was whether escalating AI investment and Reality Labs spending destroyed returns, not whether the platform had no revenue. Exact pre-entry trading range/consensus unknown. January 2026 management assurances below are excluded from this pack.

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The February 2026 report identifies Meta as one of the “clearest beneficiaries of AI.” [3] It describes network scale and first-party behavioral data improving ad targeting; better recommendations increasing engagement; Advantage+ and generative creative tools improving advertiser ROI; messaging/Business AI and wearables adding options. The purchase followed a roughly 20% drawdown after the Q3 call. At about 20x EPS including Reality Labs losses, the implied core advertising multiple was below 16x in the manager's analysis. It expected frontloaded compute/talent investment to support future earnings; management could curb costs if growth disappointed, as in its earlier efficiency program. [3]

Those are **February explanations of November purchases**, not contemporaneous public Ackman statements. The verified May 15 Microsoft X post retrospectively compares Meta's capex-related selloff to his Google/Amazon purchases. [4] A META-specific original X thesis post has not been independently recovered.

## What Pershing Square did

November 24, 2025 first purchase; inception multiple 21.1x and $625 average announcement cost in presentation. Public disclosure February 11, 2026. [5,6] Q1 2026 tracker: approximately 2.66M shares. June 30 consolidated 13F: 3,196,062 / $1,800,309,764, an approximately 20% share-count increase within the changed parent reporting perimeter. [7,8]

## What happened after

2025 gross PSH contribution +0.7 percentage points. [3] H1 2026 letter reports shares down 15% through June; anticipated 20%+ future EPS growth and 18x forward multiple. It argues unused internally built compute can serve external demand. Those remain forecasts, not proof all capex earns acceptable returns. [9]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Network/ad moat:** entrenched audience, behavioral data and advertiser demand reinforce value. [3]
- **AI use cases:** recommendations and ad efficiency already offer monetizable applications. [3]
- **Dislocation:** near-term spending reduces visible earnings without necessarily impairing value. [3]
- **Core valuation:** separate Reality Labs losses from ad-franchise earnings. [3]
- **Flexibility:** cost control is a downside lever if investment demand disappoints. [3]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Fits a familiar dominant business bought during a temporary narrative shock. Founder control and very large discretionary reinvestment require unusual confidence in management's capital allocation. [3]

## Public discussion

Reuters announced the Meta addition alongside Hilton's exit on February 11. [6] Spending skepticism is not answered by share-price gains alone; the relevant bear case is structurally lower FCF/ROIC despite revenue growth. The primary letter addresses investment returns and expense flexibility, not merely AI excitement. [3]
