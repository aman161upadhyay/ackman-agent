## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff before first late-February execution; exact day unknown.** COVID spreading outside China and the mismatch between contagion risk and tight credit pricing were the manager's stated opportunity. Full day-specific public pandemic/credit-spread pack is not assembled; use unknown rather than the subsequent March crash as a pre-entry observation. [1] The famous March 18 television interview occurred **after inception** and after unwinding had begun. [2,3]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The 2019 report's April 2020 account describes index CDS as inexpensive portfolio insurance with an asymmetric payoff if pandemic risks materialized, while preserving stakes in high-quality businesses and liquidity to buy them at distressed prices. [1] This is a contemporaneous-after-exit primary explanation, not proof he published every rationale before purchase.

He later defended the March 18 appearance as bullish conditional on a forceful shutdown response and clarified he was already unwinding protection and purchasing equities. [3] A short clip saying “hell is coming” is not a complete statement of the trade's direction or timing. [2,3]

## What Pershing Square did

Bought US/European credit-index protection in late February; publicly disclosed March 3. Began unwinding March 12, completed March 23. Paid **$27M premiums/commissions across funds** and received **$2.6B gross proceeds**, approximately $2.1B attributable to PSH. [1] Bought/increased long positions including Hilton, Lowe's, QSR, Berkshire and Starbucks rather than remain permanently bearish. [1]

## What happened after

Extraordinary realized hedge gain plus reinvestment helped PSH recover from the market collapse. Gross proceeds are not net profit, premium is not notional, all-funds figures are not PSH-only figures and calendar PSH return is not the hedge return. [1] A separate smaller credit hedge bought later in 2020 is recorded in `2020_cds_2.md`; it should not inherit this win's outcome.

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Variant risk:** contagion could freeze activity faster than markets priced. [1]
- **Asymmetry:** small premium versus large spread-widening payoff. [1]
- **Portfolio protection:** preserve quality holdings and avoid forced selling. [1]
- **Monetization:** sell when panic/central-bank action changed expected payoff and redeploy. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Unusual macro trade made consistent with a concentrated long portfolio through limited upfront premium and tactical asymmetry. It is not evidence of routine profitable market timing. [1]

## Public discussion

Controversy centered on March 18 remarks and accusations of talking down markets. CNBC's March 27 coverage gives his detailed response and the earlier unwind chronology. Preserve both the alarming remarks and the conditional shutdown/recovery thesis. [2,3]
