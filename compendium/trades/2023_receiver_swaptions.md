## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Conservative cutoff 2023-09-30; exact October entry day unknown.** Pre-entry policy, rate curve and option-surface pack incomplete. Do not use the later realized rate path as evidence of original asymmetry. The October 23 public bond-short-cover announcement is a separate dated disclosure, not proof of these instruments' precise entry day. [1,2]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The annual report says October assessment was that rates were unlikely to rise further; after selling long-rate payer options, the team bought one-/five-year instruments benefiting from declines. [1] Ackman's October cover statements cited geopolitical danger and slowing growth. Those give context but are **not a complete independently verified receiver-position valuation thesis**. [2]

## What Pershing Square did

Sold thirty-year payer swaptions in October 2023, then initiated the new receiver instruments, still held at March 2024 reporting. December 31, 2023 sensitivity table: one-year fair-value exposure $31,685,649; five-year $185,648,191. Not premium, notional or demonstrated peak size. [1] `open` reflects that last explicit report; October 2026 status unknown. Cross-reference `2023_treasury_short.md`; these are a reversal, not the same continuous directional position.

## What happened after

2023 +92bp US rate-options contribution combines instruments/tenors and cannot safely be allocated entirely to these receivers. Separate round-trip P&L unknown. [1] Later aggregate hedge costs in the 2024 letter cannot be attributed precisely without a contract ledger. [3]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Updated rates view:** no longer expected long rates to keep rising. [1]
- **Direction:** selected instruments payoff when shorter/intermediate rates decline. [1]
- **Framework:** asymmetry/protection is a general program objective; a complete entry model remains unknown. [1]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Demonstrates willingness to reverse directional macro exposure rather than remain attached to a profitable prior call. Outcome is not established merely because the switch was announced. [1,2]
