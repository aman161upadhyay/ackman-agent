## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff 2023-03-01.** ChatGPT's emergence and February Bard concerns predated first purchase and explain why AI substitution needed underwriting. First purchase March 2 and 15.7x inception multiple are documented by PSH's later chart. [1] A full pre-entry February earnings/consensus pack has not been independently extracted; unknown. The later Gemini/AI Overview adoption statistics and 2025 Cloud acceleration must not enter the March 2023 blind pack. [2,3]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The 2023 report explains that proprietary data, distribution, technical AI expertise and optimized infrastructure made Alphabet a likely beneficiary, not inevitable victim, of generative AI. Search intent/advertising economics, YouTube, growing Cloud profitability and expense discipline supported long-term earnings. [2] The thesis was not “ChatGPT will fail”; it was that the incumbent had tools and distribution to adapt.

The verified May 15, 2026 Microsoft X post recalls the Google purchase but says “late 2022.” **Conflict:** the precise PSH inception chart is March 2, 2023; prefer the specific dated chart and flag the retrospective imprecision. [1,4] A May 16 follow-up says Google was a funding source for Microsoft, not a bet against Alphabet; indexed/Reuters-corrobated, original scrape failed. [5]

## What Pershing Square did

Bought both GOOG/GOOGL classes, initially March 2, 2023, publicly visible May 15 13F. [1,2] Q1 2026 cut both classes by about **95%**, leaving 311,726 GOOG and 32,376 GOOGL common shares in the tracker. [6] June 30 parent 13F contains neither class and interim letter confirms full H1 exit. Exact final sale day/fills unknown. [7,8]

## What happened after

2025 PSH gross NAV contribution **+10.3 percentage points**. [3] Presentation reports 77% gross/58% net return through February 9, 2026; these are **interim**, not final exit returns. [1] Selling was relative valuation/finite capital allocation toward Microsoft rather than a declared collapse of Google's moat. [5,8]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Distribution/intent:** powerful starting point for monetizing search. [2]
- **AI assets:** proprietary data, research and infrastructure. [2]
- **Variant perception:** disruption fear exceeded justified earnings impairment. [2]
- **Operating levers:** Cloud, YouTube and expense control. [2,3]
- **Opportunity cost:** compare future returns even after a successful holding. [5]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Quality platform de-rated by technological fear; shows that AI-related uncertainty can create opportunity when one can bound durable economics. [2] The full sale is not evidence the original thesis failed.

## Public discussion

2026 Reuters reporting emphasizes large Google cuts and Microsoft addition. [5] Acknowledging continuing bullishness while selling prevents the false dichotomy “hold forever or business is bad.” No verified original 2023 X thesis post retrieved.
