## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

First purchase unknown. The March 2006 13D says the issuer retained Wachovia January 10 to review strategic alternatives, including a company or asset sale. It reports no trades in the preceding 60 days, so **March 27 is not the purchase date**. Original entry financial appraisal unknown. [13D, Items 4–5, signed 2006-03-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/805419/000110465906019539/a06-7728_1sc13d.htm).

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

Signed filing: the Reporting Persons “may speak with potential acquirers of the Issuer or its assets and/or participate in such a strategic transaction.” This establishes transaction-oriented engagement but not a recovered original NAV or price target. [13D, Item 4, 2006-03-27, above.]

## What Pershing Square did

Reported 2,077,602 shares collectively, 9.7%, and converted from the prior 13G reporting posture to 13D. The filing says funds were working capital and no borrowing financed the transactions. Any later purchases or private negotiations are unknown. [13D, Items 3–5, 2006-03-27, above.]

## What happened after

August 21 reporting says Revenue Properties agreed to pay $15.10 cash/share, approximately $324M equity value, and assume $85M debt. Announcement is not verified Pershing settlement or final realized P&L. [DealBook, 2006-08-21](https://dealbook.nytimes.com/2006/08/21/revenue-properties-acquires-sizeler-for-324-million/).

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Strategic monetization:** issuer sale process expressly referenced.
- **Engagement:** potential buyer discussions/participation expressly reserved.
- **Quantitative valuation:** unknown. [Signed 13D, 2006-03-27, above.]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Real-estate assets plus a sale catalyst align with early transaction-oriented activism. Evidence supports possible engagement, not crediting Ackman alone with causing the sale. [13D and announcement, above.]

## Public discussion

Contemporary deal coverage gives price and debt terms but no security-level Pershing return. [DealBook, 2006-08-21, above.]
