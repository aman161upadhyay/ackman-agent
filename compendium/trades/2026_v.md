## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Earliest purchase day unknown; Q2 window. Conservative cutoff 2026-03-31.** January 29, 2026 Visa results reported quarterly net revenue $10.9B (+15%, +13% constant dollar), GAAP net income $5.9B and non-GAAP EPS $3.17. [1] The network processes payments rather than being the cardholder lender. Stablecoins, routing competition and changing online checkout were risks to evaluate, not proof of inevitable displacement. ## Ackman's thesis in his own words

Visa and Mastercard are “capital-light ‘toll-takers’” in the August 2026 primary letter. [2] The joint thesis is specific: ubiquitous acceptance and dense multi-sided networks, nominal approximately 20bp fees for authorization/fraud protection/recourse, inflation-linked nominal volume growth, substantial remaining cash displacement and growing value-added services. Agentic commerce should expand digital transactions and increase the value of authorization, spending limits and identity verification. [2]

He views stablecoins as complementary in remittances/B2B and dollar savings, not equivalent to consumers' fraud-protected credit/rewards products; networks can integrate token settlement. Routing/interest-cap proposals were judged unlikely to materially impair economics. Entry around 22x forward EPS reflected excessive disruption/regulatory fear; he expects mid-to-high-teens EPS growth, not just a multiple recovery. All these explanations are **August disclosures**, not pre-entry public Ackman comments. [2] No Visa-specific X thesis post verified.

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Toll:** transaction economics without material lending exposure. [2]
- **Secular growth:** cash displacement and e-commerce enlarge the volume base. [2]
- **Services:** mix shifts toward faster-growing value-added revenue. [2]
- **Technology:** agents and stablecoins can use network infrastructure rather than replace it. [2]
- **Regulation/price:** assess actual earnings sensitivity, then buy at the de-rated multiple. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Classic predictable, capital-light network compounder purchased during disruption fear. Thesis overlap with Uber is distribution infrastructure retaining value when the underlying delivery technology changes. This is an analytical comparison, not a quotation. [2]

## Public discussion

Reuters framed the investment as part of a six-company portfolio overhaul.
