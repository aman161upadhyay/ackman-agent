## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

This is a distinct stock re-entry after the original position was sold in Q4 2007 near $56. It is present in June 2009 holdings; exact first day is unknown. The original 2009 letter says Pershing could repurchase slightly cheaper despite approximately 16% growth in trailing after-tax earnings, 5% share reduction, 33% higher dividend and a franchised mix reaching 80% since the prior exit. These are the **later letter's** comparisons, not guaranteed metrics as of an unknown purchase day. [Original Q2 2009 letter, pages 5–6](https://www.scribd.com/document/20150688/Pershing-Square-Q2-2009-Investor-Letter).

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

“McDonald’s brand royalty business is one of the greatest businesses in the world because it generates an annuity-like revenue stream which can grow without the requirement for meaningful investment of capital from the company.” He called its worldwide cash flow stable, currency-hedged and inflation-protected, and the approximately 13x 2010 earnings multiple inadequate to fair value. [Original Q2 2009 letter, pages 5–6, public by 2009-09-24, above.]

## What Pershing Square did

June 13F: 2.4M common shares, $137.976M market value. The original letter subsequently calls the position approximately 10% of funds' capital; these are **different dates and denominators**. The Q3 holdings digest records a 243.6% share increase. No revival of the 2005 McOpCo IPO demand was established for 2009. [Primary 13F, 2009-08-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336528/000117266109001387/0001172661-09-001387.txt); [Market Folly, 2009-11-17](https://www.marketfolly.com/2009/11/bill-ackmans-pershing-square-boosts.html).

## What happened after

Sold out during Q4 2009; December is a quarter-end bound. Exact realized return unknown. The earlier 2005–07 position's gains cannot be combined with this episode without the actual cash-flow ledger. [Market Folly, 2010-02-17](https://www.marketfolly.com/2010/02/bill-ackmans-pershing-square-dumps.html).

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Price versus improved business:** explicit comparison with the 2007 exit.
- **Capital-light annuity:** franchise fee share of a diversified global system.
- **Refranchising:** mix shift toward higher-quality royalty earnings.
- **Capital return:** fewer shares and higher dividends.
- **Valuation:** approximately 13x 2010 earnings in the letter, not an invented exact purchase multiple. [Original Q2 2009 letter, above.]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

A passive purchase can harvest value creation from a former activist target. The stated underwriting emphasizes cash-flow quality and price rather than management replacement. [Original Q2 2009 letter, above.]

## Public discussion

Contemporary holdings coverage called attention to the Q3 add and Q4 exit. Its 13F weights must not be repeated as fund-NAV allocations. [Market Folly, 2009-11-17 and 2010-02-17, above.]
