## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Conservative cutoff 2026-03-31; exact first Q2 purchase unknown.** Mastercard's January 29 release reported Q4 2025 net income $4.1B, GAAP EPS $4.52 and adjusted EPS $4.76; FY2025 net revenue increased 16% reported/15% currency neutral. [1] Existing network acceptance, competition from non-card rails and exposure to nominal transaction/cross-border volumes were the relevant business questions. Later regulatory outcomes and the manager's August services-mix estimates are excluded from the pre-purchase pack. [2] Exact entry price range and pre-entry independent bear valuation are unknown.

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The joint Visa/Mastercard section calls the businesses “among the highest-quality businesses in the world.” [2] Full joint reasoning: dense global acceptance and accumulated data; minimal incremental transaction cost and high cash conversion; nominal-spending inflation protection; remaining cash-to-card conversion; e-commerce expansion; faster-growing value-added services. Mastercard's services mix was cited at about 40% of revenue **as of the August explanation**, versus approximately 30% at Visa. [2]

Stablecoins are more relevant where cards are not incumbent and lack important consumer recourse/credit/rewards attributes; the networks can supply settlement and access. Agentic commerce needs trusted credentials and programmable limits, and may enlarge the transaction pool. Regulation was assessed as less threatening than the de-rating implied. Prospective earnings growth and a low entry multiple, rather than a promised takeover, motivated purchase. [2] No MA-specific X post verified; see companion `2026_v.md` for the same explicitly joint primary-source thesis.

## What Pershing Square did

Q2 2026 entry; August 13 public announcement; August 14 filing for June 30 shows 2,124,646 shares / $1.091B / 5.61% of the consolidated reportable book. Exact first day, fill prices and fund-specific allocation unknown. [2,3]

## What happened after

PSH reports +5% from average cost to June 30; +0.7 percentage points gross NAV contribution through August 11. June valuation estimate 24x NTM earnings and approximately 18% three-to-five-year EPS CAGR including yield. These are manager estimates and interim marks, not realized return guarantees. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Network:** acceptance, institutions and consumers reinforce one another. [2]
- **Toll economics:** earns fees without materially underwriting card credit. [2]
- **Growth:** services and digitization supplement spending growth. [2]
- **New rails:** integrations and trusted agent credentials can preserve relevance. [2]
- **Price/regulation:** fears created a rare de-rating in a long-followed franchise. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Fits predictable FCF and a durable moat; holding both networks spreads company-specific risk but does not diversify the shared regulatory/technology thesis. This is an analytical inference from the joint case. [2]

## Public discussion

Reuters reported the new position alongside Visa, Netflix and SPGI. [4] The bear view is structural fee compression or replacement of card rails, not simply recession. Regulatory changes, lower cross-border volumes and services monetization failure are relevant failure modes; the joint letter provides Ackman's rebuttals, not proof these risks are impossible. [2]
