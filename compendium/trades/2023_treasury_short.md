## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Actual first purchase unknown. August 2 is public disclosure, not initiation.** The 2023 annual report says the 30-year options already stood 92% above cost at the beginning of 2023, establishing earlier ownership. [1] A clean initiation pack is unavailable. Do not use the 5% October yield or later war as pre-entry inputs. The public August thesis can be replayed as a **hold/add/cover** decision instead.

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

Reuters quotes the August 2 X statement: “We are short in size the 30-year T.” He argued 3% rather than 2% long-term inflation could imply 5.5% long yields, with deficits/supply, deglobalization and other inflationary pressures supporting the trade. [2] It was both a long-equity-duration hedge and a standalone opportunity. [2]

October 23 X exit, reported verbatim by CNBC: “We covered our bond short.” “There is too much risk in the world to remain short bonds at current long-term rates.” “The economy is slowing faster than recent data suggests.” [3] **Retrieval caveat:** direct X scrape unavailable; the date/quotes are verified through dated Reuters/CNBC rather than falsely described as a successful original-post scrape.

## What Pershing Square did

Held 30-year payer swaptions before 2023; announced the bearish rate view August 2; sold the payer options October 2023 and announced cover October 23. [1-3] This is the same economic episode as the annual report's long-rate payer options, not a separate Treasury-bond short with independently additive profit. Cross-reference `2020_interest_rate_hedges.md` for earlier vintages and `2023_receiver_swaptions.md` for the reversal.

## What happened after

Annual report says options were sold at **33% premium above cost**, below their early-year 92% unrealized gain, because protection had been retained. Dollar profit unknown. [1] The 2023 **aggregate hedging program lost 187bp**, so do not present this one winner as the full hedging result. [1]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Inflation:** estimate equilibrium long inflation, not just next CPI print. [2]
- **Supply/term premium:** large borrowing creates price pressure. [2]
- **Portfolio:** protection can justify retaining gains longer than a standalone trader would. [1]
- **Exit:** updated geopolitical and growth risks reversed expected payoff. [3]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Tactical macro outside core businesses, structured with options and revisable thesis. The public August announcement cannot establish a three-month total holding period. [1-3]

## Public discussion

CNBC linked covering to safe-haven demand and slowing growth; rates fell on announcement. That market reaction is not proof the investor caused the prior selloff or foresaw every subsequent move. [3]
