## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Cutoff 2020-07-21.** Public investors were purchasing cash-in-trust and sponsor deal-selection ability, not a disclosed operating target. Later UMG negotiations must not enter the IPO pack. Full final unit/warrant mechanics and prospectus snapshot not extracted; do not infer public investor returns solely from NAV. [1,2]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The 2020 report emphasizes alignment: the Pershing **funds** owned the sponsor rather than the manager, and there was no typical 20% free sponsor promote. It sought a mature, high-quality business with large capital needs/opportunity for public ownership. [1] The July 2022 letter explains returning cash instead of taking an unacceptable deal. [3]

## What Pershing Square did

Raised $4B July 22, 2020, $20 trust basis. June 4, 2021 announced proposed purchase of a 10% UMG stake, a residual cash vehicle and SPARC rights rather than a conventional merger. [1,2] SEC concerns prevented that structure; the UMG commitment moved to Pershing funds, not to PSTH shareholders. Those are distinct vehicles with distinct outcomes. [3,4] Announced liquidation July 11, 2022 and returned trust capital in July. [3]

## What happened after

No consummated business combination. `flat` describes approximate cash-trust redemption, **not** a universal investor return: secondary buyers above trust and warrant holders could lose, and sponsor warrant costs mattered. [3] SPARC later gained approval in 2023 but was not a retroactive PSTH merger; August 2026 call still describes it as an unfunded transaction tool. [5]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Capital:** unusually large pool can solve large-company transaction needs. [1]
- **Alignment:** limited upfront promote and fund-owned sponsor. [1]
- **Tontine:** nonredeemers retain contingent warrant benefits. [1]
- **UMG/structure:** business quality did not solve regulatory/SPAC form constraints. [2-4]
- **Discipline:** liquidation beats forcing a bad deal. [3]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

An acquisition vehicle without a known business differs from predictable-earnings investing; alignment and optionality are not substitutes for completing an executable transaction. [1-3]

## Public discussion

The controversy was whether UMG's transfer favored fund investors while PSTH holders were left without a deal. Primary letters explain the SEC/structural constraints but cannot erase different realized investor experiences. [3,4]
