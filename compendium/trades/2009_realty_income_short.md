## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

Exact short initiation unknown. **October 6, 2009 public-pitch snapshot, not assumed purchase date:** a triple-net retail REIT with 2,338 properties, 97% occupancy, top 15 tenants supplying approximately 53% of rent, per presentation drawing on June 2009 filings. Pershing estimated $4.3B enterprise value, $2.7B equity and approximately 40% debt/preferred-to-EV. Its 7.3% 2009E cash-NOI cap rate and 14.4x recurring AFFO were model outputs. [Original deck, slides 2–5, 2009-10-06](https://www.10xebitda.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/Pershing-Square-Realty-Income-Presentation-Oct-2009.pdf).

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

“We Are Short Realty Income.” Thesis slides argue “Properties often have limited alternative use and high re-leasing risk,” “Dividend coverage is minimal,” and the monthly-dividend marketing created demand from retail investors who might not value the company appropriately. At a modeled 9.5% cap rate and 7.5% NOI reduction, the deck estimated approximately $14/share, down 46%. **That is a scenario, not subsequently observed P&L.** [Original deck, slides 2, 12–13, 2009-10-06, above.]

## What Pershing Square did

Publicly presented the short at the Great Investors Best Ideas conference. Disclaimer says short exposures may include CDS, puts or common shorts; it does not quantify the exact mix or position size. Do not assume this is identical to an unnamed GGP valuation hedge mentioned in the separate Q2 letter. [Original deck, 2009-10-06, above; contemporary coverage, 2009-10-19](https://www.marketfolly.com/2009/10/ackman-pershing-squares-realty-income-o.html).

## What happened after

The recovered sources do not establish final cover, realized result or whether the predicted dividend cut occurred during the actual holding window. Therefore no invented loss or win is assigned. A short's total return also includes dividends/borrow costs, not only stock-price movement.

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Tenant credit:** junk/unrated credits with bankruptcy exposure.
- **Re-leasing:** specialized freestanding buildings lack shopping-center traffic anchors.
- **Vacancy leverage:** landlord pays taxes, insurance and capital costs on empty units.
- **Dividend fragility:** a cut could unsettle the income-focused shareholder base.
- **Valuation:** market 7.3% cap rate versus claimed 10–11% private asking cap rates and modeled 9.5% case. [Original deck, slides 12–13, 2009-10-06, above.]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

This is a valuation/credit/holder-incentives short, not an allegation of fraud. Comparing real-estate economics across long and short holdings is not proof every REIT short was a direct GGP hedge. [Original deck, above.]

## Public discussion

A real contemporaneous counterview: Chris Rodriguez argued Realty Income had a strong team and disciplined sale/leaseback underwriting and was skeptical of the portfolio critique. This is better evidence of live disagreement than later shareholder triumphalism. [Seeking Alpha, 2009-10-09](https://seekingalpha.com/article/165650-ackman-attacks-realty-income-lets-see-how-this-plays-out).
