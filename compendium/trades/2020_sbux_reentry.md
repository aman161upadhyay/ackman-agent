## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Exact March 2020 day unknown.** This follows a fully exited January position, not a continuous 2018 holding. No verified pre-purchase price/country-reopening snapshot; avoid using later successful recovery as a March known fact. [1,2]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

March 2021 report says it “opportunistically repurchased a stake” during the March downturn and later sold because expected returns were “below our long-term targets.” [2] The stated holding thesis was temporary COVID impairment of a valuable consumer franchise, supported by digital/drive-through adaptation and recovery experience in China. [2]

## What Pershing Square did

Rebought March 2020 with hedge proceeds, after January's complete exit. Sold again around March 2021 and redeployed toward Domino's. Exact execution dates and weight unknown. [1-3]

## What happened after

The report states stock price was about twice what it paid a year earlier; that is a price comparison, not a verified fund-level net return. 2020 gross PSH NAV contribution +7.6 percentage points. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Durability:** temporary closures did not destroy long-run brand value. [2]
- **Adaptation:** digital/drive-through reduce shutdown exposure. [2]
- **Reopening:** China experience informed US operations. [2]
- **Valuation:** buy after dislocation, sell after expected return compresses. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Useful counterexample to “never re-enter a sold stock”: the same good business can be sold near value and bought back at a much better price. [2]

## Public discussion

Domino's purchase reporting identifies Starbucks liquidation as financing; avoid describing the switch as loss of confidence in Starbucks management. [3]
