## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)
**2013 entry year is provisional:** Platform existed as an acquisition platform; the exact first purchase date and original subscription documents were not recovered. The earliest detailed primary thesis authenticated here is the 2014 annual report. No 2015 foreign-exchange shock, later leadership departures or 2019 exit is admissible as entry knowledge. [1–2]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words
“Platform’s business model of investment in asset-light, high-touch specialty chemical businesses is characterized by high margins, low capital intensity, and high switching-costs.” — March 24, 2015 report. [1]

The same report says the opportunity was to invest large amounts at high returns by acquiring businesses that could operate more efficiently together. This is an acquisition-platform case, not simply a low multiple on a static chemicals portfolio. [1]

## What Pershing Square did
Owned the acquisition platform, supported its growth and continued holding through operational weakness. The 2017 annual report still includes an ongoing Platform discussion and a prospective separation of Agricultural/Performance Solutions: **it did not exit in 2017**. The May 17, 2019 shareholder letter explicitly says the position was exited during Q1 2019. Exact exit day/month unknown. [1–4]

## What happened after
Pershing's 2015 report records underlying EBITDA down 1%, an approximately 12% FX hit to reported EBITDA growth, leverage around six times EBITDA versus a 4.5-times long-term target, management departures and a **−45% 2015 stock return**. These are contemporaneous adverse outcomes, not cumulative trade loss. [2]

In 2017 the business recovered operationally but the stock ended essentially unchanged; the later 2019 exit is verified. Cumulative return unknown; `flat` is an **unverified outcome placeholder**, not a claim the −45% drawdown was fully recouped. [3–4]

## Reasoning drivers checklist
- **Asset-light:** limited plant capital needs were supposed to support cash returns. [1]
- **Switching costs:** specialized customer service/process integration differentiated products. [1]
- **Platform efficiency:** acquisitions were expected to improve portfolio economics. [1]
- **Capital allocators:** the 2015 report explicitly notes loss of the premium formerly assigned to perceived acquisition ability. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern
Research interpretation: quality niche franchises fit his preferences; high leverage, repeated deals, FX exposure and reliance on a management premium impaired predictability. No authenticated rule change attributable solely to Platform was recovered. [1–2]

## Public discussion
Pershing's 2015 and 2017 letters offer candid dated bull-case/rebuttal evidence, but are not independent public sentiment. Contemporary forum, Seeking Alpha, Twitter and Reddit discussion remains unverified. Do not use the company's later Element Solutions identity to infer a 2017 sale. [2–4]
