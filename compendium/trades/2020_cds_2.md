## Situation before the trade (point-in-time)

**Exact late-2020 entry unknown; no clean cutoff pack.** Later COVID concerns and tight credit spreads are a retrospective rationale, not a substitute for a dated entry-day spread series. [1]

## Ackman's thesis in his own words

November reporting describes protection against a worsening winter while bullish on the following year's equity recovery. March 2021 letter explains smaller credit protection remained in the portfolio. [1,2] Direct verified X post/long original quote unknown.

## What Pershing Square did

Re-established smaller index CDS during late 2020 after spreads narrowed; announced publicly by November 11. This was **not** the same uninterrupted position monetized March 23. [1,2] Closed status describes the historical hedge episode, not proof of an exact final sale date, which remains unknown.

## What happened after

2021 primary performance table records index CDS -1.1 percentage points. [3] That is a calendar-year cost, not enough to calculate the complete episode return. Do not assign the first hedge's 100x-style payoff to this episode.

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Tail risk:** continuing pandemic downside despite optimism. [2]
- **Portfolio:** hedge long equities, not liquidate the strategy. [2]
- **Premium:** take small asymmetric insurance exposure. [1,2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Records the cost of repeat insurance, counterbalancing survivorship bias from the spectacular first hedge. [1,3]

## Public discussion

Guardian and CNBC described a new hedge but emphasized Ackman's bullish equity stance. The distinction matters for judging aggregate market exposure. [2]
