## Ackman's thesis in his own words

August letter calls ICE a “simple, predictable, free-cash-flow-generative business.” [1] Approximately 70% of earnings derives from Exchanges; vertical trading/clearing integration creates liquidity and margin-offset network advantages. Energy benchmarks, market volatility and secular hedging needs support growth. FIDS and mortgage technology have smaller earnings contributions, with relatively little commoditized data exposure. He argues perpetual futures do not replicate institutional liquidity, fixed financing, physical delivery, specific-duration hedging or centralized counterparty protection. Mortgage technology offers recovery optionality after depressed originations. Entry near 17x versus 25x a year earlier, buybacks and low/mid-teens EPS growth support expected returns. These are August assertions, not proven outcomes. [1]
