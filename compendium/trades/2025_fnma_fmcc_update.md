## Ackman's thesis in his own words

The February 2026 report recounts the November 18 plan: account for repayment of Treasury senior preferred, exercise **79.9%** common warrants, and relist on NYSE while initially retaining conservatorship. That order preserves flexibility and avoids forcing a potentially dilutive IPO before a credible exit framework. It estimates common shares above $40 under the plan and over $300B value for taxpayers' stake; these are scenario values, not enacted policy. [1]

The report distinguishes later actions: revise capital requirements, retain a paid government backstop through purchase agreements, clarify post-release FHFA regulation and appoint strong management/boards. Retained earnings can recapitalize without a rushed primary offering. [1] The presentation PDF publicly linked by November 18 coverage is available; the original November 18 X status URL remains unverified after this pass's Firecrawl search rate limits. [3]

**Additional authenticated X evidence from the companion research pass:** December 30, 2024 Ackman admitted “they have not been great investments to date,” discussed approximately $34 value/$31 IPO pricing under a **hypothetical Q4 2026** scenario, and warned of risk. January 16, 2025 **The Art of the Deal** then advocated a 2.5% capital standard and taxpayer upside above $300B. [5,6] May 22 X argued current capital and continuing PSPA support protected the mortgage market. November 13 X explicitly denied sale rumors and reaffirmed **more than 210M** combined common shares. [7,8] March 30, 2026 UTC X said: “They could be a 10X and it could happen soon.” This is promotional forward-looking opinion, not a realized return or an actual IPO timetable. [9]

## What Pershing Square did

January 16, 2025 published detailed recap/release presentation; November 13 reaffirmed ownership; November 18 shifted emphasis to **relisting first while still conserved**, rather than immediate share issuance/privatization. [1,3,6,8] No verified complete share-transaction chronology for 2024-26; standard 13F tables do not disclose these OTC positions. The August 13, 2026 call cites approximately 230M combined shares and a $40-$50 favorable-resolution scenario. [4] Do not substitute a third-party 13F portfolio weight for their economic size.

## What happened after

2025 PSH gross NAV contributions: Fannie +5.8 percentage points, Freddie +5.0. [1] H1 2026 primary report records common shares down 39% and 41% respectively through June 30, with little visible privatization progress and quieter administration commentary. It still sees growing intrinsic value from retained earnings and time for action during the administration. [2] Through August 11 both detracted approximately 3.1 points each from gross NAV performance. [2]

## Reasoning drivers checklist

- **Capital accumulation:** retained earnings improve the balance sheet while politics remains unresolved. [1,2]
- **Senior preferred:** its treatment is essential to common-equity value, not a footnote. [1]
- **Sequence:** relist and address Treasury claims before forcing issuance/exit. [1]
- **Catalyst:** distinguish political indications from completed administrative/legal action. [2]

## Why this trade fits / breaks his pattern

Fits long-duration asymmetric catalyst investing; breaks predictable timing and purely corporate-control principles because government decisions dominate realization. 2026 retracement should prevent labeling the 2025 mark-to-market gain a completed realized win. [1,2]

## Public discussion

November coverage highlighted Ackman's opposition to an immediate government share sale; some summaries blur relisting with leaving conservatorship. The primary report explicitly keeps conservatorship initially. [1,3] Bear cases: punitive dilution, senior-preferred overhang, incompatible capital requirements and indefinitely delayed release. Neither favorable political language nor capital retention resolves all of them. [1,2] Peter Schiff's direct replies challenged the taxpayer/housing implications; see the authenticated opposing-post links in the original common-stock file, rather than pretend X responses were uniformly supportive.
