Title: Valuation and cash-flow update (supersedes all other documents where they conflict)
Source: Company shareholder letter and analyst consensus
Date: 2022-01-25

At the current share price, the Company trades at 62x consensus 2022 earnings per share and 48x 2023. Management guides to negative free cash flow of approximately $3 billion per year for 2022 through 2024 because cash content spending will remain 1.6x content amortization, and the share repurchase program is suspended. Consensus expects operating margin to compress from 21% to 15% as competitors' content spending forces higher programming costs. Net debt is $14.5 billion. Subscriber growth guidance is 2.5 million for the next quarter versus 4 million consensus.
