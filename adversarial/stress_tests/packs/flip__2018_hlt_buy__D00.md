Title: Updated operating trends (supersedes all other documents where they conflict)
Source: Company investor update
Date: 2018-10-01

System-wide comparable RevPAR declined 9% in the latest quarter, the third consecutive year of decline. Net unit growth has turned negative (-2%) as franchisees declined to renew 38% of expiring franchise agreements, citing the brand's weakened guest preference versus newer lodging formats and home-sharing platforms, which now take 31% of room nights in the Company's top-25 markets. Franchise and licensing fee revenue fell 11%. Management guides to further mid-single-digit RevPAR declines and negative unit growth through 2021 and has withdrawn its long-term fee growth algorithm. Pricing power: average daily rate fell 6% while occupancy fell 3 points.
