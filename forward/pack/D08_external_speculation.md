## Boundaries

This is a **sentiment/idea-generation file**, not evidence that Pershing placed an order. Searches were date-bounded to September/October and supplemented with direct CNBC/issuer materials. Most contemporary content analyzes already disclosed holdings; no reliable, independently sourced leak of an undisclosed new public-equity name was located. A freshly published article can simply repackage a **June 30** inventory disclosed in August. Old PSTH/Stripe/Starlink rumors and post-cutoff reports were excluded rather than used to manufacture a fall rumor pipeline.

## Where sources disagree, and why

**Six new positions versus four June additions.** The primary August letter discusses both first-half purchases and later purchases. Several articles collapse the entire narrative into Q2, sometimes using PSUS-specific counts/weights as though they were the consolidated manager's. Use actual dates and vehicle labels from D01/D07; do not average conflicting quantities.

**Launch optimism versus performance caution.** September Ventures commentary promotes recurring fees and private-market access; October bank notes emphasize weaker public-fund performance and fundraising timing. These are competing judgments about business execution, not evidence of a particular venture holding. D06 separates seed funding, target launch and completed external subscriptions.

**Bullish business versus attractive stock today.** HHH insurance growth can be strategically appealing while the commentator waits for a lower price. Likewise a Netflix “20% IRR” quotation has assumptions and a multiyear holding period; it is not an event-horizon stock-price promise. ## Forum/alternative-research gap

No authenticated September/October Reddit thread or Seeking Alpha new-name rumor with independently supported current information was recovered. Search hits largely recycled old discussions or August disclosure. Seeking Alpha's located **December4, 2025** portfolio review is useful history in D07, **not current-window chatter**. One Stocktwits result had an October search dateline but an August article body; it was rejected as fresh evidence. This is a retrieval limit, not proof the communities were silent. No anonymous usernames, quotations, probability estimates or prospective new names are fabricated to fill the gap.

**Analyst inference for prediction discipline:** strongest public clue is persistent conviction in already announced quality holdings, not a credible leak naming the next company. Candidate names proposed from D04 should be labeled independent screening hypotheses. Before scoring any “predicted new position,” require a dated disclosure specifying the issuer, vehicle and whether the action was executed rather than merely admired.
