## Library is an edge, not a watchlist of promised purchases

The August letter explicitly says S&P Global was owned previously and Visa/Mastercard long admired; Netflix is a demonstrated re-entry. This argues against excluding a business merely because he sold it before. It does **not** imply all historically owned companies still pass today's business-quality gate.
