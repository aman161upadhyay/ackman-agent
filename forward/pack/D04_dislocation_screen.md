## Method and limits

**46 price/size-qualifying candidates**, each above $25B equity capitalization and at least 15% below its quoted 52-week high. Quality is a research gate, not a fact established by price: Oracle, Nike and Fiserv particularly require an impairment/leverage rebuttal. US ADRs represent Western European issuers; ASML is included as requested. Prices USD except explicitly labeled European local quotes; capitalization $B throughout. Off-high = 100 × (1 − close/high), calculated from each linked overview, not trailing-year return or analyst-target upside.

Columns: **PE/5Y** = current forward multiple / arithmetic mean of the five historical fiscal-year forward-PE observations on the linked forecast page. This is a **five-observation proxy, NOT a daily five-year average**. **EPS** = consensus growth for the first two forecast fiscal years displayed (current forecast FY/next FY), not a guaranteed next-24-month CAGR. **ND/E** = (total debt − cash)/TTM EBITDA, calculated from statistics; negative means net cash. Lease treatment varies. **P/A** = Pershing connection / activist evidence: O owned currently; H historical holding; U no verified connection found; ? activist status not comprehensively verified. Do not turn U/? into a claim of “never owned”/“no activist.”

Each **S** links the 2026-10-23 price/high/capitalization; **F** links forecast/historical multiple data; **T** links balance-sheet/current valuation. Missing observations are **NR**, never estimated. “Risk” in the cause column means a hypothesis requiring causal verification, **not an established dated explanation**. Consequently the screen is usable for candidate generation, not uniformly execution-ready.
