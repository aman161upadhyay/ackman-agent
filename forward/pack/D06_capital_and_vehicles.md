Title: Deployable capital, vehicle constraints and scoring calendar
Source: PSUS semiannual report; PS Inc. results/10-Q; PSH interim/NAV; HHH presentation
Date: 2026-10-25

## Do not add AUM to cash

PSH, PSUS, Pershing Square Inc. and HHH/Vantage have different shareholders, liabilities and mandates. A consolidated reportable position is not evidence that all those vehicles bought identical weights. Insurance float is not distributable manager cash; an announced fundraising target is not a funded account.

### PSUS: IPO cash mostly invested, not fresh October ammunition

PSUS commenced operations **April 29, 2026**; combined IPO closing announced **April 30**. Gross capital raised was **$5B**. Its August report says **more than 95% deployed**. At June 30, security cost **$4,915,991,173**, fair value **$4,766,163,196**; NAV/common share **$45.93**, market **$37.38**, discount **18.62%**. Those are June measurements, **not today's discount or a subtraction-based cash balance**. The report also describes a subsequent approximately **22%** discount. [PSUS semiannual report, August 12](https://pershingsquareusa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PSUS-Semi-Annual-Report-2026.pdf).

The report shows **$50M Series A preferred**, and an intended debt/total-assets range of approximately **15% to low-20s%**. Intended leverage is not proof that borrowing was completed or unused capacity is unconstrained. Ackman on **October 22** said “we deployed $5 billion of capital of Pershing Square USA”; therefore a forecast premised on an untouched IPO war chest is contradicted by his latest public statement. [CNBC October 22](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/cnbc-exclusive-transcript-pershing-square-founder-ceo-bill-ackman-speaks-with-cnbcs-sara-eisen-on-squawk-on-the-street-today.html). Current cash and discount remain unverified.

### PSH: repurchases compete with portfolio additions

May authorized **$100M** additional buybacks; first-half repurchases **768,054 shares/$40.4M**. June debt weighted-average maturity **5 years**, cost **3.6%**, with no margin leverage or mark-to-market bond covenants. These structural features reduce forced-sale pressure, not economic leverage risk. [PSH interim, August 13](https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/13002306/Pershing-Square-Holdings-Ltd.-June-2026-Interim.pdf).

September AUM **$16,860.6M**, core strategy **$23,045.4M**, firm including HHH **$32,898.4M**; methodology adds back existing PSH bond principal **$2.3B and €1.15B**. **Do not count these bond proceeds again as newly issued September capital.** September long exposure **126%** likewise is not a cash figure. [September report, October 1](https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/01190633/Performance-Report-September-2026-PSH.pdf). Daily own-share announcements continued into October, but their aggregate remaining authorization was not reconciled here. [Issuer releases, accessed October 25](https://pershingsquareholdings.com/press-releases/).

### PS Inc.: valuable stakes, little literal cash at June date

June presentation: **$9M cash**, **$852M cash/investments**, **$235M debt**, **$617M net cash/investments**. Investments include HHH **9M shares/$643M**, PSUS common **4M/$150M** and preferred **$50M**. Debt comprised **$100M term/$135M revolver**, approximately **5.37%**, maturing **April 2029**. The net investment figure is **not $617M liquid dry powder**. [Results exhibit, August 12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2026053/000119312526347123/ps-ex99_1.pdf).

A later **August 12 $30M revolver draw** was intended to seed Ventures with cash on hand. Thus the August call's approximate facility balance differs from June presentation balances for a reason; do not merge dates. [PS Inc. 10-Q, August 13](https://pershingsquareinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PS-Inc.-2Q26-10Q.pdf).

### HHH/Vantage: a separate, incremental stock buyer

Vantage acquisition closed **June 4, 2026**, approximately **$2.1B**, funded with HHH cash and **$1B** PSH nonvoting exchangeable preferred. [Closing release June 4](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/04/3307105/0/en/howard-hughes-holdings-announces-closing-of-vantage-group-holdings-acquisition.html). The interim letter says long/intermediate bonds were replaced by short Treasuries and a common-stock portfolio similar to other Pershing funds. September presentation graphic indicates a transition from approximately **84% bonds/16% cash at closing** to **37% equities/62% cash/1% fixed income in August**; graphical extraction must not combine both dates into one allocation. [HHH presentation, September 29/30](https://investor.howardhughes.com/static-files/c377373c-e09f-4c7b-8d2f-cff34e580971).

HHH estimates **$2.5–3.0B** real-estate excess cash generation over coming years, with priorities including preferred redemption and insurance growth. This is **multiyear projected cash**, not available next quarter in full. Outside real-estate capital is a plan, not a booked fundraise. Insurance capital/liquidity/regulatory constraints require separate underwriting before assuming unrestricted concentrated equity capacity. Exact August investment portfolio dollars and current uncommitted equity capacity are unverified. Same primary sources.

### Ventures: planned permanent capital, launch uncertainty

PSV formed **July 8, 2026**, initially wholly owned/consolidated with manager; August call targeted fall/end-year. BofA subsequently warned performance could delay launch beyond the fourth quarter—**analyst expectation, not an issuer cancellation**. [10-Q August 13](https://pershingsquareinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PS-Inc.-2Q26-10Q.pdf); [MT Newswires October 13](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/pershing-square-underperformance-could-delay-145826869.html). No verified launch close, committed size, or pre-IPO name allocation as of cutoff.
