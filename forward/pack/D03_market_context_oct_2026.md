## What frightened the market

**AI spending and financing, rather than simply AI demand.** The October 22 technology selloff followed Meta results and renewed doubts about spending returns, with Treasuries rallying. Meta's share reaction exceeded **6%**, despite quarterly revenue reportedly **$64B versus $63.3B expected**. This is evidence that good current operating numbers can coexist with concern about capital allocation. [Reuters, October 22](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-10-22/); [Yahoo/AOL Meta results coverage, October 22](https://www.aol.com/articles/meta-stock-drops-more-6-151218000.html). For Pershing, this is precisely the distinction between growth capex that reinforces a moat and maintenance capex merely defending one.

**AI disruption in asset-light incumbents.** Booking/Expedia were repriced on the possibility that agents go directly to hotels. Bernstein highlighted greater agency risk than Airbnb; that is sell-side opinion, not proof of lost distribution economics. [October 4](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/ai-agents-reshaping-online-travel-095124553.html). The contrast with hotel-brand strength provides a concrete rotation test, not a blanket “travel is weak” story. [October 21 travel rotation](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ai-agent-era-sparks-trade-194845251.html).

**Credit/liquidity concerns.** Blue Owl again limited two private-credit fund redemptions to **5%**, after requests of **39% and 17%**, according to Bloomberg. Reuters described slowing flagship requests, so this is not evidence that every private-credit vehicle is failing. Liquidity limits are not the same as an underlying loan default. [Bloomberg October 2](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-02/blue-owl-again-caps-two-bdcs-after-39-17-withdrawal-requests); [Reuters October 2](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/blue-owl-flagship-fund-withdrawal-requests-slow-private-credit-turmoil-eases-2026-10-02).

**Rates, energy and politics.** September's tightening means “the next move must be a cut” is not a safe premise. October hold expectations do not settle the later path. Iran/Russia–Ukraine resolution was Ackman's conditional bullish scenario, not an established outcome. Trade/tariff uncertainty remained in Pershing's interim report; US midterms on **November 3, 2026** are a political catalyst, not a reliable near-term privatization date. [Ackman October 22 transcript](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/cnbc-exclusive-transcript-pershing-square-founder-ceo-bill-ackman-speaks-with-cnbcs-sara-eisen-on-squawk-on-the-street-today.html); [PSH interim August 13](https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/13002306/Pershing-Square-Holdings-Ltd.-June-2026-Interim.pdf).

## Earnings season: expectations versus outcomes

FactSet October 23: **24%** of index companies reported; **83%** beat EPS, but aggregate upside was only **2.1%**, versus five-year **7.5%** average. Revenue beat frequency **66%**, upside **1.2%**. Blended earnings growth **29.5%**, revenue **12.7%**; **179** companies were due the following week. Energy, IT and Communications led growth narratives, with Healthcare the lone declining earnings sector. These are blended actual/estimate figures, not all-realized results. [FactSet October 23](https://insight.factset.com/sp-500-earnings-season-update-october-23-2026).

**Do not invent a list of mega-caps down over 10% on results.** Verified examples located here do not meet that test: Meta over **6%**; IBM around **6.5% premarket/7% reported**, with earnings quality/AI commentary concerns; Netflix roughly **4%** Wednesday in overview news. [IBM Reuters, October 22](https://www.reuters.com/business/ibm-falls-analysts-question-staying-power-software-rebound-2026-10-22/); [Netflix overview, October 23](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nflx/). An October 23 movers list reports LKQ **−22.47%**, Bloom **−11.24%**, SLM **−10.78%**, but mixes preliminary results, guidance and sell-the-news effects and does **not** establish the requested mega/large-cap sample. [Insider Monkey October 23](https://www.insidermonkey.com/news/stock-market-movers-biggest-losers-and-gainers-on-23-october-2026-1858768?singlepage=1). A comprehensive event-study screen remains open.

**Decision use:** separate temporary multiple compression from lower normalized cash generation. No regime indicator supplies a reason to buy a company failing Pershing's business-quality or balance-sheet gate.
