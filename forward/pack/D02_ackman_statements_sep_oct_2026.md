## Reading rule

This is a **verified investment-signal ledger, not a certified complete export of every public statement**. No new position is inferred solely from praise.

### October 22 CNBC — strongest fresh decision evidence

All quotes in this section: [CNBC official unofficial transcript, published October 22, 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/cnbc-exclusive-transcript-pershing-square-founder-ceo-bill-ackman-speaks-with-cnbcs-sara-eisen-on-squawk-on-the-street-today.html).

**Process:** “our goal is not to find stocks that are going to go up in the next 90 days.” “we have been able to buy businesses, Visa, Mastercard, Netflix, and others at prices that we pencil out to well north of 20 percent IRRs over three-, four-, five-year holding periods.”

**Capital:** “we deployed $5 billion of capital of Pershing Square USA in some very high-quality long-term durable compounders”.

**Netflix after earnings:** “We actually viewed the quarter very favorably.” “I would say Netflix is a high-growth utility.” “I think stock today is something like 18 times earnings.” “we expect our go-forward IRR to be well north of, well into the 20s.” These are management views, not certified financial estimates.

**Meta:** “this remains an incredibly undervalued stock, and that's why we own it.” He discusses cash flexibility and AI spending; interpretation: capex fear has not yet changed his public thesis.

**Fannie/Freddie:** “Yes, very bullish.” “We own over well north of 200 million shares of combined companies.” “in the scenario I have outlined, these are well into the high $40s, $50 stock.” The scenario requires Treasury accounting/relisting and policy action; **not an unconditional target or January deadline**.

**Macro:** “If you get those out of the way, inflation comes down, rates come down, you're going to have, we're going to have an amazing 2027.” Context: Iran and Russia–Ukraine resolution, a conditional scenario.

**Personal/geographic:** “I'm a big, big fan of the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange and the country generally”; he describes an almost **5%** stake and a **ninefold** return. Do not attribute that exposure to PSH without ownership evidence. Microsoft, Amazon and SpaceX are also named in the transcript; mention alone does not establish fresh trading.
