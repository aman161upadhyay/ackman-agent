## Down since cost — do not confuse with YTD

H's June table explicitly measures 2026 initiations from average cost: **MSFT −8%, NFLX −11%, SPGI −3%; Visa +6%, Mastercard +5%**. Uber **−12%, BN −7%, Meta −15%, HHH −10%, FNMA −39%, FMCC −41%** are year-to-date returns, **not since-purchase losses**. ICE/Alcon have no June return because purchases were later. These are historical marks, not October cost P&L. Original average announced costs, where archived, were Uber **$67**, Meta **$625**, Amazon **$178**, BN **$28 split-adjusted**, HHH direct issuance **$100**; subsequent purchases change averages. ## Valuation pressure versus the return hurdle

October 23 consensus PE from D04 and linked statistics: **MSFT 26.54x, Visa 28.21x, Mastercard 29.26x** versus H's June **19x/23x/24x**; these are **opportunity-cost review candidates**, not declared fully valued. H's growth estimates **19%/16%/18%** suggest less margin to a **20%+** target without re-rating/capital returns. Different earnings definitions also matter. Amazon **26.39x** must be assessed against its higher manager growth estimate, not rejected mechanically. Netflix **18.34x**, Uber **16.52x**, Alcon **17.35x** screen cheaper; valuation alone cannot establish intact earnings power. [MSFT](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msft/statistics/), [V](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/statistics/), [MA](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ma/statistics/), [AMZN](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amzn/statistics/), all October 23.

Ackman on October 22 still called Meta “incredibly undervalued” and said Visa/Mastercard/Netflix purchases pencilled to “well north of 20 percent IRRs over three-, four-, five-year holding periods.” A high PE is not an automatic sell; a falling price is not an automatic buy. [CNBC, 2026-10-22](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/cnbc-exclusive-transcript-pershing-square-founder-ceo-bill-ackman-speaks-with-cnbcs-sara-eisen-on-squawk-on-the-street-today.html).
