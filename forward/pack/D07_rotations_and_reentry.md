## Evidence hierarchy and portfolio-scope trap

An execution date, quarter-end holding and disclosure date are separate. A disappearance from common-stock 13F can reflect calls rather than an exit, as Nike demonstrated. The June 2026 filing changed to a parent consolidation including affiliated managers; comparisons mechanically labeling its holdings “new” against the parent's tiny prior report are wrong. This ledger prioritizes manager explanations, uses filings for holdings, and does not infer the **use of particular sale proceeds** unless the manager said so.

## What the pattern actually predicts

**Re-entering is allowed.** Starbucks sold/rebought in the pandemic; Canadian Pacific returned in **2021** after the first activist cycle; Netflix returned in **2026** after the **2022** loss and a business whose monetization/predictability he now considers improved. Visa/Mastercard also have early compendium history; do not call their 2026 additions first-ever exposure without checking the old records. S&P Global is explicitly a former investment in C. [Starbucks 2020 report, March29 2021](https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/2021/03/29100010/Pershing-Square-Holdings-Ltd.-2020-Annual-Report.pdf); C.

**Opportunity-cost sales differ from impairment sales.** Hilton, historical Domino's/Agilent and Zoetis exemplify valuation/redeployment. Nike, Hertz and the earlier Netflix exit reflect underwriting confidence falling. Rebuying an impaired name requires new evidence, not just a lower quote. D05 gives individual exit sources and successor-security cautions.

**Capital deployment widened the library, not necessarily the activist appetite.** A new public closed-end vehicle and insurance platform allowed simultaneous stakes in more established compounders. Absence of board fights in the latest additions is consistent with owning good managers; HHH remains the separate controlled-platform exception. Analyst inference: an incremental buy in already researched quality names has a shorter diligence path than an unproven turnaround.

**Completeness limit:** this captures authenticated strategic entries/exits and major reported reallocations, not every execution, derivative adjustment or matched-scope quarterly share delta since January 2025. No present-day named position sizes should be imputed from incomplete comparisons. D01 is the latest verified holdings baseline; November review/13F is the next scoring checkpoint.
