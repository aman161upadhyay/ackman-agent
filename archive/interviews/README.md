# Interviews, talks and prepared investor presentations

**Cutoff: 2026-10-25. 18 reviewed entries:** seven interview/conversation records (one available through a contemporary report), plus eleven official annual investor presentations. **Prepared slide text is institutional text, not proof Ackman spoke those exact words.** This mixed inventory is transparent about format; it is not eighteen independently transcribed broadcast interviews.

Exact excerpts are brief and tied to process. Dates follow the event/original release, not later migration folders. Sources were fetched and long texts processed with `queryWithLLM`, with quotation windows checked where available. Transcript reads were sometimes partial (roughly first 50k characters, plus a second chunk for selected long sources). Automatic transcripts require audio checks before training on exact punctuation, speaker attribution or a material number.

## 1. Saïd Business School, Oxford — October 29, 2013

- **Source/type:** [Business Insider contemporary report, November 5](https://www.businessinsider.com/ackman-at-oxfords-said-business-school-2013-11); [event video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lnh0TmyVG9A). Report-based, not a full reviewed transcript.
- **Process excerpt attributed to Ackman:** “Experience is making mistakes and learning from them.”
- **Useful content:** Gotham/early financing lessons; diligence before committing capital; perseverance; Herbalife thesis and confident timetable.
- **Negative training value:** his prediction that progress against Herbalife would be visible within twelve months was a dated prediction, not a later-established fact. Preserve strong conviction when judging subsequent updating.
- **Identity correction:** this is **Saïd Business School**, not the **Oxford Union**. Do not merge venue identities merely because both are in Oxford.

## 2. Graham & Doddsville, Winter 2015 — interview reproduced July 5, 2017

- **Links:** [public Q&A reproduction](https://acquirersmultiple.com/2017/07/bill-ackman-try-to-invest-in-businesses-where-its-very-hard-to-lose-money); [original Columbia issue linked by reproduction](http://www8.gsb.columbia.edu/valueinvesting/sites/valueinvesting/files/Graham%20%20Doddsville_Issue%2023_Final.pdf).

- **Useful content:** Gotham's intellectual evolution; owner/economic earnings; good-business selection; idea referrals; relevant CEO experience; downside-based sizing; Fannie/Freddie smaller because of possible total loss; Allergan 27%-at-cost strategic bid; concentration and scale.
- **Dating/access:** Winter 2015 interview, not a July 2017 interview. Original issue identified but the fetched text was the reproduction. It contains an unrelated injected promotional sentence/link; that text was excluded.

## Use in agent building

Prefer direct transcript process passages for voice; use decks as factual/strategic institutional evidence. Do not teach an agent to mimic interviewer prompts, chairman statements, a portfolio company's management vocabulary, or automatic transcription artifacts. For historical replay, source release date governs eligibility, not the investment date being discussed retrospectively.
