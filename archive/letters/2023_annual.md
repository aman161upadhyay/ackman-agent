# 2023 annual report: mature framework and a more systematic hedge effort

**Source/information date:** [official annual report, March 22, 2024](https://assets.pershingsquareholdings.com/2024/03/22201541/Pershing-Square-Holdings-Ltd.-2023-Annual-Report.pdf). The report covers 2023 but includes 2024 plans. All findings below are sourced to this report.

## Quality and low turnover

The manager seeks businesses whose moats, balance sheets and management can withstand adverse external conditions. Concentration allocates capital to best ideas; low turnover avoids the contradiction of advertising long-term ownership while constantly replacing securities.

**Exact excerpt:**

> “Frenetic investment activity is often the enemy of long-term performance.”

Alphabet was initiated after AI concerns made valuation attractive. Lowe's was sold because expected future returns were less certain in the prevailing macro environment. These are different tests: a threatening narrative can create opportunity **if** actual economics remain robust; worsening external sensitivity can justify an exit.

## Quiet influence

**Exact excerpt:**

> “We greatly prefer our quieter and more time-efficient approach to engaged long-term ownership.”

This describes how influence is exercised, not abandonment of engagement. The letter argues influence improves value-maximizing decisions and reduces errors. Public proxy battles are no longer the assumed default.

## Hedges: include the losses

The overall hedging program detracted **187bp in 2023**, principally energy-related protection (-108bp) and Japanese interest-rate swaptions (-141bp), partly offset by USD-rate swaptions (+92bp). These attribution figures should not be casually added to separately reported gross dollar trading profits or assigned solely to the public Treasury short.

The letter explains the purpose as reducing permanent-loss exposure and generating liquidity at the most useful time. It explicitly does not promise to identify/hedge every future risk. In hindsight, it says larger sizing in some successful historical hedges could have earned more with limited added capital risk.

After the 2020 pandemic trade, asymmetric opportunity research became more systematic, involving a dedicated subset of the team: Ackman, Ryan Israel and Bharath Alamanda. **Systematic search is not continuous hedging**; premiums and payoff still determine whether to transact.

## Capital, culture and platform

The letter reviews three chapters: strong first nearly twelve years, Valeant/Herbalife setbacks, and the permanent-capital recovery beginning 2018. It discusses forty employees, long tenure, reputation and transparency about mistakes. These are explanatory hypotheses about repeatability and organizational incentives.

SPARC launched September 29, 2023. The report discusses prospective PSUS and amended fee offsets. A proposed closed-end vehicle is not yet completed funding; keep plan and consummation dates separate.

## Replay use

For a 2023 Alphabet entry, this is retrospective evidence of stated rationale, not a source available before purchase. For a March 2024 philosophy agent it is a high-value synthesis. The hedge losses are important negative training examples against cherry-picked “always hedge brilliantly” behavior.
