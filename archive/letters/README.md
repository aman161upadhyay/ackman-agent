# Pershing Square letters: process-oriented source index, 2004–2026

**Research cutoff 2026-10-25.** “Annual” names the **performance year**, not the publication year. H1 reports frequently discuss events after June 30; those passages are usable only from their actual information date. Institutional chairman text is not automatically an Ackman quotation. Quotes below are deliberately short; detailed companion notes contain analysis rather than full copyrighted letters.

**Coverage:** verified annual-report letters for 2014–2025; H1 sources for 2017–2026; selected pre-2014 quarterly letters; January 2016 annual letter separately distinguished from March 2016 annual-report update. **Not a complete surviving-letter census.** Search included official materials, Exa/web search, Scribd, Market Folly and third-party PDF/text mirrors. Missing original dates/text are identified, not reconstructed from model memory. Long PDFs were processed using `queryWithLLM`, followed by targeted text checks; first 50k-character fetches are partial report reads, not proof every later page was reviewed.

Official discovery pages: [materials](https://pershingsquareholdings.com/materials/), [events/presentations](https://pershingsquareholdings.com/events-presentations/) (accessed 2026-10-25). Re-upload directories such as `/2025/07/` are **not** original publication dates.

## 2008 Q3 — deleveraging, survival and intrinsic value

- **Date:** reproduced November 27, 2008 by AdvisorAnalyst, which attributes the letter to November 15. The 2014 anthology identifies a November 3, 2008 excerpt; **original dating is conflicting**. Use November 27 as the safe verified public reproduction date.
- **Link:** [letter reproduction](https://advisoranalyst.com/2008/11/27/bill-ackman-pershing-square-q3-2008-letter.html/).
- **Positions discussed:** Longs Drugs (real-estate/SOTP and strategic buyer), Wachovia (restructured orphan assets), AIG (small mispriced option, exited at loss); bond-insurer/CDS protection also discussed.
- **Process quote:** “Our approach to assessing reputational risk is to apply the New York Times test.”
- **Process lesson:** avoid forced sales; separate permanent impairment from liquidation-driven prices; counterparty collateral and funding structure matter. Approximately 39% cash/near-cash at the reproduction's writing is a dated state, not an enduring allocation.

## 2010 Q1 — passive investing, time allocation and selling before fair value

- **Date:** June 8, 2010.
- **Link:** [archival text](https://pdfcoffee.com/pershing-q1-2010-investor-letter-pdf-free.html).
- **Positions:** Nestlé, McDonald's and ADP sold after rapid recovery; GGP restructuring/spin plan; EMC exit after catalysts failed; Borders loan; Sears Canada; Kraft/Cadbury; Citigroup; Aliansce.
- **Process quote:** “we are delighted to be a passive owner.”
- **Lesson:** private influence is useful, not compulsory; a sale can precede full fair value if redeployment offers better risk-adjusted return.
- **Historical publication clue:** the letter explicitly says **no 2009 annual letter** was written because results were discussed at the January 28, 2010 dinner and February 4 presentation, “as in previous years.” Do not fill every pre-2014 annual slot with an imagined letter.
- **Access caution:** mirror mixes recommendations/interface text and other documents; analysis used the identified June 8 letter body.

## 2010 Q2 — risk buckets, reputation and smaller asymmetric situations

- **Date:** August 26, 2010.
- **Link:** [archival text](https://pdfcoffee.com/pershing-square-q2-10-investor-letter-pdf-free.html).
- **Positions:** YUM exit near intrinsic value; Landry's take-private repricing; GGP; Borders; BP credit protection; Peter Cooper Village/Stuyvesant Town mezzanine debt.
- **Process quote:** “We have historically also invested in other investments that have materially different risk and reward characteristics.”
- **Lesson:** a small, high-risk optionality position is not sized like a durable core business; probability of permanent loss must be compensated by payoff and lower capital at risk.

## 2013 Q2 — probabilistic investing and public mistake management

## 2013 Q3 — JCP exit and the confidence/humility correction

- **Date:** October 2, 2013.
- **Link:** [FT-hosted PDF](https://ftalphaville-cdn.ft.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/Ackman-Covers-JCP-Short_0.pdf).
- **Positions:** JCP fully sold at approximately 50% loss; Herbalife exposure partly converted to long-dated puts; Air Products governance/CEO transition.
- **Process quote:** “It is critically important not to let psychological factors interfere with economic rationality in investment decision making.”
- **Lesson:** turnarounds require aligned board, appropriate CEO and stakeholder support; public confidence can impede recognizing contradictory facts. Put conversion capped potential loss but did not prove the Herbalife thesis.
